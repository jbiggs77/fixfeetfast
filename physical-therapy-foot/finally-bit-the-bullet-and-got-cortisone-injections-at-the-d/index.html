Community comment#20

Hi Casey Lewis, the duration of relief from cortisone injections can vary depending on the individual and the specific condition being treated. However, in general, most people experience some level of relief within a few days to a week after the injection. The initial relief you're feeling after only an hour and a half is likely due to the local anesthetic that is often used along with the cortisone. This can provide immediate relief, but it typically wears off within a few hours. The cortisone itself usually takes a few days to start working, and its effects can last for several weeks to several months. However, it's important to note that cortisone injections are not a long-term solution and should be used in conjunction with other treatments, such as physical therapy, stretching exercises, or orthotics. It's also important to follow your doctor's instructions for rest and recovery after the injection. You may be advised to avoid certain activities or to take it easy for a few days to allow the injection to take full effect. If you have any concerns or questions about your cortisone injection or the duration of its effects, I recommend reaching out to your doctor for further guidance.
