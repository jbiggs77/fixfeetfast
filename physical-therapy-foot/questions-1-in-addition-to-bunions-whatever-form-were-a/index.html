Overpronation with bunion: does surgery address all issues?

Archive date 2026-09-286 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionFlat Feet & Arch Support

Questions: 1. In addition to bunion(s) whatever form, were any of you suffering with overpronation of big toe. OR an ingrown toenail same foot. Ingrown toenail was first visit to my dr and was extracted nicely. Anyways the overpronation lead to ginormous calluses on big toe and ball of foot. Which also lead to the hallux rigidus (that adjective changed every visit this past year). Part of my conservative measures to address this were orthotics, shoe change and lots of pumicing, paring (by dr) and self surgery (major no-no). 2. If any of you are past post op to know if the above has ebbed, I sure would be interested in days ahead how surgery may have changed those foot issues. 3. And being the feet are our “foundation” don’t you feel like and older building falling apart, lol. Or a car that is grossly out of line?? Just curious, bored, inquisitive person letting my foot heal up!! Four weeks post op today!!!!
