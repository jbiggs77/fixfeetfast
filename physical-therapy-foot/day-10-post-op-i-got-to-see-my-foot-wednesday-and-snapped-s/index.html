Day 10 after bunion and neuroma removal: early recovery update

Archive date 2026-10-0117 archived replies
More foot health experiences

Day 10 post op- I got to see my foot Wednesday and snapped some pictures- had to take my son with me and I made him sit in the chair turned around so he couldn’t see it- if you guy wanna laugh zoom in on his face in the one picture I totally did not know he was looking and his reaction is priceless!! Had to have an antibiotic called in yesterday and it was rather inflamed, red, and warm. and seems to be looking better today. So far I have yet to wake up in the middle of the night or even get a Charlie horse or cramp and even spasm so I’m like 100% sure the Neuroma removal was successful. It’s sore but toes are positioned nicely too- so far so good…. Just wish it didn’t hurt so bad
