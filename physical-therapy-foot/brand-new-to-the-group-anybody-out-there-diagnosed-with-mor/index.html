Community comment#4

I have Morton’s in both my feet. No injury required. I’ve done steroid shots and alcohol injections (supposed to deaden the transmitability of the nerve) but I grew tired of being a pin cushion. So now, I deal with the pain. It aches at night and hurts with every step. But the right shoes help a lot. Lots of people like Hoka’s. For me, those were instant numbing...the pressure was in the wrong spot. I like Vionics Tokyo. Get wide width in any shoe to allow your foot room to spread out. Get good supportive slippers to wear indoors. Good luck!
