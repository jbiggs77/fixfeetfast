Community comment#2

I had both done at the same time in July of ‘22. I’m back to running half marathons but doubt I’ll ever do a full again. It’s not perfect but it’s way better than before surgery. If I were to do it again, I’d get into PT sooner after surgery. I waited probably 7 months or so. PT is what helped the recovery along the most since they did a lot of work on the scar tissue.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#3

I agree with dr Bregman. I’d try alternative methods for neuroma. I have severe bunions that cause me neuromas in both feet, not sure where you are located but here in NYC I had a procedure called cryosurgery for a nerve (sometimes called a cryoablation) they basically go in during a minimally invasive procedure and freeze your nerve to restore its health ( the damaged thickened sheath of nerve sheds and the nerve regenerates) it takes weeks to do so but it’s highly effective. Once they remove your nerve it can form stump and that can cause more pain than neuroma itself. Good luck 🙂
