Shockwave therapy for neuroma: immediate relief experience

Archive date 2026-10-0122 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound Healing

So Today I had my first session of Shockwave therapy. (bunions an Mortons Neuroma on both feet) after my orthopedic Surgeon from UCSF said to give it a try. Very interesting treatment. You lay on a chiropractic type table and they put the shock wave wand on the areas. At first you feel minor pulsing until it finds the sore spots an YIKES when it finds the problem areas it is very uncomfortable on the first pass but on the second pass it was fine. The Chiropractor told me that once it starts breaking up the scar tissue in the injured area it stops hurting after a few minutes as it stimulates blood to the area and new collegen prduction. I could not believe the relief after just one session. They recommend Toe stretches and spacers for 2 to 4 hrs per day, and foot insert soles made for neuroma, Wide shoes only for now. I go back every 7 days for 3 to 5 sessions or until the pain is gone. I will report back next week!
