Anyone else have pain on the Top of your feet?

Archive date 2026-10-0319 archived replies
Post-Surgery Swelling & InflammationBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryFlat Feet & Arch Support

Anyone else have pain on the Top of your feet? I've been struggling with this for several years. Only thing that helps is inactivity and cortisone shots. I can't keep getting shots obviously, because it deteriorates the bone. I've had my podiatrist make me fancy expensive orthodontics (which help). I have no neuropathy. (Tested with those electrical shocks on my legs). I'm at a loss. I need and want to be able to walk without severe pain. Anyone ended have this and figure it out??? (No real noticable swelling, sometimes though I see a faint redness bigger than a half dollar right on top). I'd blame the shoes, but I switch them up often and it doesn't seem to matter. When the pain is at it's worse, it radiates up my shins....
