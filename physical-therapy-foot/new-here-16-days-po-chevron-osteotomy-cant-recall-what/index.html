Scarf & Akin Osteotomy

New here! 16 days PO (chevron osteotomy, & can't recall what else). I’ve had a lot of orthopedic surgeries, but never before on a foot! Pls excuse the goo from the wrap I just took off . Most of the neuropathy is under my middle toes-it’s not bad/comes & goes. II had it intermittently before & tried the alcohol injections there a few yrs ago, but they didn’t really help; my doc believes it will improve now, as the other metatarsals were shifting over the yrs from the bunion.
