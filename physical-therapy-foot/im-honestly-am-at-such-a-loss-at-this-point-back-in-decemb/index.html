I’m honestly am at such a loss at this point.

Archive date 2026-10-0215 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

I’m honestly am at such a loss at this point. Back in December I got Metatarsalgia type pain in both my feet, and then started to get heel pain. Tried orthotics, and they weren’t of much help. Went to PT and they did a massage, made my feet worse. X-rays were normal. Then I tried acupuncture, a more holistic approach, and my acupuncturist hit nerves in my legs and feet, and now I have nerve pain in my toes. I keep trying to help my feet and they keep getting worse. I’m just so exhausted from this chronic pain and am at a loss on who I see now and am trying not to panic over the nerve issue.

Community comment#13

I too have been in chronic and severe pain in the ball of my feet for 17 months now. Luckily, told me something about fat pad tissue atrophy and the doctors who treat it. Not sure if you've had MRIs or not and whether they've eliminated the possibility that you have neuromas. If so, then it could that your fat pad has worn so thin that it's because nerves on bone with no protective layer in between. Claudia helped me get in touch with some folks at Leneva or MTF Biologics, and they can help you search for a doctor in your local area who specializes in this sort of treatment. I'm still trying to figure out whether I'm a good candidate for it, but right now it seems like the most plausible thing to follow. Please let me know if I can provide any additional info.
