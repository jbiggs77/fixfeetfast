Painful feet... this is my story, so I know this won't help everyone, but it's worth writing in case it helps someone. I had excruciating pain across the top of both my feet & also pain shooting up the inside of my lower legs. This went on for years. I had various tests & xrays & was told it was wear & tear (in other words suck it up!). I had an appointment with a Rheumatologist & mentioned my feet. She referred me to an NHS Podiatrist. He looked at my test results & said to try half length arch insoles. I thought, really, is that it? Also, the bottom of my feet really hurt & I can't have anything hard on them. Anyway, I thought well ok let's try them as they're free. The insoles came, I put them in my shoes - bosh they've taken all the pain away! I've since bought my own arch insoles & when I feel my feet painfully niggling I realise I've been wearing my slippers for days I just put my slip ons on with the insoles & it goes away. It may be worth a try before going down the road of many tests only to end up with quite a simple solution 😊 Painful feet... this is my story, so I know this won't help everyone, but it's worth writing in case it helps someone.
