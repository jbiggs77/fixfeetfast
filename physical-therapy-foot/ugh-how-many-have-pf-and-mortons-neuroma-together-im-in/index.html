that may continue to add pressure to the nerve, nerves need to feel free to glide. I would get someone to see if you have bound up tissues in the area. Often times when the tissues are restricted or there’s a lot of tension in the area the neuroma is more aggravated. A therapist certified in active release technique could be helpful. If you don’t have that in your area, look for myofascial release or any other soft tissue therapies. The therapist should hopefully give you exercises for ankle, foot, and toe mobility, And they should also be working up the chain. Because what is happening higher than your feet has a big impact on your foot function.
