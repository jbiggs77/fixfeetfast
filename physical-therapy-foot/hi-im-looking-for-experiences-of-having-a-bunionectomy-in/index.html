Hi! I’m looking for experiences of having a bunionectomy in the UK? I’m around 4 weeks from having mine on my right foot , I also have a bone cyst in the area so that needs to be removed too. I have 2 young children and I’m panicking about the recovery time and how we are going to manage. How long were you off your feet for? How long were you in a cast or boot? What shoes do you have to wear afterwards and for how long? Am I mad for doing this now or will there ever be a right time?! Thanks

Community comment#1

I’m in Southampton and am one week post op for bunion removal I was terrified after hearing how painful it was meant to be online BUT I am putting minimal weight on and only taking paracetamol and ibuprofen morning and night , worst thing for me was throwing up with codeine on day 2 so stopped if but didn’t need it at all x I have a bandage and shoe and bandages come off at week 2 then shoe for further 4/6 weeks I managed a shower with bag on foot and propped up on cupboard as I have a shower chair , I go up and down stairs on knees but can now hobble up good luck

Community comment#3

I just had mine done in the US with MIS for the bunionectomy as well as decompressing a Morton’s neuroma on May 27th. I have two young children as well. If you don’t have a strong support I would recommend not going ahead. The first week after surgery was terrible. Thankfully my husband was able to take the kids full time. We also had a nanny when he returned to work. I’m just shy of 3 weeks and I’m still in my boot and I can’t catch the little buggers if I needed to.
