This past January I’ve had my 2nd revision surgery trying to resolve multiple non-union.

Archive date 2026-09-2115 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPost-Surgery Swelling & InflammationBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

This past January I’ve had my 2nd revision surgery trying to resolve multiple non-union. And this one isn’t any better but recently classified as delayed-union and I’m now using Exogen Bone Stim. However my question today isn’t about the union. Throughout all 3 surgeries for past 3 years, I have been dealing with a terrible pain on the center of the ball of my foot. Circled in photo. It’s actually more painful than the non union. It prevents me from walking correctly. I walk on the outer edge of my foot which then creates other pain in my ankle and joints. Throwing my biomechanics off. The pain is hot burning sensation when I walk (not when rested) and it feels like a lump I’m walking on. Also almost like I’m walking on the bone directly on that spot. You can visibly see swelling in that area it seems. I thought it may go away but it’s been 3 years and still not. I’ve also started to develop claw toes particularly in the 2nd and 3rd. I don’t know if it’s Neuroma or Metatarsalgia or something else. But the surgeon always seems to not worry about it when I complain about the pain. Focusing only on the surgical site and union. I have tried metatarsal pads, custom orthotics with metatarsal support, carbon fiber insoles, different shoes (hoka, brooks, altra). Cream’s, inflammation remedies. Nothing works and it extremely painful to walk. Any thoughts on a remedy. Thanks.
