Ok question: For the past few years I have been dealing with foot pain in my right foot/ankle, here recently…

Archive date 2026-10-1814 archived replies

Ok question: For the past few years I have been dealing with foot pain in my right foot/ankle, here recently it has also started in my left foot. When it happens my feet swell so much you can see it through my shoes. I have lost SO MUCH range of motion in both feet, i also can’t bend my toes, I normally wear high tops and I’m unable to bend my feet to get them on so I’ve been wearing slide on adidas. I have had so many X-rays, MRIs, boots, casts, crutches, ETC. In the beginning I was diagnosed with tendinitis, then a bunch of tiny stress fractures in my right foot, to Rheumatoid arthritis that has magically just gone away according to my lab work, im not sure what to do at this point. My doctor has referred me to the Mayo Clinic because NO ONE can figure out what’s wrong with my foot/feet. My mom asked for me to be referred to a cardiologist because back in December my heart rate went up to 170bpm and have been on medication since and she’s wondering if me going in and out of AFIB could have something to do with it, but my doctor won’t refer me unless I make an appointment for more testing. I’ve had ultrasounds, steroid injections into my joint, fluid drained from the joint, have seen a pediatrist, oncologist, rheumatologist, no one can figure it out. Pictures included in post. It has now started to effect my work and I can’t afford to lose another job due to this. When this happens I CAN NOT walk, at all. Ice doesn’t help, heat doesn’t help, I have an extremely high pain tolerance and everyone knows this and pain meds don’t even work. I’m not sure what to do at this point. Keep in mind, I’m 19.
