I had MIS surgery 2 years ago and I still have nerve pain. My doctor suggested a cortisone injection but I'm reluctant to do that. I've had them in my knees and ankle with little to no improvement. The slightest pressure is painful. Thoughts? Thanks.

Community comment#1

having them in your knees or ankle joints or any joint is different than having it for what you’re currently experiencing however, if it is truly nerve pain, it will only be temporary. It’s likely that it’s stuck in scar tissue or had been injured during surgery. ￼

Community comment#2

I am 16 months post op of bunion and osteotomy of 3 lesser toes and I am still in pain. I do my 2000 steps every evening and try to work thru it. I am looking at having the screws removed. Some people don't do well with the screws. Have you approached this conversation with your doctor? I will not do cortisone shots in my feet. I had one at the very beginning of my injury where I believe I had developed neuromas and the injection worsened my condition. My thoughts too were possibly scar tissue as Peter Bergman has mentioned. My surgeon recommended seeing a nerve specialist but since I have been in pain since the surgery - particularly with my big toe and that longer bone - 3 screws in total and a little loss of feeling - I am led to believe that the screws are causing me some issues.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#3

Removing the screw in my bone was like major surgery as the screw had become enveloped by the bone 10 weeks out and still in pain

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#4

everyone heals at their own pace. I think staying as active as possible helps the healing process. Hope you feel better soon.

Community comment#5

Look into scrambler therapy! It was the only thing that helped my nerve pain after bilateral bunion surgery in 2020.

Community comment#6

Ask your doctor about amitriptyline, it might solve your nerve pain.

Community comment#7

CORTISONE injections are not definitely ideal but they may work well for knee issues and shoulder issues. Unless it is really scar tissues within the deep level of where the incision was closed. Often cortisone injection do worse than do harm. Then honestly the question is: will you cope more then? I had them only up to 3 times and no more for my left shoulder.
