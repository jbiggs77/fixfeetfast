Stiff big toe after bunion surgery: hardware removal options

Archive date 2026-09-299 archived replies

Big toe still stiff—did hardware removal fix yours? Hi everyone, I had bunion surgery in October. Healed well—no pain—but my big toe is still stiff and won’t flex on its own. Surgeon says screws probably aren’t the cause, but removing them might help. Recovery would be: • 3 days in a surgical shoe • Walking normally in a week • No high-impact exercise for 4 weeks Has anyone had titanium screws removed? • Did it help stiffness or motion? • Was recovery easy? • Any downsides? Thanks for sharing your experience!
