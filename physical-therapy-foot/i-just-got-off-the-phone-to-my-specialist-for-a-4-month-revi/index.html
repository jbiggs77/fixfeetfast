Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#11

Tenex for plantar fasciitis is a minimally invasive procedure used when heel pain has not improved after months of treatments such as stretching, physical therapy, orthotics, or injections. During the procedure, the doctor uses ultrasound guidance to insert a tiny probe through a small incision. The probe uses high-frequency ultrasonic energy to break up and remove damaged, degenerative tissue in the plantar fascia while preserving healthy tissue. The goal is to allow the plantar fascia to heal more normally, reduce chronic pain, and improve function without the larger incision and longer recovery associated with traditional surgery. Most procedures are done under local anesthesia in an outpatient setting, and patients typically wear a walking boot afterward before gradually returning to normal activities over several weeks. It’ also covered by insurance.
