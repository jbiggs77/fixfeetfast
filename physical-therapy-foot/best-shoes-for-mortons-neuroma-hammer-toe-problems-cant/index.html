Community comment#20

do you have custom orthotics? if not no shoe will feel good. i was diagnosed with morton's in both feet in 2000! 22 years later i'm still wearing custom orthotics. the key to morton's is not only room in the box of the shoe to keep the toes from squeezing together & pinching the nerve(s) but to also wear orthotics. brooks walkers have been the best shoe for me. but when you have foot issues if an orthotic is required to minimize the issue no shoe is going to help. when it comes to mortons neuroma you have to take the pressure off the metatarsal area.
