Community comment#9

Yes, I also have them in both feet from competitive dancing. Get custom orthotics that offload the pressure on the nerve (Metatarsals). Wear low heeled shoes with wide toe box. Soak in Epsom Salt and accupuncture for pain. You can put Metatarsal pads in your shoes but position them below the Metatarsals. Cortisone shots help but don't last.

Community comment#10

See if you can find Superfeet Orthotics at a Running Store or look them up online to see who carries them. They will cut the insert to fit your shoe. Better than the expensive ones at the doctors.
