Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#28

Julie B. Witter I believe in bunion surgery. I don’t believe in MINIMAL INCISION bunion surgery. Being in the medical field I have seen many complications from these procedures. If you have had good results I’m happy for you. Complications from minimal incision bunion surgery (MIS) include: Bunion recurrence: The bunion may come back right after surgery or decades later. Bone healing issues: The bones may not heal properly. Nerve injury: This can cause numbness, tingling, or pain. Painful hardware: Hardware may need to be removed if it becomes painful. Infection: Overcorrection: The big toe may extend too far away from the other toes. Other complications include: Rigidity, Inflammation, Delayed healing, Complex regional pain syndrome (CRPS), Transfer metatarsalgia, Osteonecrosis, and Joint stiffness.
