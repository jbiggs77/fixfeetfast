I do. When I started PT my therapist told me that the neuroma pain would likely go away as the PF improved. I haven’t had any neuroma pain since about my 2nd week of PT.

I do, I have my third neuroma and my PF is 12mm. I am just about to start SWT . I have had steroid injections which haven’t worked, my PF is much more painful than my neuroma at the moment , but it will eventually be removed.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#6

Graston technique, massage, stretches & exercises & worked on strengthening my ankles & hips.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#7

Do they do the Graston on the area where the neuroma is or on your arch, heels ect

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#8

The Graston is done to the bottom of the foot. It breaks up the scar tissue on the fascia. My neuroma was on the top of my foot. My PT never did anything for the neuroma except to fix the PF.
