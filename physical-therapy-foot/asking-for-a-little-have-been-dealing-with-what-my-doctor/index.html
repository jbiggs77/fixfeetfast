Entrapped nerve after bunion surgery: cortisone shot options

Archive date 2026-09-2719 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryPain Management After Foot SurgeryPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Asking for a little . Have been dealing with what my doctor believes is a entrapped nerve 6 months out from bunion surgery. On Gabapentin (which I hate …doesn’t help pain either) and 5MG Oxycodone..which I want to STOP asap). FINALLY got my surgeon to take me seriously about the pain and yesterday he gave me a cortisone shot in tissues surrounding the nerve in hopes that it will loosen up toes and release nerve. So far today pain is unchanged..maybe slightly worse just from pain of injection. I am really hoping I notice some relief ! Thank u
