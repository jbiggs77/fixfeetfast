Community comment#30

I have both. My PT told me when we treated the PF the neuroma pain would most likely go away. He was right. You have 6 weeks until Thanksgiving. I strongly recommend finding a good sports PT trained in Graston technique who also believes in fixing the whole problem, not just focusing on the fascia. My PF was caused weak ankles & weak hips. I also recommend going to a local orthopedic shoe store to find the right pair of sneakers for you. (My local DSW doesn’t carry the better quality Brooks & Hokas than the orthopedic shoe store Carrie’s.) Tell them you have PF. I also recommend compression socks & PowerStep ProTech orthotics. And the day before the parade get a massage that focuses on your legs from the hips down, especially focusing on the calves.
