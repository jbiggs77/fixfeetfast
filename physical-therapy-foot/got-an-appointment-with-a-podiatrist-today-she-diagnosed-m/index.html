Got an appointment with a podiatrist today & she diagnosed me with a trapped nerve/mortons neuroma in both feet.

Archive date 2026-09-288 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot Surgery

Got an appointment with a podiatrist today & she diagnosed me with a trapped nerve/mortons neuroma in both feet. After years of being in pain & not knowing what's wrong, I'm so relieved to know what was going on. Been measured for orthotics next week as waiting on mri results from tomorrow's test. Also told me to ice my feet for up to 5 mins in and out..I can say, while it's not a total fix, I'm able to much a bit more freely around and I can move my toes better lol. It's amazing what one person's diagnosis can do. I'm alot happier than I was about it all today than I have been in a while.
