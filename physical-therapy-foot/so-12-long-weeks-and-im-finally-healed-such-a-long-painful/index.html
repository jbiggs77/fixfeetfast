Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#6

Kirsty, even extra wide sketchers are not sufficient as they are an extra wide rounded toe box design. Sketchers get a 0/3 anatomical unfortunately. The memory foam also wears and puts your feet into unstable positions. Raised heel, stiff sole, rounded narrow toe box. Elizabeth, Dansko clogs unfortunately get a 0/3 anatomical as well. Raised heel, stiff sole, rounded narrow toe box as well. We started wearing rounded toe box shoes 550 years ago in Elizabethan England when the middle class first got access to horses. Rounded toe box shoes helped us fit into our stirrups and heels on shoes helped us not slip out of our stirrups. There's no anatomical reasoning aside from that. Our feet will eventually conform to the shape of these shoes and risk bunion, plantar fasciitis, hammer toes, morton's neuroma, peripheral neuropathy and about 50 other specific ailments. Anatomical footwear has 3 components. 1) A foot shaped toe box that allows your toes to splay out completely and align with their metatarsals 2) A thin flexible sole that allows your arch muscles freedom of motion and won't cause arch collapse/flat feet 3) A zero drop design that won't put you on a perpetual downwards slant and overload your forefoot.
