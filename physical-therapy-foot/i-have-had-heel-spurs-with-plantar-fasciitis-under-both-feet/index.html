Community comment#49

I work as a recovery nurse, which means I also work with the orthopedist who performs surgeries on patients who have completed their treatment. He told me to stop everything I was doing and to buy a pair of good running shoes to give my plantar fascia a rest and to wear them consistently every moment of the day so that it can heal. No massage, stretching, shockwave therapy, or expensive insoles. Often it is a tear or overuse of the plantar fascia, and the only thing that works is rest!! This can take up to a year, so then look for the cause. Stop treating the symptoms; it only costs money. I am doing this now, and I must say, it is finally getting better, my pain is so much better!! It just takes patience and time. ❤️
