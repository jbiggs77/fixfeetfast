Sometimes feels like little needles going into all my foot. Is that how plantar fasciitis hurts?? It does hurt more when I wake up and there's a tightness feeling. Community comment#1

I have a condition called Chronic regional pain syndrome in my left foot, and your description matches thst more than PF. The problem I'm having is that CRPS makes anything/everything hurt way out of proportion to what's going on so the PF in that foot is off the charts. Many other neuropathy pain conditions often feel that way too. If I were you, I wouldn't try to just gut it out trying suggestions you read in here. Neuropathy is a whole different animal and can become even more debilitating than PF if left untreated, or treated the wrong way
