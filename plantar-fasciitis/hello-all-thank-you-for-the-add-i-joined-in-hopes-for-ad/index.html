Hello all! Thank you for the add! I joined in hopes for advice on some wide toe box shoes! We're trying to put off surgery until my youngest is a bit older (doc is who suggested it, thinks wide toe box shoes and insoles will help with pain until we can proceed with surgery) I ordered a pair of shoes off amazon, but they still seem a bit small in the toe area. Can anyone recommend a good pair? Should I size up? My feet ache all the time and I have dealt with bunions for years, but now my toes are starting to go crooked which is causing more pain (more so on￼ my right) These were my diagnosis from podiatrist: Valgus deformity of both great toes Mononeuritis: Medial proper nerve bilateral Long second metatarsal bilateral Capsulitis: First and second metatarsophalangeal joints bilateral
