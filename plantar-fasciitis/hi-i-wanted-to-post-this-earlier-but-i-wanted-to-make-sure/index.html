Hi, I wanted to post this earlier but I wanted to make sure it was working, I hope this helps someone as I was losing hope and for the first time in months I am starting to be able to go for walks pain free . I’ve had plantar in my left foot for 3 months, been to docs, physio, foot docs and podiatrist, got all the same answers as google, given orthotics (didn’t help at all for me it just added more foot and archilles pain). Before I try shockwave, cortisone or surgery I tried this) Here’s what I have been doing for a whole month every day not sure what works and what doesn’t but this is my recipe. Rest a lot, gave up running and walking and changed to biking and weights building strength in my lower body (calf’s,hamstrings, glutes, hips) Taping helped while I began doing these. Changed footwear, no more crocs etc, hard soled sneakers and cork soled slippers for indoors with cheap Temu insoles for cushioning Stretching is key, every day I did toe stretches, calf stretches 5-10 times a day 1 min hold, circled my ankles 50 times both ways twice a day, calf raises and hamstring stretches. Start out slow does hurt to start with and as you get stronger increase the reps etc. When you start to feel better I tried walking barefoot 5 mins a day to strengthen foot by walking heal to toe slowly on carpet or grass. Massage Gun is a massive help with my pain relief, I just do both legs, hams, quad, calf’s and lower setting on feet I also stand on a shakti mat before bed for 5 mins hurts but increases blood flow as it’s a tissue issue Epsom salt foot bath 20 mins at night every night I also take collagen powder daily but have been for years You have to be patient this isn’t a fast fix and I’m not fully healed but feel I’m on my way finally. This is an everyday job to get better and the less on your feet the best until pain eases then you can began the strengthening etc. it’s hard to slow down when your so active but there’s no way around this unless you want to stay in pain I had to give in. I really hope this helps someone I’m not a specialist but this combination of things have helped me get pain free, I found wasting money at appointments wasn’t needed but if this doesn’t help you may have to try something else, everyone is diff. Goodluck and be patient
