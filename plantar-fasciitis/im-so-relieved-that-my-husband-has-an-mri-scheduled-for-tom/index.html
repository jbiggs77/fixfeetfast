Six months of plantar fasciitis: MRI time

Archive date 2026-07-037 archived replies
Post-Surgery Swelling & InflammationBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

I’m so relieved that my husband has an MRI scheduled for tomorrow so we can finally find out once and for all what’s going on with his foot! He did CPR on a friend on a moving boat which started it and then he jumped off a scaffold and felt a sharp bee sting like pain and it’s now been 6 months of agony. Ultrasound didn’t show any tears but apparently it won’t show micro tears. Every night it’s red, hot and swollen again. He’s doing everything you should, wearing the Asics Gel Kayano 32 shoes, no barefoot walking, icing, podiatrist recommended exercises … all of the things. He also did shockwave therapy but we’ve since been told that’s not much use when it’s still so inflamed and we need to get rid of the inflammation for anything to work. Here’s his ultrasound so it’s definitely thickened. Has anyone had an MRI for this and did it help pinpoint anything? See less
