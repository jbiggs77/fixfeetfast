If anyone is suffering from plantar fasciitis pain due to tight calves, I just purchased a slant board on Amazon and it has been a game-changer. Have not had any pain in almost 4 days now. Doing calf lowering stretches on the edges of steps puts lots of strain on the metatarsal and can create new problems in your forefoot/balls of feet. The slant board stretches your calves and evenly distributes the weight. You can start at a low incline and work your way up.
