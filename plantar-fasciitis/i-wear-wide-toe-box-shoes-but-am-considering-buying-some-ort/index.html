Hi Mary Sue, I have heard from many people who have had positive experiences with orthotic insoles, especially when dealing with foot pain or conditions like plantar fasciitis. They can provide additional support and help alleviate pressure on certain areas of the foot. However, it's important to make sure you get the right type of insoles for your specific needs and foot shape. It might be a good idea to consult with a podiatrist or other foot specialist to help you select the right insoles. Best of luck!
