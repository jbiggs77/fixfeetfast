I’ve had plantar fasciitis in both feet for about 9 months.

Archive date 2026-09-1335 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

I’ve had plantar fasciitis in both feet for about 9 months. I lost all hope at one point. I’ve tried everything from sleeves, tape, shoes, rollers etc. I’m on my feet all day everyday for work. Nothing helped. For the last 2 months I have tried toe yoga. I have also added in strengthening my ankles and calves. I also do toe separators daily for 10min. I can say that I’m so close and about 95% healed. If you are avoiding surgery or steroid injections….this is what worked for me. YouTube toe yoga. Tons of easy starter exercises. So glad I found this info in this group because I was at a point where I didn’t think I was ever going to be active with my kids again.
