I've had plantar fasciitis for 10 months now (diagnosed by ultrasound) this started in one foot, moved into the other and I then also developed Achilles tendonitis. Tried all the usual including course of laser therapy, shockwave, shoes, strengthening exercises, massage etc. Past couple of months I've had pain in my inner knee. This has now escalated to the point it's incredibly painful to drive, struggle to turn when walking and even with knee support I occasionally feel it is going to give away. I am wondering if this is to do with my PF and if anyone else has had a similar experience? It has proved unresponsive to exercises suggested by physio. Many thanks.
