Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#9

Anonymous member 566 I got my first pair of custom (expensive!) insoles in 2012 and wore custom insoles for years. Finding out about Altra running shoes and Topo running shoes from this group in 2024 has been even better for my feet. I was wearing my insoles to help with over pronation so when I went to the Altra and Topo websites I typed in “stability” which is the keyword for running shoes that are designed for overpronation. Whatever your needs, whether you need neutral or stability, I recommend trying these brands if you haven’t already. The shoes are cheaper than custom insoles and I don’t need custom insoles when wearing Topo or Altra, the foot shaped shoe with the toe box for toe splay makes a difference 🙂

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#10

yes, this! And for the original poster, also get shoes that have enough toe box space for toe splay. Altra running shoes and Topo running shoes are foot shaped shoes that have that toe splay space.
