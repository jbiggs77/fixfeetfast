Anyone have the procedure called TenJetHydrocisiom done to eliminate PF pain? Description below: The TenJet procedure is a minimally invasive treatment for chronic plantar fasciitis. It uses high-velocity saline to remove diseased tissue, promoting faster recovery compared to traditional surgery. The procedure is performed under ultrasound guidance and typically takes about 15 TenJet procedure involves several steps: Ultrasound Guidance: The procedure is performed under ultrasound guidance to visualize the diseased tissue and the positioning of the TenJet device. Anesthesia: Local anesthetic is administered to the affected area to minimize discomfort. Incision: A small stab incision is made to create a pathway for the TenJet device. Debridement: The TenJet device is guided to the diseased tissue, and high-velocity saline is used to debride and evacuate the damaged tissue. Post-operative Care: Steri-strips are placed over the incision, and the patient can be discharged. Post-operative care may include a walking boot and physical therapy. The TenJet procedure offers several advantages: Minimally Invasive: It is a minimally invasive procedure, resulting in less pain and faster recovery compared to traditional surgery. Outpatient Procedure: The procedure can be performed in an outpatient setting. Faster Recovery: Patients typically experience relief within the first two weeks of the post-operative period.
