Community comment#2

suffered for years. I thought the pain was actually from my toenail and I kept telling podiatrists that it was so bad that I just wanted them to remove the nail permanently. I finally went to a podiatrist who insisted that it’s referred pain from a neuroma. He did a series of injections and it hasn’t bothered me since. I did not have the same luck with plantar fasciitis, but I’m really glad to be done with the neuroma. The neuroma injections are very safe. There isn’t even a steroid in them. ￼
