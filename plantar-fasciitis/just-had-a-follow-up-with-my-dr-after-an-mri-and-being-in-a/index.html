Just had a follow up with my dr after an MRI and being in a boot and non weight bearing for almost 6 week.

Archive date 2026-10-202 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Just had a follow up with my dr after an MRI and being in a boot and non weight bearing for almost 6 week. MRI (6 weeks ago) showed chronic Plantar Fasciitis, Plantar Fascia Tear, Morton's Neuroma, Bursitis in all 5 toes and something with my ankle I can't remember. No wonder I was in so much pain 2 more weeks with the boot, non weight bearing and then following up with her. Also have Plantar Fasciitis in my left foot that is very painful but no tear. Going to start PT in 3 weeks. I hate my feet!!!!!!
