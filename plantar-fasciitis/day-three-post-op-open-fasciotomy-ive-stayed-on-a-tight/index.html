Day three - post op open fasciotomy. I’ve stayed on a tight schedule of alternating every two hours Tylenol, ibuprofen, and 5 mg Oxycodone the first 36 hours. Slept well last night. Toes have thawed out. I do feel pain when I move them and a heavy feeling in the foot but manageable. Have three Oxy left for tonight and am making it through the day on ibuprofen and Tylenol and ice behind my knee. Only up to go to the bathroom. Foot elevated.
