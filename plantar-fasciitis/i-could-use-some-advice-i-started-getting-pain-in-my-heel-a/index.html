I could use some advice, I started getting pain in my heel about 2 months ago.

Archive date 2026-09-1312 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesFlat Feet & Arch SupportPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

I could use some advice, I started getting pain in my heel about 2 months ago. It got so bad I ended up seeing a doctor and he said I have plantar fasciitis in my heel and the bottom of the outside of my foot. He gave me a steroid medication and it helped a bunch but since it's back to painful. The doctor didn't give me much information but I'm assuming I'm flat footed. I ordered the arch support they recommended and started using that. Some days were bad some were good. It's been more bad than good lately so I decided to try a zoprez foot sleeve hoping for some relief but I'm on day 4 of using it and it seems like it's getting worse.i use the arch inserts in my tennis shoes daily but I guess I'm just looking for more things I can try since the $40 foot sleeve isn't helping. What has worked for you?
