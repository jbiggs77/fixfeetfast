Community comment#5

Be sure to get yourself measured. I've been wearing a size 11 regular most of my life. When I went in to get new shoes for my PF, they wanted to measure me. Turns out, I'm actually a size 12 wide. This may be the reason I developed PF in the first place. Now I'm wearing a pair of Altra Paradigm wide toe box zero-drop shoes in size 12 2e and my feet are loving it.
