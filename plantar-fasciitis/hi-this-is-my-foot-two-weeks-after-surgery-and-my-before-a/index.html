Community comment#2

My foot almost 6 weeks after surgery. The swelling is worse in the evening. I elevate my foot as much as possible & apply ice packs throughout the day. I had to use a knee scooter until 1/15 and was told to do partial weight with crutches and this Sunday I can apply full weight in the surgical boot. My follow up appointment is 1/29. I was told swelling can last for 6 months and it will take about a year to fully recover. It’s pretty nerve wracking! Btw I rode hunter/ jumpers growing up. I miss it so much but now would just want to ride western. Best to you!
