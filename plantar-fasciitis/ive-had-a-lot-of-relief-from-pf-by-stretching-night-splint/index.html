I’ve had a lot of relief from PF by stretching, night splint, toe spacers…& new balance fresh foam to help…

Archive date 2026-10-0625 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesToe Spacers, Orthotics & BracesPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

I’ve had a lot of relief from PF by stretching, night splint, toe spacers…& new balance fresh foam to help with during the day. I started with the night splint that I wore at night for a bit….but when a friend told me about toe spacers, I felt the blood circulation in my feet, I repeated this process each day for 15 minutes. Until I wore them when I went to bed, slowly the pain went away & I was able to wear my regular shoes again. BUT….my Dr said I’ll need to wear a wide toe box shoe, no more cute Nike shoes, which are to narrow….i haven’t been back to my daily running because I was told to take a break & heal. I do get a flare here & there but the pain isn’t what it was like before….I believe the toe spacers did help. I hope this helps anyone who will try anything to have relief. *stretching* - I laid on my stomach & almost like a push up but your feet are curled against the bed & I did that each morning & night. I could definitely feel that stretch.
