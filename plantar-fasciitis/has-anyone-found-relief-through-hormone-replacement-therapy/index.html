Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#27

face a counter. Step back about a yard (farther if you can) and lean forward so your hands (on the counter) support your body, which is straight like a plank. Keep your legs straight and your heels on the ground. Lean forward so your chest approaches the counter. Hold that stretch-- you should really feel it, especially the closer your chest gets to the counter. Keep your head up and don't bend at the hips. Now do it with slightly bent legs keeping the heels on the floor. Super important to do both straight and bent leg stretches. When this gets too easy, I stand on something that lets my heels drop -- such as a board. I found out I was terrible at doing the endless stretches I found on YouTube. This is easy and fast, and seems to work. I hope I was able to explain it ok, I am not a professional -- just a 65 year old woman who is sick to death of PF.
