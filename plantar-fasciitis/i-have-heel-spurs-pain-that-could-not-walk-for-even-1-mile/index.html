There are fairly many podiatrists that do heel surgery through an incision of less than 3/4 inch. Minimal incision foot surgery. I did these for over two decades. Very high success rate. No bad results

Community comment#3

I had day surgery in hospital with an orthopaedic surgeon to remove a nasty, big , heel spur. As soon as I woke up, I knew it was a success because the pain was just gone. It was a six week recovery, stiches came out after around 2 weeks, and that's when I could finally start to weight bare.
