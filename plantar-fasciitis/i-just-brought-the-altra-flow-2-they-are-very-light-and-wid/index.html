Archive date 2026-10-069 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

I just brought the Altra flow 2. They are very light and wide toe box. Really good in the heel. This is my first pair of this brand. Have pf for months and for the first time seeing a an improvement even after first day. The only thing that was sore were my legs but it was my first day using them.
