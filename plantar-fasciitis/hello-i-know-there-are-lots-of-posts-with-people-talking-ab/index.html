Community comment#2

I am similar in status, except my foot has fallen arch from bone out of place causing the flat foot issues. So... My preference of shoe and insert is Keen lightweight hiking shoes. They are not a true wide unless you order that way but they are wider than normal shoes in general. Paired with original Walk Hero inserts. Taping my feet (Kenetic tape) helped fix the worst of the pain, now I just wear the shoes and insoles and I don't tape. I did tape for almost 4 months before it really helped. I buy a new pair of shoes about annually, shoes do wear out. But they have been my best fix for the every day need.

Community comment#3

Custom orthotics can help a lot with fallen arches and PF pain in my experience. Try to find a podiatrist who does hard plastic shell insoles that can be tuned up periodically versus the rubber or cork ones. They are expensive ($900 for mine now) but they last a few years and help a lot.

Community comment#4

I have very flat wide feet. I’m wearing recovery slides in the house and hoka gaviota with superfeet insoles outside. I have also been working on walking with my feet parallel, which is a healthier alignment. Many of us who pronate walk like a duck and over time, this may cause other joint issues. I had to roll my feet in and out to stretch the muscles a bit and I’m walking straighter now.
