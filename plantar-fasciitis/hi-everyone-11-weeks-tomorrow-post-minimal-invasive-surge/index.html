Community comment#3

Your walk affects your entire body. If you favor one part (surgeriezed foot) other parts take up the slack, like your hips, knees, and back. It was hard for me to remember I could walk normally again, and after thirty years of bunions, I didn't know what normal was supposed to look like. So I just started being very conscious of how my foot hits the floor with each step. Take 20 minutes a day to practice your new normal walking gait. Check in with your back, hips and knees as you walk, see if they are doing too much and adapt your walk to take the stress off them and put it back on your foot. I still don't do well without shoes on hard surfaces. I can do soft sand or carpet, but tile or concrete I need shoes.
