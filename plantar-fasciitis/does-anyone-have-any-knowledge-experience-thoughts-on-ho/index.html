Does anyone have any knowledge / experience / thoughts on how another problem in the opposite foot could be…

Archive date 2026-09-248 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Does anyone have any knowledge / experience / thoughts on how another problem in the opposite foot could be affecting PF? I have a small bunion on my right foot. I’ve had it a few years and it doesn’t cause me much thought. Occasional discomfort and a slight decrease in mobility of that joint. My PF started in my left foot only about a year ago as my bunion became more noticeable to look it (no increase in pain or other though) It came to me that I wonder if the bunion has caused the PF in the sense that somehow, if only slight, my gait has changed for running, walking etc? I do wear a spacer for my bunion foot when I run or walk distances. I’ve had my gait checked while walking/running and been told it’s neutral by several independents… Photo of my poor feet! (Apologies if you’ve seen this post already in another group. Just trying to reach as many minds as possible! )
