So, I am not five months out.

Archive date 2026-09-126 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

So, I am not five months out. So glad I did this! I have been wearing TOPO tennis shoes and love them. Here is the deal, I dress up a bit for some classes I attend and wear Klogs (They are going out of business). They are platform no heel or wedge. I was wondering how others did wearing clogs of any type after surgery the first year. It is going to get cooler fast and Klogs are my go to. The new ones I bought last year were actually the reason I had surgery as I figured they were the only shoes that fit but the new ones nearly had me in tears wearing them on the foot I had surgery on. I even had them professionally stretched. All recommendations are welcome! Thanks in Advance!

Community comment#6

Hi Jenn, not sure if you’re talking about the specific shoe pictured or not, but if you are then I’ll mention that I saw a post by Dr Conley at a few months back. Dr Conley said that footwear needs to be secure to feet. If we wear flipflops, mules, and clogs without a heel strap then our toes claw and grip to keep the footwear on. That toe gripping action causes plantar fasciitis and bunions. So if wearing any sandals or clog type footwear it’s best to have a heel strap to keep it on. We just ordered Origo shoes for a younger family member and the quality is impressive. The leather of the Origo smells really good and the stitches look tight and seams meet well. Maybe it’s worth a look at Origo? I’ll see if I can find another brand to suggest, so many on the website are pretty but maybe too thin of a sole for your purposes?
