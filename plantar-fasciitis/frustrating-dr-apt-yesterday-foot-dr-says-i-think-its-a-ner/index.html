Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#22

yes chances are you have neuropathy from the chemo. I have 2 friends that have gone thru chemo and they each got some level of neuropathy in their feet and hands. The test for neuropathy is just a matter of the Dr using a tooth pick and poking the bottom of your feet. You may have neuropathy on the complete sole of your foot or just partial. Either way make yourself an appt with a nurse practioner or podiatrist to get your toenails trimmed and at the same time he or she will clean up calluses etc. When you have neuropathy in your feet it is very important to have your toenails trimmed and feet looked after every 6 weeks. You may have something going on that you can't feel but the nurse or podiatrist will be able to see it and treat accordingly.
