I have performed over 1500 bunion surgeries in my career minimum and I’ve been doing minimal incision for the last year or so and I would say as long as you have a good surgeon who knows what they’re doing that’s the way to go ￼

Community comment#2

I had MIS on my left foot April 2024. Started running 5 miles/day by mid July 2024 and have been very active since then. My right foot started the same progression (bunion site pain, sesamoid pain) so I had another MIS surgery on Monday. Five days in and I’m feeling good. I hate not being able to workout and drive, but overall the pain is very tolerable and I’m heel walking enough to get around the house and walk the dog. Good luck with your decision! I’m very happy with my results 🙂
