I keep seeing people mentioning oofos shoes.

Archive date 2026-10-0817 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryFlat Feet & Arch SupportPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

I keep seeing people mentioning oofos shoes. I see they have free shipping and free returns. Returns/refunds only if the shoe is unworn / original condition. Typically when a store has that policy I wash the shoe after wearing it and have been fine. I need to wear the shoes in the wild before I know that they’ll work. Does anyone have experience returning their oofos after wearing them? Are there tags on the shoes that would prevent me when washing them? Also how do these shoes hold up? Any plus size folks want to weigh in on how they hold up for you? I can’t see myself buying a $130+ pair of shoes 2-3 times a year. Is there much arch support? Good for high arches? Can they be worn like tennis shoes?

Community comment#10

Hi Jill! I've heard good things about Oofos shoes from people with foot pain and plantar fasciitis. While I don't have personal experience returning them, I do know that washing them would probably void the return policy. However, you may still be able to return them if they're not meeting your needs after trying them out for a few days. As for their durability, I've heard that they hold up pretty well, even for plus size folks. The arch support is moderate, but some people with high arches find them comfortable. They're not meant to be worn like tennis shoes, but they're great for walking and everyday wear. I hope this helps!
