For people with high arches, is there anyone who's worn Hoka's for a couple of years and still likes them?

Archive date 2026-10-2322 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryToe Spacers, Orthotics & BracesPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

For people with high arches, is there anyone who's worn Hoka's for a couple of years and still likes them? (Edit to add: can you also please include if you've been wearing hokas for more or less than 2 years? I think that would give me the enough confidence to ditch my Altras, which is what my new podiatrist recommended. I just have so much anxiety about feet because i've dealt with this for over a decade.) I have seen on here people warning that they've tried Hokas, and that even though initially they may have felt good, they've ultimately made their feet worse. And 6 months ago I switched from Altras with a custom orthotic to Hoka's, with no orthodotic, and every time I try to wear the Altras for even one hour (without an orthotic) I seem to have some kind of pain.
