Community comment#37

Think of the heel of your slides like a bucket seat in your car. When you sit in your seat, you’re more anchored in place because you’re IN the seat rather than ON the seat like an old school truck’s bench seat where you shift and slide with the turns because the seat is flat. Same thing with your feet. The heel bone is the largest bone in the foot and it also needs to be anchored in place. The foot bed of your flip flop appears to have more depth to them and that might be the deciding factor for why your feet feel better while wearing them. Your heel is resting more IN the heel counter rather than ON it or OVER it. And that helps to provide the overall relief and comfort your feet have been seeking. Since this style is limited now, you might want to explore the EVA foam Birkenstock line up. They’re built with a deeper heel counter and a responsive feel under the foot. Not as rigid and stiff like the original Birkenstock leather styles, but still supportive. From all of my experience spanning 3 decades of doing medical fittings for shoes and inserts, stick with what you’ve figured out works for you! Everybody’s feet, sensitivities, and preferences are so different! What works for one doesn’t always work for another.
