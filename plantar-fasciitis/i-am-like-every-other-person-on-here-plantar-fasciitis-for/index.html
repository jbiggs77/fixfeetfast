Community comment#3

Ok, so achilles tendon pain is misidentified as tendinitis for most people. I believe it’s roughly 80% to 90% of cases are actually tendinosis (degeneration). Tendinosis lacks active inflammation, resting and icing the area will not fix the underlying structural breakdown or dysfunction of the tendon. Instead, treating tendinosis requires rebuilding the tendon's load capacity and stimulating tissue remodeling through controlled, progressive strengthening exercises (like eccentric calf raises). This has to be done safely and efficiently. Less is more. But your tendon needs to be loaded to heal.
