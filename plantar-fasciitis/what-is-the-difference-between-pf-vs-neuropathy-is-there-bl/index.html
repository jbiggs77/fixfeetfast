Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#9

The most common tests to confirm Baxter's neuropathy are an ultrasound-guided local anesthetic injection and an MRI. The injection test involves injecting a numbing agent near the nerve, and if pain significantly decreases, Baxter's entrapment is likely. An MRI can show muscle changes related to nerve damage and is useful for excluding other diagnoses. In some cases, an EMG and nerve conduction study can also be used to confirm nerve damage.
