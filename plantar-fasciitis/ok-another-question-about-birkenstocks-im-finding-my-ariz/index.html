Ok, another question about Birkenstocks! I’m finding my Arizonas really help my Achilles tendonitis and peroneal tendonitis (I loosened up the top strap and they are finally getting comfy!) and I do think they help my PF as well. My question is - do they help to strengthen your feet? It feels like you have to “grip” a bit with your toes which is similar to some of the strengthening exercises they had me do at PT. Will this help? I’ve found overstretching has done a lot of damage and strengthening seems to be the most helpful so I am hopeful this may help! Thoughts? See less
