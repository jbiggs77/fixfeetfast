Whelp not the news I was expecting to hear but here it is.

Archive date 2026-10-176 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound HealingPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Whelp not the news I was expecting to hear but here it is. I would need another surgery on my scar, another skin graft to close the area properly. I had issues with the sutures so i dont have enough skin to make it nice and smooth. I have tendon and tissue buildup on my ankle incision which would need cut open and released. The hardware can come out bc it did its job of closing the bones together. I have severe plantar fasciitis that needs surgery. They have to cut the facial muscles to release the tension and pain. I would be out of work for 3 weeks. 1 week of nwb then 2 weeks with a boot. The plantar fasciitis wasn’t this bad back in may when I had my 6 month checkup. It’s honestly something I’m willing to consider so I’m not in excruciating pain anymore as shoes and inserts won’t be the 100% fix.
