Got results from MRI.

Archive date 2026-07-065 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Got results from MRI. Yes, to PF…. But I think that’s the least of my concerns! I have two more things going on. Something called tarsal co-ollition of sub tailor joint. Apparently I was born with it. Basically 3 bones on the inside of the ankle and one is fused to others and arthritis in the area. And….. have a longitudinal tear in the peroneus brevis tendon on the outer side of foot. That explains why I have had pain on both sides of my foot. Going to ortho specialists in a few week. Surgery may be in the future. Anyone else have these crazy things - other than PF? See less
