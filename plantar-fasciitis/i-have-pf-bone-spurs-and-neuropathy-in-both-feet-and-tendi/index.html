Managing plantar fasciitis, bone spurs and neuropathy

Archive date 2026-09-2854 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

I have PF, bone spurs, and neuropathy in both feet and tendinitis in my right ankle. I’m pretty much always in pain. And it’s caused my depression to worsen. How do yall cope with it? I had to quit my job. I can’t keep my home clean. The things I love to do, like driving or cooking, I can barely do anymore. (I do have a chair in my kitchen so I can sit down but the getting up and down constantly is killing my knees). I can barely even walk to my car nowadays because of the pain. And I don’t even want to start on the weight gain from the lack of movement…. The depression has made my insomnia (which I’ve had long before the feet issues started) so much worse. My Dr has even told me there’s nothing more for me he can do. I don’t feel like I can even try and get better if I can’t fix that first. TIA for any responses I get.
