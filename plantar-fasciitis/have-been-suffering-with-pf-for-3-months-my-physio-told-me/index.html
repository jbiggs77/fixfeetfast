Have been suffering with PF for 3 months.

Archive date 2026-10-1138 archived replies
Walking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Have been suffering with PF for 3 months. My physio told me to stop running and reduce walking which I have done during this time (and going mad as a result). I haven't improved symptoms despite strength training, stretches etc. BUT I have since seen a consultant foot surgeon and he says no reason I can't get back to running now, and slow running is equivalent to same forces as fast walking! I'm so excited to try to get back but also slightly apprehensive due to conflicting advice and not even done an hours walk for 3 months. I'm not going to jump back into say a 5k run, but could a short 20 min run/ walk be OK after this long out? Does anyone else run through PF despite ongoing symptoms?

Community comment#5

I developed PF from running. Took 3 months off also, then went back. I started with three three-miles runs per week and slowly kept increasing mileage. After two months I was at five 4 mile runs per week and could barely walk after each run. I realized I was getting worse not better. I’m now not running at all (biking, strength work, etc) and waiting to start a four week course of shockwave. The podiatrist says I can’t run during that, but hope to try again when it’s completed. My advice is is to start super slow with very low mileage. It sucks, but I think it’s the best way to approach it.

Community comment#7

I'm an avid runner for years, hence the cause of PF. Super common running injury. I completely stopped running for 4 mo, as the pain was so intense. During those mo my physical therapist had me do specific stretches (mostly with my calves), foam rolling, and additional calf strength training moves. I'm a very active person so I always maintained my normal weight lifting workouts and incline walks on the tread-making sure my pain never went above a 4. Slowly but surely my PF has subsided 99% after 6 mo, minus a few flares here and there when I over do it. I'm back to running, but not to my full potential/pre PF pace or distance. I refuse to over do it too soon and it return. I know once ya get PF we will likely get it again at some points in our lives, but I will always continue with my stretches & exercises to hopefully prolong my time period of not having it. Running is LIFE to me so returning to it finally has been heavenly. Listen to your body, recover appropriately, and do those PF stretches and exercises like it's another full time job! 😁 Good luck!
