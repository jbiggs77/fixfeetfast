My foot is still sore in a few areas and a little swollen under/pad) of foot. I know people have asked before, but I can’t find those posts and responses. Can anyone give me ideas for tennis shoes that may work? I’ve lived in ASICS for the past 10 years, since I had plantar fasciitis pain. I’m crossing my fingers I can wear these. But, I think I’m going to be disappointed when I try and can’t wear them. Any suggestions? I’d love to be able to purchase off Amazon so I can return if they don’t work. Thank you for any help!

Community comment#2

You are more likely to avoid plantar fasciitis and a recurrence of the bunion if footwear is wide toe box. Dr Perez and Dr Conley explain this at Gait Happens. I haven’t had surgery but I donated all my other running shoes and sandals once I learned about toe splay. Most of us were born with pizza slice shaped feet and pizza slice shaped footwear is better 🍕🍕 Topo Athletic Footwear and Altra Running Shoes both have their own brand websites with great return policies so trying the styles and sizes works well. I haven’t had surgery and I wear Altra Running Shoes without any insole and find them comfortable that way, just thought I’d mention in case more room is needed if there is swelling that is expected to go down. Both brands of shoes are wider in the toe box and regular fit in the midfoot and heel.
