So today I went to see a podiatrist .

Archive date 2026-09-1815 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesToe Spacers, Orthotics & Braces

So today I went to see a podiatrist . I got a Cortizone prednisone, lidocaine combined shot in my heel.. Heall pain is caused by severe bruising from plantar ￼ fascist in my arch. Over 2 months of horrible crazy pain. Shot wasn’t too bad considering the pain I’ve been struggling with. Not I have to wear my night splint every night… use ice bottle cold daily after working not 1st thing in morning. Take naproxen… oh and yes do foot calf stretches as much as I can throughout the day and before bed. Can use heating pad at night morning. Have another appointment in a week evaluation and probably another shot. My new shoes are ok but he said something about needing arch insoles. I bought some awhile ago . Not sure about having something pressing up into my arch in my shoe, being on my feet all day. Maybe without compression socks my arch would be perfect. Reason I decided to go was because my other foot was starting to give me problems. Hopefully it will improve. These shoes have made walking more doable. Lots of cushion thick bottom slight wide toe. ￼ Sketchers air code Goga, Matt wide fit tennis shoe.. it has a cushioned insole. I can’t stand shoes with hard insole. Feels like I am walking on a a brick in my shoe. ￼ ugh
