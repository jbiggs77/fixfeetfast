I’m not a doctor, but my thinking is that if it isn’t causing more pain, you’re not re-injuring it or otherwise over doing it. Lower drop shoes, like altras, will help strengthen the feet compared to cushiony, higher drop types like Hokas, so I would think that this switch would be good as long as your foot is ready for it.
