One week Post Op Appointment from Open Fasciotomy Surgery - healing well! I stayed in bed all week and only up for bathroom. Scooter is much easier than crutches. Will be non weight bearing one additional week and the stitches will be removed. Pain has been minimal at incision. Foot is still a bit numb between the ball and heal. Nerves are wakening - causing sharp, quick pain at times from toe to heal when I flex the foot. Hopeful!!!!!
