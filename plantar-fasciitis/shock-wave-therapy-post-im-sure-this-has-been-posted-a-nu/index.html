Community comment#1

Do you do flat on the floor heal raises? Both feet same time? That exercise helped me the most because it burns into your glutes. Look into gastrocnemius recession surgery. What I know now, I would of done this surgery and think I wasted about a year of severe pain. 25 years of age, way too young for this type of pain. It’s very hard to cure but I’m 95% pain free after a 20 months of strengthening my lower body. Hips, glutes, lower back and calf’s. Focusing on those instead of your feet you can get out of pain. It took a year to cure the last 20% of my pain and stiffness.
