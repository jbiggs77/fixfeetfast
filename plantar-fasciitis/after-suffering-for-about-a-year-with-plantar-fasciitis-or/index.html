After suffering for about a year with plantar fasciitis..

Archive date 2026-09-2615 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

After suffering for about a year with plantar fasciitis.. orthopedic apts , physical therapy , new shoes , inserts , all the gizmos and gadgets that I could find .. most not doing a darn thing .. what is helping me the most are a few things … I am following the advice of my orthopedic and staying off the treadmill at the gym.. only doing bike or strider and only walking for exercise in 5-10 min increments. I did splurge for the custom inserts which have helped , stretching of calves , hips , IT band but the one thing that I am giving a lot of credit to is dry needling .. I finished my second week and this has given me the most relief.. started with needles in my feet , ankles and then moved to thighs , IT band , hips and glutes .. I know it probably won’t work for everyone but I am enjoying the relief .. it’s been a long time
