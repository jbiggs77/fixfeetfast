Managing plantar fasciitis with fallen arches and heel spurs

Archive date 2026-09-2826 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesFlat Feet & Arch SupportPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Here is my story. I have PF in both feet for over a year now. According to my physical therapist, I have fallen arches and I have tendinitis. He said when I walk, my arches collapse! I also have possible neuropathy in my feet. Going for a nerve conduction study on Wednesday. I have heel spurs - probably caused by the PF. Just started my 2nd round of PT. I have had steroid injections that didn’t work (I will never do those again). I roll my feet on frozen water bottles and spiky balls. I stretch my calves. I wear Hoka Arahi wide width shoes with firm inserts I bought at the podiatrist office. But as soon as I go to the mall and try to shop a little - I think I’m gonna die from the pain! Just standing is agonizing! What in the world is wrong with this picture?
