Community comment#41

Look up and research Plantar fasciitis Embolization procedure. It’s a new procedure and I’ve had fantastic results with both feet. I had my left foot done last January 2025 and at the time there were only 6 doctors performing it here in the US and 13 in the world. While there are more doing them now, it’s still a very small number. Check your area for a provider and consider making an appointment for a consultation to see if you’re a candidate. I just had my right foot done this past Tuesday 2-3-26 and it is already greatly improved. I even went shopping for several hours Saturday with my daughter. My left foot is still completely pain free at a year post procedure.
