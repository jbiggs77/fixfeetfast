I developed this awful condition almost a year ago (thanks to my years of wearing minimalist sneakers).

Archive date 2026-10-114 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

I developed this awful condition almost a year ago (thanks to my years of wearing minimalist sneakers). It got progressively worse over the winter. In March, I saw an ortho who after an X-ray and a super quick exam, diagnosed me with PF and told me to do stretches for 4 weeks. Did the stretches and no change. Saw a PA who questioned the PF diagnosis that the doc gave me because my pain gets worse as the day goes on, not better. I then wore a boot for 5 weeks. Took it off and after a day, the pain was back. In May, the PA gave me a cortisone injection. It lasted from May until last week. Now the pain is back. Like you all, I have spent a lot of money on shoes and inserts. I wear Oofos and Hoka’s. I am on my feet a lot of the day which doesn’t help. I just started to tape my foot and I’m seeing a chiro this week to maybe get shockwave treatments. Here’s my question. I haven’t had an MRI yet, but do any of you also have no pain in the morning but terrible pain at night? Could this be something else? I’m also looking for feedback on the shockwave treatment? Thanks!
