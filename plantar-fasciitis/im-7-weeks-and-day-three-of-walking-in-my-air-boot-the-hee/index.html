I’m 7 weeks and day three of walking in my air boot. The heel of my foot and bottom of foot hurt the most when I walk. It feels like I have plantar fasciitis. Is this what others feel when they first start walking in the air boot. I had the Lapidus procedure.

Yes , I started using a crutches at first before I put my full weight on tge foot in the boot. But it will losses up. And I put my foot up alot. Just go slow and it will start feeling better

Community comment#2

Yes I got into an air cast at 7 weeks, had lapidus, second toe claw toe, plantar plate repair and I definitely felt it in my heel, ankle .. I used crutches at first for a few days and made sure I was wearing the same heel size on the non surgical foot.. Then gradually went to one crutch, then none.. When I went out though I used a cane for awhile … ..Still rested through out the day and took the boot off ..

Community comment#3

Yes. I solved the problem by putting an insole from a running shoe in the boot.
