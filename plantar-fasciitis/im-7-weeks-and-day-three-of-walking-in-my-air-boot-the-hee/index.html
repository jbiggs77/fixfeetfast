Community comment#2

Yes I got into an air cast at 7 weeks, had lapidus, second toe claw toe, plantar plate repair and I definitely felt it in my heel, ankle .. I used crutches at first for a few days and made sure I was wearing the same heel size on the non surgical foot.. Then gradually went to one crutch, then none.. When I went out though I used a cane for awhile … ..Still rested through out the day and took the boot off ..
