Just wanted to share my experience.

Archive date 2026-10-061 archived replies

Just wanted to share my experience. I have had foot pain for the past 3 months. Today I finally went to the orthopedic doctor who sent me for an x-ray, and I have a heel spur. So he has put me on a course of anti-inflammatory meds for 8 days, then I will have 10!sessions of physio. He also told me to do the toe stretch with the belt and roll my foot on an iced bottle. If none of this works we will then go to cortisone injections. He told me crocs (which I have) are the best footwear for around the house. So fingers crossed I can get back to normal and start walking my dog again without having it hobble home.
