I have tried a night splint, 8 shockwave treatments, 5 osteopath visits, orthopedic insoles, stretching and strengthening exercises (including with a ball), cooling, and massage devices. Nothing helps; I am at my wit's end. Do you have any tips? Or good stories, because I am afraid this will never go away. I am a young woman of 29.
