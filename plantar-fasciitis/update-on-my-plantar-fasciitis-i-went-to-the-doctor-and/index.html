Update on my plantar fasciitis!!

Archive date 2026-10-0945 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesToe Spacers, Orthotics & BracesPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Update on my plantar fasciitis!! I went to the doctor and she asked what my pain level was on a scale of 1-10, and I told her a 12 (I wasn’t lying)! She ended up giving me some pain meds, a night splint, stretches, and some recommendations for good shoes. I asked her about working out and she said only low-impact exercises. It’s day one and my foot feels a thousand times better. I pray I get more relief by next week because i really want to get back to my routine!
