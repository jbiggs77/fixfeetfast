Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#17

There is no quick fix. These are the things that helped me get back to running: good supportive shoes, 4 x weekly strength training; foot strength and balance, calf, glutes, hamstring strength. Dorsal flexion and big toe mobility. Daily stretching, daily walking. Calf raises whenever you can at home, walking on top toes, Using your feet to pick up marbles, vibration plate on the bottom of the foot, toe separating and toe yoga. Morning rolling on a foot roller. All of this supports the strength and development of the foot and the plantar fascia. Anti inflammatory for flare ups, consistency and determination. It is the only way. I also used a foot brace for an hour each evening to keep my foot in an upright position
