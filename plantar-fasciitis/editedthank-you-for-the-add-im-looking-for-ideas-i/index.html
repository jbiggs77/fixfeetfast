This sounds like plantar fasciitis based on what you said about pain in the arch to ball and heal and that the pain goes away when you walk on it. I had it for several months and started wearing these compression socks for it. Wore it every day for about 6 months. Haven’t had an issue since. If you try them out, they seem to run big and I had more support buying the ones that were a size smaller than recommended i.e I should be an XL but Large worked best.
