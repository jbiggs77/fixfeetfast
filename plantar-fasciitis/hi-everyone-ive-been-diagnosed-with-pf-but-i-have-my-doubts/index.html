Heel pain 4 months: is it plantar fasciitis?

Archive date 2026-07-212 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Hi Everyone Ive been diagnosed with PF but i have my doubts that is correct! My pain isn too bad in the mornings, it comes on when i stand around and walk, its so bad i limp, been going on for 4 months, no treatment alleviates it. My right foot pain is mainly inner side, back of foot, along the edge of my heel below the ankle bone that sticks out, the pain is there and right at the back of my heel and sometimes the outer edge as well. The pain is a stinging sensation and it feels like im walking on bone! Does this sound like PF to you?
