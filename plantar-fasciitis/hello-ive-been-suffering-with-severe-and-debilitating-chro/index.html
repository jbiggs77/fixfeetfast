Hello, I've been suffering with severe and debilitating chronic pain in the ball of each foot for almost two…

Archive date 2026-10-0211 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Hello, I've been suffering with severe and debilitating chronic pain in the ball of each foot for almost two years now. After seeing 12 podiatrists, the conclusion has been that my feet are fine. There is no need for fat pad injections, cortisone shots, or other strong anti-inflammatory medication. Instead an EMG revealed that I apparently have acute polyneuropathy. The pain management specialist I am seeing gave me some options, and since I wanted instant relief, a spinal cord stimulator was implanted to mollify the pain. The results were dubious, so we've graduated to using Quntenza over the course of the next 9 months to help reduce the pain. Should that not work, I've been exploring other options. I have heard that a supplement named alpha lipoic acid can be helpful. So, too, is cryotheraphy whereby the affected nerve endings are chilled to a temperature of -170C, resulting in a numbing. My reading on the subjects suggests that it can also be effective. I'm writing to see if others in the have suffered as I ave from acute foot neuropathy and to see what treatment options they have considered. If you have a moment, please let me know your stories. Thanks in advance.
