Hi bunion buddies!

Archive date 2026-09-239 archived replies
Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryFlat Feet & Arch Support

Hi bunion buddies! Had my 6 week check up this past week and was told I could ditch my boot! I came home and put on my altra’s and got to it! Was weird at first but better everyday! A little off walking in my house slippers. Both these and my sneakers have arch support, I use power step inserts in my shoes. I’ve got plantar fasciitis so it’s a must! Altras are a great choice for pre and post bunion thanks to the wide toe box! Oh and I took our new foster pup for a short walk yesterday, and since it was his first walk and also mine .. well I had to carry him for most of it but we got it done together Anywho my last scab came off near my big toe and I guess this may be one of my last posts! I have one more follow up at the end of December. This whole experience has been pretty positive! Sure the first two days were ROUGH. Then the first two weeks were a little difficult. But I am really happy with my results and I hope this can help someone else who is looking into getting bunion correction surgery. Thanks everyone for your suggestions and help along the way! Hey who knows maybe in a couple years I’ll get my right foot done. No pain yet.. so
