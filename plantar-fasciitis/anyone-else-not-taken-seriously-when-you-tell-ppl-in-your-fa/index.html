Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#34

It is hard to find what works for you sometimes. I know some of the exercises that were recommended in regular PT. Do not work for me. They hurt it worse. I do have exercises that I use but not the ones against the wall or the ones you do on the stairs. They seem to hurt me worse so I understand what you’re saying. Have you thought about going to physical therapy and asking them to do a sports medicine approach to help strengthen your supporting muscles? Somebody on this site also recommended when you step start with the heel and roll to the ball of your foot. She said it seems counterintuitive, but I think that has also helped me even though it’s painful to do that. Epsom salt foot baths for 20 to 30 minutes one to two times a day have also help me some. If you do that, be consistent for a week or two, and then if it doesn’t help, at least you tried it.
