I have been dealing with a heel spur and plantar fasciitis for approximately a year . I had shockwave therapy , took anti inflammatory medication , magnesium glyciante at night had a hormone patch and tried stretching etc . Last week I went sailing and on a boat you are barefoot most of the day . I found that by day three the pain had disappeared almost completely and I no longer have the tight feeling when waking up in the morning.
