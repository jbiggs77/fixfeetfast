Hello beautiful, strong ladies I'm having surgery next Thursday to correct my hallux valgus (bunion), and I'd really appreciate hearing from anyone who's been through it. My surgeon told me he'll be using external wires (K-wires) to keep the bones in the correct position, and my foot will be in a cast for 6 weeks. Has anyone had this type of surgery? What was your recovery like? Do you have any tips for managing the first few weeks, the cast, or the wires? I'd be grateful for any advice or experiences you can share. Thank you!

Community comment#1

I had a lapidus, second claw toe repair which I had a pin in for 7 weeks ,plus plantar plate tear done so I was non weight for 8 weeks .. I was in a cast for three weeks, then à post op walking shoe - heel walking for 4 weeks , then after my pin was removed he put me in a air cast walking boot for the last 4 weeks , all I can say is be patience with your self and take one day at a time and keep on telling yourself I’m better than last week.. Listen to your dr and elevate,24/7 during the first few weeks, take your pain meds asap, as I was saying oh I’m feeling great then the nerve block wore off and the pain was horrible and I had to play catch up .. You will feel so many aches and pains but it’s normal… Remember to buy laxatives that you can take at night as pain meds block your system up .. But remember the end is better being out of pain .. It will be two years since I have done it in September and back to walking, hiking, gym, weights it’s awesome ( just can’t do lunges on my foot as second toe is permanently straight .. Otherwise I’m great .. Good luck to you ..

Community comment#2

If you search for wire from the main page, there’s a lot of posts about it here. Make sure they give you a splint, hospital shoe or boot that extends beyond that k-wire. It’s hard not to bump into things. Ask the anesthesiologist for a long term popliteal nerve block, you won’t feel pain for days.
