Get the suede ones with the soft footbed as they are easier to break in than the regular leather ones. Only wear for a short amount of time per day while you are getting used to them. I found the wide to be too big even with bunions and wide feet. The adjustable buckle works great to create more room near the bunion area. Go to store and try on, buying on line is difficult I found.
