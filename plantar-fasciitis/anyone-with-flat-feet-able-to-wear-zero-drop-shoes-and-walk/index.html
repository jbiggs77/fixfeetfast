Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#18

here’s a summary… Professor Keith Barr of University of California is the leading edge of this research. And all top sports teams and athletes rehab their sports stars like this (ie where the money is). We do 30 second isometric holds around the connective tissue (NOT stretches) The 30 second hold is very important to overcome Stress Shielding (it is why standard eg calf raises don’t fix the injury itself they just make the surrounding area stronger) So the beginner one I show (pinned on my profile) is sitting on the floor with feet against the wall and push the wall away with the toes for 30 seconds for 4 reps with a couple of minutes rest. Twice per day, 6 hours apart. Doing this consistently got me pain free within 7 weeks of starting it (13 weeks after injury). Any questions let me know, good luck 🤞 Here is a video of one of the basic exercises from my profile...
