Swollen middle toe for 2 months: what could it be?

Archive date 2026-09-2737 archived replies
Post-Surgery Swelling & InflammationPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

I am desperate to get some advice! I have had a swollen, stiff middle toe for about 2 months. I have no pain in the toe and is not red all the time but the bottom and top of foot by the toes does have pain and swelling sometimes especially when walking for a bit. I have tried everything to reduce swelling and 2 podiatrists and my family doctor cannot tell me what is wrong. I have been limping for about 5 months due to plantar fasciitis in the OTHER foot so bearing weight on the "swollen toe" foot. Bloodwork has shown no gout and xrays show no break. My physiotherapist thinks something wrong with the tendon going to the toe. I am so frustrated and desperate for any help/advice!
