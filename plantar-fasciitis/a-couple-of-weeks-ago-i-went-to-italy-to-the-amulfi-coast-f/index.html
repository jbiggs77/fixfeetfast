A couple of weeks ago I went to Italy to the amulfi coast, for those of you who know its very mountainous with…

Archive date 2026-10-1114 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

A couple of weeks ago I went to Italy to the amulfi coast, for those of you who know its very mountainous with tons of steps and a lot of uphill walking. After a couple of days I definatly felt my calves where stretched. And a day or two later my pain had gone down to practically zero. So basically I attribute it to walking a ton uphill plus tons of steps. The pain went from 8/10 to close to nothing.
