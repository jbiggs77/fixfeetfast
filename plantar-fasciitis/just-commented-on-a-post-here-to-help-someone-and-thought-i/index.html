Just commented on a post here to help someone, and thought I should stop to share what helped me with pictures…

Archive date 2026-10-2340 archived replies
Post-Surgery Swelling & InflammationBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryFlat Feet & Arch Support

Just commented on a post here to help someone, and thought I should stop to share what helped me with pictures from previous posts on this page. I tried so many things: therapies, all the stretches and shoes and inserts and medical orthotics and supplements I possibly could. The pain lasted 4.5 years and I used a wheelchair at times like the one pictured at Disneyland, discussed surgery with my podiatrist (and was really hesitant because of how hard it was for my body to bounce back from a recent carpal tunnel surgery, how he said it was possible to not have success, and how much I hated wearing the boot). Personally I wouldn’t do softwave therapy again-it’s so expensive and I didn’t see enough impact to justify it. I’m 50-50 on cortisone injections because it helped in one foot and made the other worse. But, I would do acupuncture for plantar fasciitis 1000 times over. I would stretch in the swimming pool and workout there for low impact, and I would use a percussion instrument nightly in the arch of my foot, especially digging deep into the painful places to get the tissue to release over time. I’d find the right shoe for my foot (for me new balance 1080s and oofos at home was the best combo, though I actually can go barefoot now and feel pretty good). I think some weight loss helped too and overall a combination of those things, with nightly percussion massage and acupuncture being at the top of the list. I am so grateful for this group and the people who shared a little hope with me. I’m mostly pain free now (unless I walk several miles in a day, but it doesn’t flare to the point that percussion massage won’t do the trick to bring the inflammation down. I can even HIKE and enjoy it). I have other health things I’m working on, but I look at my feet, fully functioning and painless in a way I never fully expected, and it’s a reminder to me that I CAN heal. I hope for the same for all of you!
