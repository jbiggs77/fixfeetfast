So I have insanely high arches and always have.

Archive date 2026-09-2819 archived replies
Flat Feet & Arch SupportPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

So I have insanely high arches and always have. Many years ago - around menopause- I got neuropathy in my feet and went to the podiatrist who has done nothing but give me insoles and cream. A couple of weeks ago the back of my left heel started to hurt - especially in the morning. I have had stiffness in my legs and feet in the morning for a long time now. I bought several types of braces and they just make my feet hurt more when they are on. Does anyone know how to manage high arch version of plantar fasciitis? Is it different than the low arch version? I tried to figure out how to search to see if someone else has asked about this before, but I couldn’t find the search feature - sigh - so I apologize if I am asking a common question.

Community comment#6

I would advise consulting a qualified podiatrist. I worked as a registered nurse on a busy medical ward and developed PF. This was very painful and debilitating, especially with the kilometres a nurse working on a ward clocks up daily. I saw a qualified podiatrist and was prescribed custom made orthotics. These literally saved my life, let alone my 40 year plus working life. Don’t try over the counter remedies or shoe inserts. See a podiatrist and get the professional help that will literally save your mobility . You may well need to buy new footwear but this too is so worth it. Choose a shoe store that advertises its familiarity with specialist shoe knowledge and assistance. An example in New Zealand where I live would be Shoe Clinic. I wear Birkenstocks without the orthotics for casual wear. I chose Rockport lace ups for the kilometres I clocked up in 8 hours on an acute medical ward. These same shoes also carried me many, many kilometres on a wonderful trip around China. The orthotics have been refurbished and are still going strong after many years. I remain pain free.
