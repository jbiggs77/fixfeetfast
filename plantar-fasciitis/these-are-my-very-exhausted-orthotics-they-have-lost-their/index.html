These are my very exhausted orthotics.

Archive date 2026-09-1210 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryToe Spacers, Orthotics & BracesPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

These are my very exhausted orthotics. They have lost their supportive ability. I am waiting to have news ones made. In the meantime I’m developing plantar fasciitis, post tib tendon pain, outside of my ankles and developing a neuroma on right foot. Does anyone know of over the counter orthotics that have have the high sides to hold foot stable. Firm supportive base with some cushioning on top? Any shoe recommendations with good inside orthotic? I’m concerned I don’t want the neuroma, post tib tendon pain and plantar fasciitis to set in. So difficult to get rid of TIA

Superfeet orthodics and Topo shoes Topo has many different models of shoes with different levels of support, stability, and cushioning. I have attached a picture of a good one that helped me through surgery and than a broken bone. Community comment#2

I have tried several of the OTC orthotics listed here. They just did not work for me. I spent a small fortune on them. Thank goodness Amazon takes returns! What I don't get is how they are supposed to work because both of my feet are different. How can two of the same orthotics work with two different feet. I decided to bite the bullet and order personalized orthotics. Someone on this site suggested that I find a company who uses biomechnics to create my orthotics. Miraculously I was able to find a company near my home. My evaluation took about an hour. They use scans and computer imaging of the pressures of when you walk but also a foam imprint. I picked up my orthotics a couple of weeks ago. It takes a few days to work into them. I've gone back once for adjustments. I feel that they are helping my feet but I have another foot issue that we are working on. That is my story!
