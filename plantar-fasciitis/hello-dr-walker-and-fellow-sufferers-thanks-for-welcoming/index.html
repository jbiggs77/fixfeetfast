Archive date 2026-10-135 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Hello Dr. Walker and fellow sufferers. Thanks for welcoming me. I'm here hoping to hear some success stories about non-surgical correction of tailor's bunions and PF. I have been bothered with PF for about a year in my left heel. Several weeks ago, I tried a pair of spongy cushiony slides (as pictured on left). They helped with the PF pain ... but, after wearing occasionally and only for a few days, I developed excruciating tailor's bunions on both feet, accompanied by painful, marble-sized lumps on the bottom of each foot under each little toe ball. I can barely walk, although wide recovery-style slippers help. To be sure, the tailor's bunions have been coming on for a few years -- for a long time I've long had the protrusion at the base of the pinky toe -- but these spongy shoes broke the camel's back! My bunionettes are bruised and swollen, which has never happened before. I am wearing only open and unrestricting shoes, recovery shoes, toes socks, and toe straighteners and hoping to hear that this will work in time. Your experiences welcome. Thanks much!
