This is what my foot is like when I push my bunion in versus weight bearing.

Archive date 2026-09-228 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

This is what my foot is like when I push my bunion in versus weight bearing. My foot collapses down, my bunion is flexible. I don’t have a lot of pain but it’s very uncomfortable and driving me crazy. I wish surgery was not so difficult! I really wish there was easier way. I already use custom orthotics and have an extremely hard time finding shoes.

Community comment#2

Whether they hurt or not bunions are a structural deformity. My bunions caused me no pain for over 20 years, before the discomfort/pain started. I'm 6 weeks out from my MIS bunion surgery and have no regrets. I know the idea of surgery is a scary one, but it might be an idea just to make some initial enquiries? None of those bunion gimmicks work and I know because I tried most of them! 😂 Best of luck to you .

Community comment#3

I just had MIS on my right foot last Tuesday and I was terrified to have the surgery due to all the horrible stories I’ve heard & read. I know that Everyone’s story is different though! I’m here to tell you, I haven’t experienced any pain, discomfort or issues. My healing has been way better than I’d ever expected. Honestly I worry I’m over doing it the last few days because I’ve felt so good so I have to make myself sit still and elevate my foot! My one week checkup is this Thursday. Just DO IT!
