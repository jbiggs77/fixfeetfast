Three exercise machines that has help me out with plantar fasciitis.

Archive date 2026-07-250 archived replies
Plantar Fasciitis Treatment

Three exercise machines that has help me out with plantar fasciitis. Please stretch before exercising. 1:Hip abduction machine 2: leg curl machine 3: leg curl extension machine. Note I also had my doctor make me arched support soles for work. Off work only wore gel heel cups hope this helps.
