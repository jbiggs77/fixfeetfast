Just sharing here: Bit the bullet and bought the Brooks!

Archive date 2026-10-1886 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesFlat Feet & Arch SupportPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Just sharing here: Bit the bullet and bought the Brooks! (say that 10 times fast) I included a pic of my large bunion, and was pretty surprised that even in B width, the Adrenaline is wide enough for my bunion and it's pretty comfortable right out of the box. I'm a severe overpronator, so I'm hoping the guardrails will help. I already have the Asics Gel Kayano 31, NB 860 Fresh Foam v13, Hoka Arahi 7 & Gaviota 5, and now this. Thinking to say goodbye to the Asics soon, even in a Wide width my bunion hurts in them after an hour I'm spending way too much money, and it's so tiring trying to find THE perfect shoe. My podiatrist recommended shoes with good ankle support, so I'm going for that. But I'm starting to wonder, maybe it's all about the insoles? Like I can get shoes with the correct uppers (eg wide fit for bunions) and ankle support, but don't have to worry too much about the inside because it'll be ok with the right insoles?
