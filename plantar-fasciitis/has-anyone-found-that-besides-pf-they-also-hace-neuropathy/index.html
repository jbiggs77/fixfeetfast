this time last year I could barely walk. I do a lot of PF stretches every day and use a massage gun. For neuropathy I take 60mg of Cymbalta daily. I am back to walking about 8000 steps a day and doing things with my family again. I still have pain but it is decreased a lot in the last couple of months. I also am very careful about what shoes I wear and not overdoing it when I start to have pain.
