Dry needling just done. Best it been in a long time. Had a major flair up, extremely painful both feet. Dr gave me my first cortisone shot in worse foot. Taped both. Ordered custom orthodics (years ago had one made from a cast, this one was digital). Pain was bearable considering how bad I was. Week later was a little better, but still lot of pain. She suggested dry needling that she heard some positive things from it, though it be worth a chance. I HATE needles, but desperate! Physical therapy gave me couple of exercises and did dry needling afterwards. 4 total needles, one inside and outside of calves on both legs. Next morning basically no pain, was a miracle for me. He started with my calves cause I felt tight and wanted to see how much it would help. He told me probably be 2-3 times before I would notice anything. He was surprised I got so much relief from one time. I did my 2nd one, pretty good, some pain, not terrible...but, right after my 2nd session when I should have been taking it easy, I was unexpectedly going up and down a very uneven and rocky hillside after goats, because was emergency to get couple separated. I don't know what I would have been able to do couple of weeks before this.
