I am 4 weeks + 1 day PO.

Archive date 2026-04-046 archived replies
Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

I am 4 weeks + 1 day PO. I had lapiplasty on NYE. Today i graduated to a boot. I feel great so far. And am excited to get moving again. The hardest part of all this for me has been being entirely dependent. My husband has been AMAZING. I am hoping to get in a tennis shoe in two weeks. I have included pics for you. I followed my docs instructions and took the pain meds. I am in MO, USA.
