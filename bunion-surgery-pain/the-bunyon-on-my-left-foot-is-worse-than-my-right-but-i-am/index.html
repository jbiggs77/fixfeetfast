The bunyon on my left foot is worse than my right, but I am by no means debilitated by it.

Archive date 2026-10-183 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesFlat Feet & Arch SupportPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

The bunyon on my left foot is worse than my right, but I am by no means debilitated by it. However, in the past few months I have developed a callous on the left side of my left foot and occasionally I have terrible shooting pains in the heel area - actually the heel and part of the arch area. Another friend had surgery in January and the recovery sounds just awful, so I want to avoid that if I can. A friend uses Correct Toes and over the course of a year she has seen a change in her bunyons, so I ordered a pair and wear them but should be wearing them more often I think. Anyway, is the heel pain related to the bunyon? How do I get rid of the callous and what can I do for the heel pain? I've been using a tennis ball under the foot, as a friend who has plantar faciatis suggested.
