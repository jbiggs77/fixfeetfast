Community comment#1

Aching feet is nothing compared to MN. Generally speaking most people with MN suggest it's like walking on a pebble or a rolled up sock just under the base of your toes but symptoms vary greatly. I have 2 x MN in my right foot and had one in my left foot that I have had removed. The 2 in my right foot have been decompressed but I still have a high amount of pain. Most of my pain presents itself as severe stinging in all of my toes that spreads across the whole foot. It took me over 4 years to get a diagnosis after suffering for 10+ years and it was diagnosed by someone who specialises in MN with a physical examination for the Mulder's click and then an ultrasound by someone very skilled in looking for them. There is a great Facebook support group you can join to get loads of info to see if you think you've got it. Search Support For Mortens Neuroma on your search bar on Facebook
