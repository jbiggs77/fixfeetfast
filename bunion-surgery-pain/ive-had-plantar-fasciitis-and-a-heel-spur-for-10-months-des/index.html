As horrible as it is..it will resolve and eventually go away. Ortho doctor told me average is 9-12 mos. For me i had it SEVERE in the left foot. Had to stop all my workout yoga/ Pilates classes, daily hikes, everytime i tried to start back up, flared up worse. HORRIBLE. After 14 mos.. Gone …and I did EVERYTHING…nothing really helped all that much Then after 6 weeks or normal feet…..it flared up in my right foot — 7 mos intermittent, this time i am doing massage, stretch, KT tape and isometric loading.. pain is rarely above a 2 and i am having pain free days mixed in. This week I walked or hiked 3-5 miles per day, back to baseline by morning. There is no quick fix. Doctor and other health professionals told me anything surgical is a 50/50 shot and almost all create more and new foot problems and pain. Hang in there
