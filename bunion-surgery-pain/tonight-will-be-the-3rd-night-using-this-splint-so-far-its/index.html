Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#14

podiatrist here. The point of wearing it at night is that your PF will be at maximum stretch, which is the same as when we weight bear. At night the ligament tissue will start repairing at maximum stretch, in the morning you don't therefore get the pain of these new repairs retearing as they repaired at maximum stretch. There are similar devices on the market that are made from fabric, which look more comfortable at night. I agree these look to bulky for bed time use.
