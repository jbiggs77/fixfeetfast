Feeling fed up. Nearly 3 weeks post op. Stitches came out on Monday and now I feel like I'm kind of in limbo. Dr suggested I try wearing trainers every day for as long as it's comfortable. My foot just hurts so much and it's torture even trying to put a sock on over the scars. The nerve pain is ridiculous and on a night my foot is freezing cold and my toe just feels like it doesn't belong to my body. Compared to others in this group, my surgery wasn't too bad MIS akin but I wonder when it will start to feel half normal again.
