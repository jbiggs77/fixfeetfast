Community comment#1

I had minimally invasive surgery done January 5, 2025. I am 78 and had no issues. No pain needing medication after the anti-inflammatories of the first week. Bunions never get better. Mine were present but not bothering me for about 20 years, and then went from 0 -100 pain in three weeks, so I decided to get it done. My doctor said my general good health and fitness helped my recovery, so I would say to take a few months to get fit before surgery and that will help recovery.
