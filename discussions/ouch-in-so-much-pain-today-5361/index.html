I would get a second opinion on minimally invasive surgery. That’s a lot of work to be done and could cause serious nerve damage. I had MI surgery on far less than that and I have HORRIBLE neuropathy and nerve damage & recurring bunion. The podiatrist I just saw told me he doesn’t even do it anymore for this exact reason. Now I’m facing a surgery to attempt to repair what the first podiatrist messed up. And there’s no guarantee it will help.
