Red light is great for healing. The trick is consistency. You need to do it daily and for recovery like this 2 sessions a day for 20-30 minutes is best. I personally invested in a Kala panel about 5 years ago and I use it daily just for health and skin benefits. It has also been used for a brain surgery recovery, hysterectomy recovery and most recently bunion surgery. My surgeon has been very happy with my recovery. I am about 2.5 Months post op and have almost full range of motion back. I am walking daily on the treadmill for 45 minutes. I would say it is worth the invest as you can continue to use it even once you are healed. They are great for skin health. I sit infront of my panel for 10 minutes and I find it helps my skin look more youthful. No one ever believes my age. I am 45 and usually told I look 35 at the most. I will take it! lol
