Had my first week check up.

Archive date 2026-10-063 archived replies
Walking & Weight Bearing After SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound Healing

Had my first week check up. Doc says I'm doing really good! Stitches will come out next Thursday . Pin in pinky toe will come out in 5 weeks if I can tolerate it. I get to go back to work after the stitches come out. Hubby will have to get me back and forth to work because I will still have the boot on my right foot. Im able to walk baby steps with the boot on. Just wanted everyone to know, this is going way better than I thought it would!
