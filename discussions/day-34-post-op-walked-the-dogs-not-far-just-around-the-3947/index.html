Day 34 post-op. Walked the dogs! Not far, just around the block. I have been scared to do this because they are pullers and take off after bunnies, squirrels, etc. They pulled hard once, and I did not lose my balance. That was scary but also gave me confidence. One of the life things I have missed is walking them a few miles a day. It all starts with one around the block. 😀 Today is my third day of doing PT exercises, and I feel like mobility is retuning slowly. It is not too bad lifting the toe up. But the toe scrunches trying to pick up a towel are all about effort and not the goal. The physical therapist said that I just need to make an effort, even if I cannot scrunch. I have been wearing my Crocs all the time at home, and the PT wants me to wear my Hokas more, trying to extend them time I can tolerate them. I have them on now. Let's see how long I can go.
