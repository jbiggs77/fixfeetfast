Just an update! Good morning y’all. I had r-foot traditional bunion surgery, including a bone graft, on June…

Archive date 2026-09-1320 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryPost-Surgery Swelling & InflammationPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryScarf & Akin Osteotomy

Just an update! Good morning y’all. I had r-foot traditional bunion surgery, including a bone graft, on June 24th of this year. The procedure was Scarf/Reese and I have permanent hardware (2 tiger screws). I was cleared to return to work full-time without restrictions at 9wks post-op on August 27th. I’m very happy to report that work has been going fairly well and I’ve gotten back into the “groove of things”. The stiffness in my toe remains, just not as much as it was before. Dorsiflexion exercises, massages, standing/seated heel raises and walking all continue to aid in decreasing that. I don’t have any “constant” pain. However, those unwelcome intermittent “zingers” have returned. The “zingers” only happen each morning after I’ve clocked out of work, are localized to my great toe and last until about early afternoon. I’m sure it’s due to the constant standing and walking. My ankle has started swelling a bit as well. I haven’t been as diligent as I intended with wearing a compression stocking when working like I said I would. I have returned to elevating and applying cold therapy in the mornings. Gosh, that helps so much! This recovery is something else, that’s for sure. Hope you’re all doing and healing well.
