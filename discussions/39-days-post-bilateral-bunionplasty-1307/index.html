39 days post bilateral bunionplasty.

Archive date 2026-05-195 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryPain Management After Foot Surgery

39 days post bilateral bunionplasty. I think I turned the corner. Nerve pain not as bad. I can manage to do a load of laundry and load/unload dishwasher. Friendly advice. Don’t overdue it. I lost muscle in my whole body and tweaked my back unloading the dishwasher. Now my back hurts worse than my feet.
