10 weeks post op, still in boot. My bone wasn’t fused at 6 weeks. I have to keep my foot up all the time. My foot can’t dangle. To my understanding this has been the norm for a lot of people. Community comment#7

I had lapiplasty on 2/28 and started physical therapy three weeks ago. It is helping a lot with stiffness, balance, and strengthening my ankle. In turn it helps with my walking. I’ve also started walking on my treadmill at a slow pace and it’s not bad at all. The home exercises you’re given to do from physical therapy helps as well. All in all you just have to be intentional each day with doing the exercises, icing/elevation as needed, and most of all being patient. I’m thankful that my recovery is going well so far.
