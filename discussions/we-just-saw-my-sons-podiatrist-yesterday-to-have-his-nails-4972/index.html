Managing toenail fungus: topical treatment challenges

Archive date 2026-09-1236 archived replies
Toenail Fungus Treatment

We just saw my sons Podiatrist yesterday, to have his nails trimmed, and check on his toenail fungus. He is 34 years old, and has Down Syndrome. He has a lift in one shoe, and plastic inserts in both. I wonder if that situation has contributed to his fungus.... He had a terrible case of Athlete's Foot, since last fall, that I, successfuly treated, with vinegar/water soaks, and Tinactin spray. We have been using Tolcylen, a topical medication, for 2 months, with no improvement. The tube, that can only be purchased from the doctor's office, for $50 (!!!), lasted 4 weeks, NOT the 6 months that the doctor suggested!!! We are on our 2nd tube, and probably our last! The Podiatrist said that just choosing to NOT treat the fungus would be okay. It's unsightly, but won't turn into any other health problems. I was considering the oral Lamisil, but am concerned about side effects, and potential liver damage. I happened to speak with a retired Podiatrist recently, and his advice was to take the oral Lamisil, after having a Liver Function test, before, during, and after the 3 month dosage. He said that that was the ONLY way to successfuly get rid of toe fungus. And, that in all his years of practicing Podiatry, he never had one patient that suffered liver damage. The current Podiatrist says that if he prescribes the oral Lamisil, my son would still need to also use the $50-a-small-tube Tolcylen... Hahaha! I'm just hoping for some testimonials....
