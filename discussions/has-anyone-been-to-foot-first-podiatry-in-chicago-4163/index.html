My bunion progressed so bad that my big toe is touching the middle toe and the 2nd toe sitting on top, spacers help relieve but to some extent only. All went well and the medical team is fantastic! Im now focusing on recovery. I was asleep during the procedure (general anaesthesia) and was given nerve block. Surgery commenced 2:30pm. I was woken up at 4:20pm in recovery room to tell me it was all done. I was oblivious! I was freaking out about the surgery for no reason, reading too much internet will play tricks in your mind. Its almost 24 hrs post op and I can weight bear as tolerated with some crutches. Im getting on top of pain management strictly following dr’s orders. My BP drops so I am light headed, i needed to be supervised when going to toilet to avoid falls. I am a triathlete and running with bunion is a torture. My coach who is now in his 70’s had MIBS in 2024 to both his feet, he’s back training in just a couple of months post op (he just finished another ironman this year). I am permitted to go back swim training once my wounds are healed, and then slowly progressing back to cycling (unclipping bike shoes will be a challenge) then last get back to running.
