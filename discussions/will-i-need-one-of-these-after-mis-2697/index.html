Community comment#4

I’m 3 weeks post op and still taking ibuprofen and Tylenol around the clock. When I had the dressing changed at 1 week, my foot was unbelievably tender. I was terrified for anything to touch it. A friend who is a PT recommended that I use a dry washcloth and gently rub it across my foot for a minute or so to wake up the nerves and shock them out of being on high alert. I did this a few times a day and it really helped with decreasing sensitivity. You could also try icing and elevating.
