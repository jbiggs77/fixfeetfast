Is there such a thing as a big toe joint replacement?

Archive date 2026-09-232 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryPain Management After Foot Surgery

Is there such a thing as a big toe joint replacement? I have been dealing with chronic pain for years as a result of botched bunionectomy. The botched surgery left me with horrible sesamoiditis, which eventually led to a sesamoidectomy (can’t recall at the moment whether it was tibial or medial, but it did nothing for the ball of foot pain). I have very little hope, but always hoping that there will be medical advances for foot surgery. The bottom of my foot protrudes so much that most toes don’t touch the ground and all the pressure just hits the one area. The onset of cooler weather and humidity always makes it worse so today just sucks in general. ￼
