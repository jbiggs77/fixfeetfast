Mine does the exact same thing. I often wear Correct Toes spacers and that helps a lot. Community comment#2

When you are looking at twisted toes, you need to look at what is going on in the previous joints toward the foot. They will impact the position of the toes. Sometimes, in the case of the big toe, what is going on at the thigh will impact the position of the big toe.
