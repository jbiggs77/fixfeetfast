Hello, I am a 50 year old white male, A few weeks ago I woke up with my right foot hurting a little, it was…

Archive date 2026-09-146 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

Hello, I am a 50 year old white male, A few weeks ago I woke up with my right foot hurting a little, it was the a joint just below the ankle and hurt when pressure was applied, lasted about 2 days and got worse as time went on, it went away, then the next day my left foot had the same pain except it was in the middle but low on the foot in the middle toe top joint, hurt for a day, I was limping around. Then the left foot happened 2 days later, this time it was the big toe, very very painful, couldnt walk at all, dealt with it for 2 days and then I couldnt take it anymore and went to the emergency room, I had to hop on one foot. I was there for a total of 7 minutes, the doctor barely even looked at me and immediately told me I had cellulitis and prescribed ibuprofen 800s and an anti inflammatory and told me to stay off it and raise it up. They gave me crutches as well, after asking. I took the pills as directed and yes it went away within a couple days after that. It came back on the right foot on a different joint and I am limping around again. Now it hurts when I bend the sides of my foot downward. Flexing the foot back and forth hurt. I have not had a fever, the first 2 times it happened the foot did swell, pretty much all over the foot, especially the top. Not really hot and not very red at all any of the times. My chatGBT symptom checker said it might be gout, but Im not really thinking that. Before these problems I was drinking 1 and sometimes 2 mikes hard lemonades a day, I love red meat and seafood, not much of a sugar and sweets eater. I have been drinking more water as well. Any help would be greatly appreciated, as I dont have health insurance. I just moved to Florida and haven't gotten established yet. 5' 9" 240 lbs
