I had my MTP fusion and bunionectomy April 10.

Archive date 2026-10-1318 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot Surgery

I had my MTP fusion and bunionectomy April 10. Fast forward to today, I just completed a 4-day trip to Disney World. Well over 20k steps each day in the absolutely blazing heat. The recovery has been a long road and I felt so discouraged at times. But recovery is possible and anyone who is feeling down and depressed can use my success story to motivate you through your pain. Shout out to my Hoka Cliftons and recovery slides for supporting me!
