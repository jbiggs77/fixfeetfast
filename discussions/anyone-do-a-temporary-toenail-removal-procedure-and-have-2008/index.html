Community comment#2

I had a partial/temporary nail removal on both big toes. I had 5-6 layers of nail on each of them and he removed all but the base layer on each, and buzzed/buffed the bottom layer down. But he’s not having me on a topical antifungal. He has me on an oral antifungal (Terbinafine) on and off over the next year…on my “off” months, I’m getting laser treatments. So I’m really hoping this type of treatment helps keep the fungus away. I don’t want to do this again.
