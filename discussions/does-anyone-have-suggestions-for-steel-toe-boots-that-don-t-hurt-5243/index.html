Does anyone have suggestions for steel toe boots that don't hurt?

Archive date 2026-10-141 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryToe Spacers, Orthotics & Braces

Does anyone have suggestions for steel toe boots that don't hurt? I'm pretty sure my boots are the root of my pain. I had timberline pros and used them for YEARS, but my new pair, I believe, changed something and that's when this flare up started over a year ago. It's become absolute pain in the past 6 months and I bought new composite toe wolverines. No better. I've tried every insole in the world and every combination of them. It's excruciating to walk until I get them off every day. My next try may be keens. Has anyone had experience with those? I have to have steel or composite because I work with horses (I'm a farrier) and they like to aim for your toes. I also don't like broken feet. When I take these boots off in the afternoon, I am pain free. My teva moosh flip flops feel wonderful all day. I can go barefoot. My running shoes don't hurt unless my feet are aggravated and inflamed by the boots. I need better boots. Help!!
