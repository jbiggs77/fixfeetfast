Community comment#1

Look for "wide toe box" shoes. Trust me on this one, I learned the hard way! Wide toe box shoes are totally different than "wide" shoes. Easily found U.S. brands that have wide toe box shoes, are: Altra and Topos in my city. OR, go on Amazon and search "wide toe box shoes for women" and you will see all kinds of them in "off" brands. Ignore reviews on Amazon that say they are too big in the toes-that's what you'd actually want 😉 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=klBBgtOJtT0.
