Update from my post last night about me falling I got the okay to start walking in my boot without my crutches!!

Archive date 2026-10-2014 archived replies
Walking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

Update from my post last night about me falling I got the okay to start walking in my boot without my crutches!! My x-rays looked good and my doctor said since my fall happened so far into my healing process nothing bad happened. But I’d love some input on how you all started weight bearing on your foot after not walking on it for 7+ weeks? I feel like a new born baby, I swear. I thought it’d be a little easier but I guess I’m just scared to put my full weight down and pull my other foot off of the ground. What did you all do to start weight bearing and walking around for the first time? How many hours a day did you walk for? Did you go back to work right away? I’m a waitress so I feel like it will be harder for me to get back into my 12 + hour days but man do I miss it!

Good news, glad you are ok! When I transitioned back to weight bearing (with my left foot, two years ago), I was in a boot and used a walker for support. I started with 25% wb for a week, then 50% wb for two weeks, then finally 100%. I'm recovering from my right foot surgery now, and find out this afternoon if I can start transitioning to wb yet... Fingers crossed!

Community comment#5

It was hard to go weight bearing after not walking on it for so long. I found a youtube video that helped me. I would stand by my kitchen island, put my bad foot down and slowly transfer the weight to it. Eventually you can slowly pull up your good foot, putting more weight on the bad foot. Then soon you can take a step with the good foot, bearing weight on the bad one. It was weird not "knowing how" to walk! Hope that helps!!
