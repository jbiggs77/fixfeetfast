Round one complete.

Archive date 2026-09-2814 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound HealingFlat Feet & Arch Support

Round one complete. Left the hospital at the 10:30 AM this morning. As of now, the nerve block has mostly held out and it I had a mix of general with it. Have some tingling (I have RSL from stenosis only a amphlication of the usual for me) and a few little pin pricks of pain, but otherwise so far so good. Have been advised to sleep with the boot on tonight. I return in two weeks. Because I have flat feet (collapsing arches with weight) and my age, doc decided to go conservative and forgo any upper foot fusion in case I need something done for that later. I will say, having a mild UTI had about killed me with this though. At least the bathroom is only about 10 feet away.
