20 days post-op...

Archive date 2026-07-194 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Pain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

20 days post-op... And for the first time since the Macarena hit the charts back in the late 90's, I’m travelling with one bunion instead of two. And this has been the MOST challenging post op situation I've put myself in. Moving through a busy international airport, I spent most of the day on my feet; waiting in queues, wheeling luggage, carrying a rucksack, and giving snacks with no nutritional value to my kids. Although I do have wide toe box sandals, wearing my orthopedic boot was DEFINITELY the right call... because of the extra space around the foot, nothing felt too tight or rubbed against my healing wounds... Not to mention, my "exaggerated" limp around the airport was so good, I think I might nab an Emmy nomination. But here's the irony... The "tight" or "throbbing" sensation in my right foot has been far easier to manage than a day with both my bunions at their worst! So I will continue with my mobility exercises, hydration, increased vitamin D & K2 and utilise my secret weapon... sleep (which I'm pretty good at! No one has to be Dave Goggins here, as everyone's recovery is going to be individual. I wouldn't recommend anyone travel unless you want or have to... and if you do... you MUST build in elevation and rest as much as possible, which is pretty easy to do on a beach holiday. Look after yourselves my friends. See less
