For over a year now I've been working on going somewhat barefoot.

Archive date 2026-04-220 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesToe Spacers, Orthotics & Braces

For over a year now I've been working on going somewhat barefoot. Seems it's not doing my feet any good. I do wear toe separators (not 100% of the time) and in that time have developed a tailors Bunion (left foot). Little toe still wants to move "in". 2nd and 3rd toe seem "displaced on both feet. No I'm not wearing arrow shoes. Have been doing foot exercises...guess it's off to the foot doctor...just hope he can help with prescribing very costly orthotics.
