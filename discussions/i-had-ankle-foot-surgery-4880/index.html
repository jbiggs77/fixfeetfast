I had ankle/foot surgery. The surgeries I had were peroneus longus to brevis transfer, excision OS peroneum, and lateralizing calcaneal osteotomy. I was told my recovery would be 6-12 months. Well it's been over 14 months and the pain is 10 X worse then before I had the surgery. Standing and walking on it for any length of time is extremely painful and causes my ankle to bulge out. Any suggestions on what I can do to help alleviate the pain besides putting it up, icing it and taking anti inflammatories? Thank you in advance.
