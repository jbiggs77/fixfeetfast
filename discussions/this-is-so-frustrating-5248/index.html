This is so frustrating.

Archive date 2026-10-1414 archived replies
Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryPost-Surgery Swelling & InflammationPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryScarf & Akin Osteotomy

This is so frustrating. After 2 surgeries (failed Lapiplasty non union, revision lapidus) I have horrible pain in areas not even touched by procedured. 1. Navicular area pain started after first surgery. It feels like a small lump in my shoe when walking and the pain is a ln intense bruising link pain. No abnormal X-rays. It’s now last for 18 mths. I have been in 6 mths of PT. Range of motion and strength and stability exercises. It won’t go away. 2. Constant swelling bulge on top of my foot. Since the revision surgery. Doesn’t go down. Doesn’t hurt but no answers from doctor. Said it’s just inflammation. I feel it’s a signal something not right. Had an injection here and it seemed to work for about 2 weeks then pain is back. 3. Sharp pinchy impingement pain in front of ankle when walking. Prevents dorsiflexion bc it hurts so bad. Had an injection in ankle joint capsule and it didn’t work. PT hasn’t worked either. Been going on for 5 months. Dr. Has no answers. PT thinks it’s scar tissue impinging the tendon. 4 constantnt puffiness and pins and needles and sensitivity in the ball of foot toes area. PT focus on stretch long gastrocs and Achilles and range of motion. Frustrating bc after almost 6 mths PO and PT the pains have not subsided and it prevent me from walking on the foot. Thoughts on these adjacent pains and how to relieve.

Community comment#3

It’s pronation and forced medial column dorsiflexion that is causing the non-union and navicular pain. The bulge is from the screw at 2nd TMTJ fusion. Kind of a strange approach instead of throwing the screw from the metatarsal side first. Probably have a small herniation in the deep fascia allowing a small muscle to bulge through. Ankle impingement likely just tight calf muscle. Surgical plan… 1. Medial displacement calcaneal osteotomy 2. Gastrocnemius recession or Achilles lengthening 3. Revision 1st TMTJ and add naviculocuneiform joint fusion 4. Hardware removal of 2nd TMTJ screw and repair of fascia defect. 5. Removal of the small os tibiale external bone at the navicular insertion with tendon repair as needed.
