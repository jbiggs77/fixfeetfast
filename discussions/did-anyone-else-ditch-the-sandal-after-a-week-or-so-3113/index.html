Did anyone else ditch the sandal after a week or so? I feel walking on my heel on the side of my foot is easier than clamping around in that thing.

Community comment#1

My dr did not want me walking on my heel. He said to take short steps with my feet flat.

Community comment#2

I am two weeks out and the sandal is driving me nuts because of being uneven with my other foot. I just ordered something off Amazon that goes on my good foot under the shoe that is supposed to help. My doctor doesn’t want me to try regular shoes for another four weeks. I was going to ask him about a Birkenstock sandal.

Community comment#3

Im 4 weeks post op and ive only just started to take my sandal off here and there. But i put it back on if it starts to ache. I got my dressing wet after week 2 and had to have my dressing replaced. My surgeon was very adamant I keep wearing my sandal until the 15th of this month, 5 weeks post op. Id ask your surgeon on your two week check up what they suggest. Your foot will change massively over the next few weeks and you don't want to rush your recovery 🙂

Community comment#4

I wore my offloader heel shoe for 8 weeks with a high New Balance trainer on my good foot to even height. Was okay with it but glad to finally go into trainers again.

Community comment#5

Ladies you need to wear the shoe for the specified time you were told. The shoe gets easier to wear after 2-3 weeks as your bodies mechanics have to adjust. I do agree that both surgical shoe and other need to be the same height. Dr Chelin

Community comment#6

I wore mine for weeks because my foot was too swollen for any shoe🤦‍♀️ though my tennis shoe was about the same hight so it was very comfortable 🤷‍♀️
