Surgery Complications & Wound Healing

A bone infection that developed following an injury to both feet is a serious situation that calls for prompt, thorough treatment, and we want our Lancaster County patients to understand exactly what that involves. Treatment typically starts with a targeted, often extended course of antibiotics, guided by cultures to ensure we're treating the specific bacteria involved in each affected foot. For some cases, particularly when caught relatively early, this antibiotic-focused approach can bring the infection under control. However, bone infections are notoriously difficult to fully clear with antibiotics alone, and surgical debridement to remove infected bone and tissue is frequently the most effective way to eliminate the infection source and allow proper healing to move forward, especially when both feet are involved. If you have a bone infection following an injury, please don't wait to be seen.
