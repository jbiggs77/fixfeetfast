Great question!!! It took me weeks to see progress. It’s not easy. Nothing is with the feet. Lol. Try shifting your weight slightly to the outside part of your foot with your toes flat. This naturally raises the arch dome. And in due time, you will get stronger and have more control. Stay with it. Rome wasn’t built overnight. Best of Luck

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#3

I’m no shoe expert by any means but I can give you my opinion. You are only 20% from the finish line. Your feet seems happy with your shoe combo and your rehab routine. Continue doing what you are doing and don’t change squat. PF is such a slow healing process…and extremely temperamental to quick change…Don’t look at day to day progress but rather week over week or month over month Having said all that…my rehab journey from start to finish taught me several lessons. Anything that goes on my feet gets microdosed…I will only wear wide toebox shoes with toe socks…I will always stretch before any physical activity…And I will never neglect the strengthening of my posterior chain again
