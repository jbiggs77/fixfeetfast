I’d like to share a couple things I do that have helped my pain go from a 4 to a 1, on a scale of 1-5.

Archive date 2026-10-1912 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

I’d like to share a couple things I do that have helped my pain go from a 4 to a 1, on a scale of 1-5. I’m sure they’re unconventional, but they’ve worked for me. I was dx’d with PF on 1/27 by a podiatrist. She gave me some stretches to do and sent me on my way with an appointment in 4 weeks if the pain hadn’t been reduced by 50%. I canceled my appointment. I started with the daily stretching, then added a balance board after doing some research. I bought shoes to continue my daily 3 mile hikes after the dr told me walking was ok. My dog and I were both happy to return to our routine. Things improved a bit. Then I started doing dance videos and rebounding, both barefoot. My pain greatly reduced with these, I feel like the dance is like the work on the balance board and stretching at the same time. Plus I’m having fun so I stick to it. The rebounding I usually do just before bed and it stretches out my foot - my movement is something like jumping rope. I don’t think you need to specifically do these activities, but I want to encourage people to try something unconventional. My calves are not the issue, I’m sure it’s the arthritis in my hip instead and I’m doing exercises for my hips as well. I used to be barefoot 95% of my day as a sahm, but that’s what caused the problem. I’m not sure how to get back fully to where I was, but I’m still working on it. I was hoping this might encourage some of you because I’ve seen some really saddening posts lately. Everyone is different and I don’t think the fix is the same so you have to figure out what works for you.
