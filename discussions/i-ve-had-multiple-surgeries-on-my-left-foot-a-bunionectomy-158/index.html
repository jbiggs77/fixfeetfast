I've had multiple surgeries on my left foot, a bunionectomy, 1st MTP fusion and sesamoid removal. I don't have much fat padding on the bottom of my foot. I saw a podiatrist who wants to give me a cortisone injection since I'm still having pain on the ball of my foot. I'm leery to get a cortisone shot since I don't have much padding now, does anyone have any recommendations?

Community comment#1

I had injection for about 2 years every few months which helped with the pain ( ball of foot) but now I’ve been told by the surgeon that the injections ultimately progressed my hammertoe much faster . Also, it weakened my bones enough to have a fracture on hammertoe just from imbalanced walking. Now- I think it’s safe to have one injection and get some pain relief but I’d research before you go further. I needed planters plate repair along with my bunionectomy, hammertoe correction so hopefully ball of foot pain will be gone . Best to you . The injection itself done correctly is no big deal. They spray freeze as they inject so not that painful. For me anyway!!

Community comment#2

Cortisone injections can damage bones and soft tissue. I regret having the couple that I had for hallux rigidus (big toe arthritis). I believe they contributed to the breakdown of my cartilage. I now have an implant. I don't understand what the specific reason for the injection is for you since you no longer have a big toe joint or your sesamoid bones. You might ask the podiatrist what the purpose of the cortisone injection is. If it's just to (temporarily) remove inflammation without looking into the cause, that does not seem like a long-term solution and could be damaging. You could always get a second or third opinion.
