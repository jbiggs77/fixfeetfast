Community comment#9

Did you have both feet done? My bunion reoccurred 3 months post op, it can happen, if you operated your right foot then yea, it did come back. After my second surgery, it's been like 8/9 months since and I can already see my big toe trying to shift inwards again. It's imperative that you do all the preventative measures, such as physio (specifically targeting your intrinsic foot muscles), correct footwear, custom orthotics and overall, id consider seeing a podiatrist to get your gait checked. Bunions are a byproduct of poor biomechanics, if you fix the bunion but not the cause, you might find it'll come back again (what happened to me)
