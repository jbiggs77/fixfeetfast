I just spoke to my flight attendant in flight with Southwest. She thanked me for forgiving her one of those little coupons that you could give to Southwest staff for their good work. I received these in the mail from Southwest. She told me that she can enter this coupon￼ in a drawing to win something. She was very excited. So please, if you have received these little coupons to give away, please do so to those who deserve it.
