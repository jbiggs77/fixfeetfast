I wanted to share that I refused the typical bunion surgery.

Archive date 2026-10-1527 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryMinimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Lapiplasty 3D Bunion Surgery

I wanted to share that I refused the typical bunion surgery. My photos demonstrate why. The one with all the bone cut out is a typical. This of course doesn’t rotate the metatarsal back in place enough- which is the cause of a bunion, and it’s taking away a great deal of the joint support that is needed by cutting away bone. I opted for a 3-D Lapipasty But if your bunion is not overly severe you would be better off getting the Syndesmosis. My photos are the ones with my name on top, in blue
