Archive date 2026-09-1613 archived replies
Toenail Fungus Treatment

I’m curious how many of you diagnosed yourself? I noticed over 7 years ago that one of my big toenails was thick then it ended up spreading to other toenails (3 on each foot) so I researched it and learned that it was toenail fungus. I have treated toenail fungus ever since (I have used everything from fungi nail, listerine, apple cider vinegar, Vicks vapor rub, Lunavia, Lakeim & the only oral medication I have taken to get rid of toenail fungus is Terbinafine) I have filed, soaked and done everything I could think of to get rid of it, I finally can see pink in my toenails and my toenails are much better but the bottom of my toenail still isn’t pink so when I went to the doctor at the foot and ankle institute (because my foot was hurting, after X-rays I found out it was broken, while I was there I asked him how I could completely get rid of my toenail fungus he said I don’t look like I have toenail fungus, then when I was at my PCP to get my liver tested because I had been on Terbinafine over 3 months she said my toenails look fine then she said that I don’t look like I have toenail fungus. I took this picture before I stopped putting anything on my toenails June 7th, then I stopped the Terbinafine July 15th and now my toenails aren’t as pink.
