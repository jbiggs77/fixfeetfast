Community comment#3

You can only find the answer to that by visiting a podiatrist that is experienced and successful in the treatment of bunions with orthotics. Orthotics cannot reduce your deformity but they may mitigate your pain by reducing the forces that create the bunions. The orthotics have to be created in a manner as to cause your body to correct the way you walk, the way the forces travel through your foot and especially the function of your 1st MTPJ. I have had the most success using Sole Support Orthotics made by an orthotics lab in Tennesee. They keep a registry of podiatrists that use their product and would be a great resource for a Nurse to know about.
