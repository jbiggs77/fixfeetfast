7 weeks post-op. Today I got my first pedicure! There is still bruising around the incision and my foot is a…

Archive date 2026-09-1217 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryScarf & Akin Osteotomy

7 weeks post-op. Today I got my first pedicure! There is still bruising around the incision and my foot is a bit swollen. My incision site is sensitive, but I have been padding it so that it does not bother me when wearing sneakers. I also walked the dogs a mile today. They pull, chasing bunnies, squirrels, other dogs, etc. I had been scared to walk them more than around the block for fear of falling if they suddenly pulled. I feel totally steady now. I could have probably gone further, but will just go a bit further every day. Aiming for 2 miles by end of week. Still going to PT 3x a week, working towards lunges and planks. Downward dog is no problem now. Lunges and planks require weight bearing on the toes while they are bent. So taking a bit longer to get there. But I see progress every week. I am overall very happy with my outcome so far.
