Hey everyone -tomorrow will be 7days since I had my surgery on both feet.

Archive date 2026-10-0815 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Walking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryScarf & Akin OsteotomyFlat Feet & Arch Support

Hey everyone -tomorrow will be 7days since I had my surgery on both feet. I have only been getting out of bed to go to bathroom which is only a metre away several times per day. When I get up I can hear crunching -its prob me being paranoid I am doing damage by walking. I am trying to follow surgeon's instructions on complete bedrest. I won't be seeing him for post op visit for another 9 days. At what point post surgery did you guys walk further even if its venturing into the rest of your house. I have a home office at other end of our property and I would really like to go there but I am more worried that I would do damage. Any thoughts?

Community comment#3

Had mid-foot fusion and MIS bunion surgery 3 weeks ago. I was permitted to go short trips wearing my post-op shoe that prevents me using my forefoot. I use a knee scooter to get around too.

Community comment#4

Akin chevron Ist metarsal min invasive yesterday morning by nerve block for moderate to large bunion, small pin in metarsal. 30 minutes in surgery. Walking with post op shoe immediately easier once block wore off after about 8 hrs, pain under control so far with paracetamol and ketoprofene, so far no swelling.Lots of throbbing going on but not really painful. Keeping foot raised in between pottering about. So far so good, fingers crossed and all the best to you xx

I actually walked on my own two feet right after surgery, in those post-op surgical shoes. That evening, I walked downstairs for dinner, for example. My surgery was mid February and I'm currently still doing PT. My left foot is better than the right, but the bunion on that foot was also bigger. This week I'll have my final visit with my surgeon, hopefully the x-ray show everything is healing as it should. But it will give me the opportunity to ask if the pain in the middle foot on my right one is 'normal'. As of right now, I tolerate only sneakers, even one inch heel feels very uncomfortable still, and they tend to swell if I do too much. Birkenstocks indoors are my life saver, the arch support is crucial.
