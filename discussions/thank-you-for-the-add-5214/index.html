Thank you for the add! I’m looking to find out if anybody has had the same symptoms and or pain issues that I am having with my foot. I have been to the orthopedic doctor and the podiatrist, both, and have not gotten the answers that I think are acceptable. The pain initially started in the top of my foot midway through between the toe and ankle there was swelling to the point where I could not fit into my shoe. The pain then moved to my big toe￼. Felt like there is some type of hairline fracture sprain of some sort. When I lift my big toe back I get tingling that goes from the toe all the way to the top of my foot. I experience intermittent excruciating pain in my big toe throughout the day. Not on the side of butt in the middle of at the joint. Also experience awful pain in the center top of my foot throughout the day. Doctor said it’s my bunion. My bunion is visible but not very large hasn’t changed in size in the last 30 years. I have no pain on the outside of my foot where the bunion with rub my shoe. ￼I have not changed the shoes that I’ve been wearing for years if not, I have made better choices in my shoes that allow more room for my foot. The pain between the top center of my foot and my big toe goes back-and-forth throughout the day. I can be sitting in the chair doing absolutely nothing and shooting pain just goes through my foot randomly. Thoughts? ￼￼ I might add that no breaks arthritis or anything of that nature were found. But they also did not x-ray the bottom of my foot which I think was important￼
