Toenail Fungus Treatment

Fellow sufferers! I have a plan. The problem is getting the medication to the fungus. So - I’ve bought an inexpensive nail drill and filed my nails down as much as I dare (although I might get braver). I crushed up 2 terbinafine tablets & put them into an old over-the-counter nail treatment bottle that I had in the house, with water. My nails look better already. Wish me luck! X
