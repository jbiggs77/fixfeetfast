How is this guest able to leave a review?

Archive date 2026-07-276 archived replies
Flat Feet & Arch Support

How is this guest able to leave a review? I had a guest claim the lockbox wasn’t working when he arrived around 8:15 p.m. I send every guest backup check-in instructions in case they have trouble with the lockbox. Instead of trying the backup instructions or ringing the doorbell to the apartment next door (where I live so I can hand guests a key personally), he immediately contacted Airbnb and demanded a full refund because I didn’t respond within five minutes. Rather than spend the evening dealing with an angry guest, I told Airbnb Support to issue a full refund and move on. The guest never checked in or entered the property, yet I now see that he left me a review. How is that possible if he never stayed at the property? Is there a way to have this review removed? Community comment#1

Hey, hi! I know it sounds unfair, and you’re probably not going to like my answer. But as far as I know, once guests attempt to check in, Airbnb considers that a check-in, even if they couldn’t actually get in. Even though guests may not read the instructions, what matters to Airbnb is that they tried and were unable to access the property. I really hope I’m wrong. You can definitely try requesting a review removal. Read the review removal policy very carefully and see if you can match any part of it to your situation. Best of luck!
