At this point, updated imaging helps us clearly assess how much healing has occurred and whether the fracture line remains active. Extended non-weight-bearing immobilization, sometimes for a longer period than the initial course, may still allow the bone to heal fully without surgery in some cases. Addressing contributing factors like bone density, training load, and biomechanics also supports better healing going forward. Many athletes and active patients throughout Lancaster County and York County work through this more extended conservative phase with good results when given adequate time and strict adherence.
