Keeping warm while elevating foot after winter foot surgery

Archive date 2026-09-138 archived replies
More foot health experiences

Bit of a silly question but if your surgery was during winter how did you elevate your foot during the night with the duvet on & still keep warm ?

Archived from Bunion Surgery / Foot Surgery Support. View the original discussion on Facebook (sign-in may be required).

Community replies

8 in this archive

The visible discussion was fully captured at the time of collection.

Community comment#1

No duvet, just use fluffy, airy fleece blankets for that leg.

Community comment#2

I used a heated blanket on my body with my foot elevated and sticking out at first. It was heavily bandaged so it didn’t get super cold

Community comment#3

My husband made a frame out of metal like a cage and the blankets went over the top

Community comment#4

Get a wedge (sold on Amazon) to put yr foot on and then the duvet loosely over that. It’s trial and error.

Community comment#5

I used a squishmallow to put my foot on and then put the covers over last. Or even a squishmallow on top of another cushion, as long as the squishmallow is the top layer, your foot can sink into it and it gives you a bit of squish either side too xx

Community comment#6

Very carefully 🤣

Community comment#7

I wore overly large grippy socks - two on that foot. I was just really slow snd gentle putting them on.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#8

Im having a boot on for 8 weeks as have Over active bladder so up a few times at night & Dr said a boot would be safer incase I bump my toe x
