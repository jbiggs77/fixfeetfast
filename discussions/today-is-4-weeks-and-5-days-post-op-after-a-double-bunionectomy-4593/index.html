Double bunionectomy aftercare: cleaning, oils, and healing tips

Archive date 2026-09-1312 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryBest Shoes After Foot Surgery

Today is 4 weeks and 5 days post op after a double bunionectomy. Bandages and compulsory shoes have now been removed. Can someone please assist with after care for my feet please. Should I soak them and if so in what solution and what ointments/lotions are best to assist with the healing. I’m thinking a warm salty water solution and possibly a vitamin E cream but happy to hear any further suggestions. Please don’t judge the toenails . They are well overdue for a pedicure. Once I get a bit more movement that’s next on my list. Thanks so much .
