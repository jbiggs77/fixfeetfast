I had my surgery 1 week ago (August 17th) and I can honestly say it has been good so far.

Archive date 2026-09-1214 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Pain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

I had my surgery 1 week ago (August 17th) and I can honestly say it has been good so far. The only time felt pain was when I slept through my alarm for my meds on day 3. I felt it for a couple of hours. The numbness is going away and I can feel little things here and there. I am a bit swollen but the doctor says no cause for concern as I am only 7 days out. I get the stitches out next week and we will begin walking and PT. She had me try to stand on it, it felt weird so I was a bit nervous but able to do so with no pain. The itching is starting but tolerable so far. I made it ... i thought this week would.be a lot harder than it was ***Excuse the big toenail, I had trauma to that nail from an ingrown so they use that to protect it and help it grow back. I can't wait until I can get a pedicure!!!
