Community comment#1

I had a lapidus on my left foot in February 2025 and one on my right foot in January 2026. After surgery the big toe is stiff. Each time I started PT at 3 weeks post-op. Last year I did 6 months of PT and have full range of motion now and no pain. I was cleared at 12 weeks to do any activity I felt I could do. I went to Europe last summer at 5 months post-op and walked around in the heat with no issues. I generally walk over 5 miles a day, ride my Peloton, play tennis, and do pilates and yoga, and my left foot gives me no issues. My right foot is coming along nicely. I have no complaints whatsoever. I think the most important things are to find a competent surgeon you are comfortable with and then to be patient with the recovery. Bunions do not heal themselves. I had minimal pain until I developed hip and back pain due to my bunion causing a misalignment.
