My recovery is the same as with metal since my bunion was shaved, big toe dilated, sawed at 2 points, relocated and screwed with 3 screws. After 3 weeks my big toe is supported with rolled gauze to stay straight. Your big toe looks to lean so it would need surgical support to keep it straight in the future.
