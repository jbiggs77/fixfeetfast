New to the group. Joined to share a dime store remedy I found for sleeping at night with a Tailors bunion. I couldn't sleep on my right side because of the bunion pushing into the mattress. Perhaps others have discovered this and it is old news but for me...it's new and awesome! Partially inflated "water wing". Put it on just below the bulge of the bunion. It stays in place for the most part and keeps the upper part of my foot elevated off of the mattress.
