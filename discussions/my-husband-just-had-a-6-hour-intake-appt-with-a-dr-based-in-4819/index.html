Toenail Fungus Treatment

My husband just had a 6 hour intake appt with a Dr based in Chicago and they talked about his toenail fungus. We have both opted to forego the pharmaceutical prescription due to worries of liver damage. The Dr said most people are taking it improperly based on the fact the Dr is just prescribing it and telling them to take it until the problem is resolved. This Dr said he’s read extensive research that shows you take this for 3 weeks and then take a week off and you will not get liver damage. He said this fungus is so weak it doesn’t create a response by our body that’s why it lingers so long, our body isn’t amped to fight it off. I found it fascinating and maybe useful for anyone looking at maybe taking the pharmaceutical route.
