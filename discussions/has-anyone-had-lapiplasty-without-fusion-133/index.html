Has anyone had Lapiplasty without fusion?

Archive date 2026-10-113 archived replies
Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryHammer Toe Surgery & Correction

Has anyone had Lapiplasty without fusion? My doctor offered this option because I’m worried about toe mobility after surgery and the long recovery time. He said it’s possible, but it would require a larger incision and more stitches since he needs better visibility of the area during the procedure.

Community comment#3

Lapiplasty/lapidus itself is a fusion at mid-foot, of the cuneiform and first metatarsal bones. I assume you're asking about big toe fusion, which is fusion of the metatarsophalangeal (MTP) joint. Many doctors caution against getting 2 fusions in one foot, e.g. lapidus and big toe fusion, because that can really lock up the foot and affect gait. (Either one can alone but many people adapt.) It is definitely not standard procedure to get a Lapiplasty/lapidus and a big toe fusion together. You might speak to your doctor again, because you might have misunderstood what he was saying.
