Foot paralysis and numbness after knee replacement surgery

Archive date 2026-09-164 archived replies
Physical Therapy & Foot Exercises

Having joined this group, I'm very surprised to see that no comments are allowed or seen. This is rather odd. My personal foot issue, and if anyone else has had this occur please respond if able, occurred during my left knee replacement. My surgeon made incision only 8 cm in length, then crammed the Stryker Triathlon replacement through that small opening, while retracting my skin, but "no bueno." Next morning, while I was doing a self-assessment, realized my left toes and foot were paralyzed. Had just a small oval of skin on top of my foot that had feeling. The remainder of my foot was numb, with very limited ankle ROM. Saw a Foot & Ankle Specialist, after I'd had an EMG. Bottom Line: Cramming the device in took out my Tibial and Sural Nerves, as well as the insertion of the left ITB into the Tibia. Foot doc said, in 2015, that the muscles in my foot would slowly atrophy and eventually the bones in my left foot would fall. Well, my foot bones fell about 6 months ago. Sought an MD opinion. There's absolutely nothing that can be done. Curious as to whether this has happened to anyone else out there. Thanks so much.
