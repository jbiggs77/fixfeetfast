I had bunion surgery and then ankle surgery a year later because my ankle somehow got messed up from the bunion surgery. At that point, my big toe had angled way in again so I had bunion revision surgery a year ago. My big toe is staying pretty well aligned now, but all my toes on that foot get stiff, crampy, and uncomfortable and still swell. Also, a lump formed near the ball of my foot after the first surgery. It didn’t get resolved with the revision surgery and it is getting bigger and moving. Today it hurt quite a bit to walk on so I’m trying some gel pads. Basically it’s just been one problem after another and distresses me every single day.
