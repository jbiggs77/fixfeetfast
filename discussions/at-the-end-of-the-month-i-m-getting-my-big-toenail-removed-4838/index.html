At the end of the month I'm getting my big toenail removed.

Archive date 2026-09-255 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot Surgery

At the end of the month I'm getting my big toenail removed. I've been wanting to do this for a very long time as I've dealt with so many ingrown toenails and I know it affects the way I walk. They are going to do both feet eventually but they said they can either do 1 big toe or 2 little ones at 1 time. I'm hoping this will give me some relief. The bones in the tops of my feet always feel like they are broke but doctor says x-rays look fine. I've always worked at fast paced factories and have had to wear steel toed boots/shoes. Plantars in both heels. I quit drinking alcohol over a year ago and so of course all my aches and pains are coming forth as I had used beer mainly for my medicine. I'm 50 years old and move like a turtle now. Very painful and quite embarrassing to say the least. Wish me luck and thanks for listening.
