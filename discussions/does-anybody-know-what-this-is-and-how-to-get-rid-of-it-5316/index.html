I had one of those a few times on my finger. Your tissue came through to the outside of the nail. I don't know what it's called. It will go away by itself. Community comment#4

Community comment#5

I used to have ingrown toenails on this side of the big toe and I had them cut out decades back and they froze them or put some kind of chemical down into that nail bed where they sliced it out to make sure it didn’t grow back. And it worked on one but I had to go back and get it done a second time on the other nail. So it kind a looks to me like some thing right along that nail bed area where they slice out the ingrown part of the nail that shaped kind of like a C where it was digging back the soft tissues. This sort of almost looks like some of the soft tissue that’s kind of bubbled up or kind of like a growth up through that little slice. I guess if it’s not infected or seeping yellowy type drainage, it might just be part of the healing process

Community comment#6

I used to get ingrown toenails on my big toes but then realized it was from cutting my nails too short. Once I stopped doing that I never had a problem again with ingrown toenails. Idk if that works for everyone but it worked for me.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#11

if she gets those ingrown toenails like that fairly often she should go and have the procedure done where they cut that whole piece out that curves down around into the soft tissues. I actually got it done while I was in graduate school and it was through the health center. It was performed by doctors that were just right out of medical school and doing a residency and it wasn’t anything difficult. I mean it was kind of like they wrap a rubber band around the big toe to kind of cut off some blood supply and then they numb your toe area and they take a scalpel and they just cut a slice right up that side that gets in grown. And then they put some kind of chemical down there to keep that nail from growing back. And after it heals up you don’t even know you ever had it done. It was so worth it!! Never had an ingrown toenail since.
