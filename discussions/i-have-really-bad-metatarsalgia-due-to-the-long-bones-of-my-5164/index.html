Community comment#6

Keen boots have a wide toebox and have enough structural support for my feet. I replace their insoles with something like Powerstep Pinnacle with Met Support and then I also add a Pedag T-form metatarsal Support underneath the insole. I was introduced to all these products by a pedorthist that owned a shoe store. I went in saying I needed custom orthotics and heard he could make them and he said try this stuff. Been using various combinations for at least 15 years now and it’s saved my life. I started with the Keen shoes and have tried the insoles plus extra supports in other shoes. It’s also worked in some Kurus and Altras. The key seems to be that the shoe needs to have enough structure in the sole. It’s a lot less expensive than custom orthotics for every pair of shoes you own and you can self adjust the location of the extra support as you need to. Powerstep Pedag
