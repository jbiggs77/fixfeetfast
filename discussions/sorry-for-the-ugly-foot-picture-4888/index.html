Toe Spacers, Orthotics & Braces

Sorry for the ugly foot picture! I obviously have a bunion and crossover toes (surgery this winter) but the WORST problem is the pressure from this on my pinky toe. There is a "crease" at the bottom of my pinky toe from whre it is smashed and this actually causes limping. I have tried toe wraps and an insert that bound my two toes (fourth toe and pinky toe) together but was too painful in my sneaker. I am traveling to Portugal for a vacation in October and am looking for a temporary solution. I would have surgery sooner but I am only paid when I work and cannot take off now. I am thinking perhaps an orthotic that can lift my pinky toe from under the others but do not know if that exists. Any/all ideas are appreciated! Thank you in advance.

Community comment#8

I wouldn’t buy any toe spacers from Amazon but I’d suggest correct toes from their website! Only wear about an hour a day and increase the time and NOT in shoes to start until you’re comfortable wearing them out of shoes. Whether or not you use them you need a wide toe box shoe. Depending on other variables from the correct toes website they have a lot of suggestions for shoes, sadly they’re all zero drop like lems, xero, Altra etc I have Achilles issues so can’t wear those, if you don’t they’re all great. ON Running has great wide toe box shoes that aren’t zero drop and can wear with correct toes once you’re ready.
