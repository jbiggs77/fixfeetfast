Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#8

Hi Julie, the shoes you link to are wide width—-Wide width is wider in the midfoot, it still tapers in the toe box area. We deform our feet by wearing pointy toed footwear. We are born with wedge shaped feet with the widest part being the toe area, it’s baffling as to why there aren’t more anatomical shaped footwear styles in the mainstream stores. There are some great wide toe box shoes like Shapen and Origo and I see another one online called Zeazoo. The website has some recommendations that are worthwhile to look up. When toes can spread, our toes support us like roots support a tree
