Looking to see if this pain has happened to anyone else so I can maybe get answers.

Archive date 2026-10-067 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

Looking to see if this pain has happened to anyone else so I can maybe get answers. I sprained my ankle in July 2022 and have been in pain since. Nothing showed up on X-ray so I wore a boot and did PT. PT only made the pain worse and even went up to my knee area so my podiatrist ordered an MRI. Nothing showed up so he sent me to pain management where I have gotten 2 nerve blocks. The first block (Jan ‘23) lasted 12 weeks before the pain came back. The second block (May ‘23) worked for maybe 4 weeks. Just had another MRI 2 weeks ago and again, nothing is showing up so pain mgmt doctor is sending me to an ortho doctor. I am in constant pain, can’t exercise, and just ready for someone to cut my foot/ankle open and see what’s going on . Including pictures to show where the pain is.
