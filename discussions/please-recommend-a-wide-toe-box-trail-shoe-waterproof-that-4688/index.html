Community comment#3

My background is this- we have a small farm on the side of a mountain and I'm outside most days, rain or shine and average 2-5 miles each day, up and down hills, thru mud/grass/rough rocky steep paths and flat pasture. Your options really depend on what size you wear and what your width is. If you can size up into a man's shoe it gives you more options.... Men's shoes are wider from the get-go. We try to stay with a natural foot-shaped shoe, I cannot wear a truly barefoot shoe. I wear a women's 6.5 EE – so I cannot find a men's shoe small enough. And, most of my width is across the forefoot...but not ALL of it....so bean-shaped shoes don't work for me. Just because a shoe is described as wide doesn't mean it's foot shaped. I can get a new balance in a double e but it's still squeezes my feet because it's not foot shaped. And for me there's NOTHING "waterproof." I can choose between fit or moisture. I have a pair of Meindl boots that are the SHIZNIC when it comes to water proof and durability- but they are not at all comfortable/ wide enough. I remove the insert and even pried up the footbed... I wear them on really rainy but hot days (because I got nothing else) ...after an hour I am limping. Daily wear- Altra Lone Peak mids (most dry days.) They do have a water proof version, but I have found it doesn't hold up as long. To be honest, Altras don't hold up that long at all. If it's a "wet but not splashy/deep water" summer day, I wear Altra Alpine- they are really water resistant (but not a truly a trail shoe IMO.) For truly wet days I just wear ill-fiting muck boots. I do plan on trying the lun'r boots this year...but that's not what you are asking.
