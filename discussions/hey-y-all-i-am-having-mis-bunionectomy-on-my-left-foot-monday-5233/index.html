Hey y'all! I am having MIS Bunionectomy on my left foot Monday. I am going to be home alone for a bit (I have a couple friends nearby if I need some assistance). Do you have any tips/tricks for healing process or what to expect? -I already purchased a knee-scooter/walker -I have my floors cleaned up (able to scoot around) -I bought a leg support/elevation pillow -I have plenty of school work that I can do sitting on my couch -I am going to pick up some easy meals (any suggetions?) Thank you in advance! Super stoked to finally be getting my bunions taken care of (been 10 years of wearing toe spacers and even longer for foot pain!).

Community comment#1

If possible, move your bed out into your front room so you're near the kitchen and door for visitors. Remove all ur area rugs and anything else that may trip you up.

Community comment#2

If you don’t have a laptop computer, find a stool about the hat you need and put a pillow on the stool. Worked for me. It wasn’t above my heart, but I’m 4 weeks in and all is ok. Wasn’t fortunate enough to have MI. I was pretty down and out the 1st 2 weeks. Good luck!!🍀

Community comment#3

Don’t for forget ice packs or ask for the ice machine. I personal train online. And and rest my foot on the pillow.

Community comment#4

Get a shower mat so you don’t slide and I bought this from Amazon . It stayed completely dry. Good luck

Community comment#5

Hey everyone, surgery went well today. I wasn’t given a boot. I am having a lot of pain and I just bumped my toe (not hard but enough to cause even more intense pain). Did anyone else bump their toes right after surgery?

Community comment#6

Elevation pillow was invaluable. Get plastic bags and waterproof tape for showering. Shower chair too was great. Didn’t need a scooter. Thought I would do heaps of computer work but couldn’t figure out how to balance computer with foot elevated above heart. So watched lots of Netflix for 2 weeks.
