Just left my week 7 appointment!

Archive date 2026-09-1729 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Post-Surgery Swelling & InflammationBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

Just left my week 7 appointment! I can finally start to walk flat footed with a little weight (will continue using walker) and begin limited PT. My swelling and pain is virtually non existent, I haven't iced or elevated for days. He said I could move to sneakers if I want and the nurse suggested Birkenstocks. My fear is that with a sneaker I'll feel an urge to walk normally which I cannot do. I have no pain so I'm ready to actually walk! Also, I think my definition of walking is different than everyone's post surgery I went in today think I would move to sneakers and start walking NORMALLY right away. Walking like a baby short steps and flat footed is not what I consider walking lol. So here's to the next month with these directions, hopefully I can REALLY walk by week 11!
