Community comment#3

So important to rest with your foot elevated for most of the day particularly during the first 2 weeks. Take pain meds as prescribed especially at night so you can be as comfy as possible allowing you to sleep. I found raising my foot helped ease the pain too. Recovery days are long so try to make sure you have things to keep you occupied….i have never watched so much Netflix in my life! Are you able to weight bare at all after your op? Xx
