Community comment#1

My oldest daughter had a double bunionectomy in 2018, about a year before she began nursing school. Her orthopedic surgeon had said that he had never seen a kid with bunions like that. The pain was brutal after the surgery, but she has had zero problems since she recovered and she is now a nurse practitioner and is on her feet all the time. She wears Hokas to work and is careful about what shoes she wears in her downtime. She doesn’t have any problems with her feet now.
