Right foot 6 weeks left foot 3 weeks when off pain meds

I’m at 10 weeks and can’t drive until 12 weeks because I still have the boot on. I was supposed to take it off at 9 weeks but my bones lost density and he’s having me wear the boot another 3 weeks.

Community comment#5

Hope to get the OK tomorrow at 8 weeks. My bone wasn't healed as much as she wanted at my last appt 12/1. I did drive to the grocery store today but it's just outside my gated community so I felt safe.

Community comment#6

When they get you out of the boot, that's when they clear you for driving. I my case, 8 weeks

Community comment#7

My doc gave me the ok at 6 weeks, but I didn't feel comfortable doing it until 8 weeks. She told me I could wear my surgical shoe, but not the boot. I had Lapidus, Akin Osteotomy and a Tailor's bunionectomy.

Community comment#8

You have to get out of the boot first. Can’t drive with it on. My doc said not til I feel comfortable making a full stop safely. I had surgery in August of this year, went into shoes and out of the boot 12/1. I’m comfortable, if I were to drive.

Community comment#9

I had the same thing as you but on my left foot and I don’t drive but I think at like the eight week mark when you are not weight-bearing, you start walking and then you’ll be put in a shoe and then you’ll be able to start driving so it should be about about 8 to 9 weeks
