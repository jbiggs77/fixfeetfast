Ty Pie I think I can kinda relate to what Ariel was going through, because since my MIS bunion surgery, about 8 weeks ago, I have yearned to be a part of the human world again so I could walk, run, and play all day in the sun. I can't quite run, but I can comfortably walk and play in the sun if it isn't over 24°C (75°F). The zingers are like mosquito bites; I have an occasional random visit, but they are far less frequent than before, and the swelling at the base of my toe is get Patty Baker 1 week post op! Still taking Tylenol Sarah HB Edited to remove me saying I had x-rays as I've not had any since before my op Hi all. I had MIS, no hardware, NHS UK back in July.
