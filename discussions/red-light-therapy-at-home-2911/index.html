Community comment#5

Joel Mattson I'd be careful when the owner doesn't have confidence. That might mean he's leaning on the books. To verify his numbers you can look at deposits over the last 12 months and watch the business. But to answer a LOT of people's questions in the comments there are ways to verify a legit deal. When I bought a gas station I asked the owner if I could see the daily sales and watch the store. He agreed so I watched it for 2 weeks and regularly came in and had the cashier print out the sales for the day. I was able to determine the numbers were legit, and verified the bank deposits, and bought the store on an owner finance deal. He gave me 15yrs to pay it off, with a good down payment, and I was able to increase business over 3 times what he was doing and paid him off after 4 years. So there are ways to verify a legit deal but you have to put in the time.
