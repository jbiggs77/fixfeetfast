Good morning hopalongers & bootyboot clompers. So looks like I'm in for pre-assessmet op next Wednesday Part 2 !. Just had the call. So I should imagine (with past experience) it will be approx. 2 weeks, unless a cancellation comes through. So back on the ol chop table it will be. So place ya bets now to see what I end up with. 1. Tootsie with his crappy friends metalwork are given the boot (scuse the pun) and his mate Bone is happy, solo life, peachy & fab. Sew me up doc. I'm outta here. 2. Tootsie and Bone mate are just not coming together need a little incentive. So a new friend joins, Ankle Bone. A little bit of 'fusion' for harmony. 3. Tootsie, Bone and Ankle Bone are just not hitting it off, so another joins the merry family... #2 Plate and his screwy cousins !!!!!. So, I know which bet I wanna go for ! Anyway folks we shall see. I'm just enjoying my limpy freedom time while I have it. Oh I get Pammy the scooter out again. I tried to sell my scooters but no takers. Look at that ! There's a reason they hibernated in their boxes for the last few months. I would say let the fun begin whizzing round the house, but everyone knows what happened there !!!!! I have a helmet now and full bike leathers. Right I'm outta here. Be well. Be happy. Be the best person you can be, never underestimate you. Adios... V x
