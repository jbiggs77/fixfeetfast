New to the group and wish I had found it sooner. Long post, but sharing my experience so far with PF + tarsal tunnel surgery for anyone considering it. I didn’t realize how extensive recovery would be and still haven’t decided if it was the best or worst decision yet. Recovery has been a PIA for an active person, and I went on 3 trips during recovery, so I probably wasn’t the best patient. I had 6 years of PF and tried shoes, insoles, injections, PT, massage, and stretching.
