Community comment#3

I had it over 3 weeks ago. Lots of pain first week. Non weight bearing for two full weeks. I magically thought at two weeks I’d be walking in a boot. I couldn’t even stand at two weeks. Slowly started putting a tiny bit of pressure on it and I’m at 3.5 weeks and I can sort of walk in the boot. I had calf recession too. Right now I can walk with the help of one crutch. But it’s really been way slower than I anticipated!!! Just take it easy. My doc says everything is fine, give it time. I don’t have any pain just weird nerve sensations or zaps at night.

the recovery is very slow than most doctor say. Had the surgery 3 times. It took me 6 months to be pain free. Don't listen to the doctor about weight bearing listen to your body. If it hurts stop.
