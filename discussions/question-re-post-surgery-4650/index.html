Community comment#1

I was told not to get new orthodics until I was a year out due to your foot changing and healing for that long. I saw an ortho doc for my first pair. They did a "mold" of each foot and they were made at another facility. We have a place called The Good Feet Store where they will do something similar and you can get them the same day. I would call your podiatrist and ask if s/he does them or where you should go to get them. Best to you in your journey.

Community comment#2

You need an orthotist. Arrange now as it can take several months to obtain an appointment!! Your surgeon should be able to recommend someone.
