Community comment#1

I just had a hammer toe correction 2 weeks ago. I walked out of the hospital in a surgical shoe, didn't have a nerve block and basically had minimal pain. I took 600 mg. of Tylenol before bed. Nothing else. Also, I had no pins. Next week, I'm having bunion and hammer toe on my big toe and the one next to it. Rather nervous about that one.

Community comment#2

Hi. I didn’t have that surgery but all toes were broken to re-align and I had 5 pins. I was so nervous but I didn’t look and it was so fast that I had no time to feel anything. Try not to stress. Hopefully someone can accompany you. I am very squeamish. You got this!
