It’s so crazy I’m in way less pain being barefoot than wearing shoes.

Archive date 2026-10-1517 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryHammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryFlat Feet & Arch Support

It’s so crazy I’m in way less pain being barefoot than wearing shoes. I had bunion surgery (revision) and plantar fasciitis surgery March 28th. I despise wearing shoes tbh. Still haven’t found anything that works for me. I’m currently wearing acics. I hate hokas and brooks so don’t even recommend them. They save the more support you have in your shoe the weaker your foot gets.

Please look in to Altra Paradigm 7 or 8. I got plantar fasciitis in my non op foot post op and it was because Brooks were so restrictive and wouldn’t allow my feet to bend as they naturally should. I researched the heck out of what to do for PF and the Altra paradigm 7 was one that was highly recommended as a transitional shoe. My PF went away over a month or so time after wearing them daily. I then told a doctor that I know who struggled with PF about them. Saw her wearing them one day months later and he said it cured her PF. I also wear topo, Splay and other barefoot shoes now
