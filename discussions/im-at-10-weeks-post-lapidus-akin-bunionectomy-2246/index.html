Im at 10 weeks post lapidus akin bunionectomy. Anyone else at this time post op can te me how everything is going for you. I have a couple things that are bothering me and im not sure if it is norm and need reassurance. The upper part of foot still hurts. Across all toe joints when applying pressure while walking.
