goldpeach25 I had bilateral scarf akin maestro with distal soft tissue release in the UK three months ago, and could weight bear (in the post op surgical sandals) from the day of surgery. Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#11

goldpeach25 I’m doing well thank you. Have started a new job and am commuting into London a couple of days a week. I was late starting physiotherapy owing to a mess up with the insurer but now have a comprehensive set of exercises (which take a while owing to having to be sure to do both feet!). I’m starting back at gym classes - I still know I’ve had surgery but every day gets better. I initially used occasional ibuprofen and paracetamol for pain relief but luckily didn’t need any past about the three week mark. If you can, you might be able to see some of my previous posts from along the way. I’m super glad I had both done at once 😊
