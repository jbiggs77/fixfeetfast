Terbafine tablets for toenail fungus

Archive date 2026-07-083 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryToenail Fungus Treatment

Been suffering since 2016, was scared to try medication so went through everything else instead. All 10 toes affected and nasty looking. Haven’t worn open shoes and flip flops for 10 years now. I started Terbafine (3 month prescription ) end of May. Look at this result…the pink is coming through and I am no longer disgusted by my own toes!!! Still a long way to go. Regarding side effects: none whatsoever. Minor one: vivid dreams but I wouldnt call it a major side effect I wish I had done this sooner I wish all my socks 60 degrees celcius, use shoe spray/powder and will keep doing so for the rest of my life See less
