Anyone else feeling panicky hot & sweaty in their layers of dressings/bandages.

Archive date 2026-10-086 archived replies
More foot health experiences

Anyone else feeling panicky hot & sweaty in their layers of dressings/bandages. Feels really hot in there and want it all off!!!. Just one week post op for me and then this heatwave!! Trying to lie still in shaded room and relax/breathe. One more hot day to go. Bonus is that I don't feel I'm missing out being active outdoors as too hot for me! A week has actually gone by reasonably quickly. Got a really sore heel under those bandages which isn't helping as I can't look at it - but just one more week until visit to surgeon and dressing off
