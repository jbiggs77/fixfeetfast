To the people that bike on Loudon rd and Raccoon Valley st.

Archive date 2026-06-032 archived replies
More foot health experiences

To the people that bike on Loudon rd and Raccoon Valley st. WHY?? It is so dangerous! There are so many wonderful bike trails in the area. Granville to Johnstown. New Albany has many great ones you can go all over. Buckeye lake you can bike around the water. Invest in your life and buy a bike rack for your car. If you are biking for exercise it won’t matter if you are splattered all over the road!!! STOP RIDING ON LOUDON AND RACCOON VALLEY!!!! See less

Community comment#1

Pelotonia training is big in Granville. Especially on the roads with the most hills and climbing. They are riding to End Cancer. Please be patient and share the road. Cars lawfully need to slow down and give 3 feet of clearance.

Community comment#2

Sobjack The “bike path” is a multi-use trail and it has a speed limit. It is not safe if you are riding a road bike at an adult speed. I ride with my kid on the bike path, and I am grateful for the road bikers who use the road so that the path is safe for children! I also ride myself on the road. Loudon and Raccoon Valley is a popular route. Groups ride from New Albany and Columbus to come out here because it is beautiful, good hills, and the drivers here are usually kind about sharing the road. I appreciate the drivers who look out and help keep us safe!
