Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#11

Mindy Fried In my personal experience, these have the widest toe box I've managed to find. I haven't contacted customer service myself, but others mention they are responsive via the email on their US website. Because I have ingrown toenails on all 10 toes, I specifically prefer maximum width. I had to size up by one compared to regular shoes, which many others also recommend too. I chose a model with soft leather (pebbles teracare leather) that didn't need breaking in and molded to my feet. (Note: They offer styles that perhaps look similar in photographs, but keep in mind that they can use different types of leather ). I use a shoe cover when I try them at home first since the sole gets dirty easily (in case I want to return). They have a community on Facebook with lots of info—I guess it is better to join them first to find pros and cons for your potential choice. I love mine 😜
