I’m 5 weeks post op had a cast for 4 weeks and now in a boot for 2 weeks I find first thing in the morning is…

Archive date 2026-10-086 archived replies
Walking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

I’m 5 weeks post op had a cast for 4 weeks and now in a boot for 2 weeks I find first thing in the morning is the hardest, after laying down (tossing all night) then to put the boot on to walk is like starting all over again. Yesterday I realized I was walking weird And had to focus one foot at a time! 1 1/2 weeks to go before trying to get into a regular foot wear. Then 2 weeks to drive! It’s my right foot so that will be weird. I’ll need to focus to be “Normal” Anyone else feel this way?
