Community comment#3

I did.🙋‍♀️I had a non union - bone didn't grow together. First surgery was 12-30-25. Second surgery was 7-21-26. I walked around for 7 months with a broken foot and it was miserable. Second time I researched (Googled, AI, Looked at the Dr review boards, etc). I chose a Dr with good ratings/reviews. I asked LOTS of questions including asking what he would do differently from the other Dr to have success. He took out the plate, 4 screws and a couple of staples. I have two staples now and that's it! I have transitioned to a walking boot. Much less swelling and pain than last time. For me, getting a highly recommend Dr has made all the difference. Do your research before you let the same guy who didn't get it right the first time give it another try.
