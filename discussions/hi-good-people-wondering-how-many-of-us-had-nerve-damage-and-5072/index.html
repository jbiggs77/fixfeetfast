Hi good people. Wondering how many of us had nerve damage and onset CRPS? I had bunion surgery beginning Aug. The aftermath was very painful due to neuropathy. The pain now is manageable... more tingly. My problem is lack of motion in foot and toes. I have crps. Currently having physio. My toes are not flush to the floor and very rigid. Any advice would be very appreciated. I'm due total knee replacement in a couple of months on same leg. I'm a little worried. I'm in the UK by the way. X

it stands for Complex Regional Pain Syndrome. Basically it's nerve damage.. your body has an abnormal reaction to the operation resulting in pain, tingling, loss of flexibility. My toes hardly move and my foot now has limited movement and is weak.
