Community comment#1

Hey there I’m in Sydney and in my case, I found the surgeon I wanted to use then went to my GP for a referral. My surgeon doesn’t participate in the medical gap scheme with my private healthcare provider so my out of pocket is $5000 for my surgeon, assistant surgeon cost is about 20% of surgeon which is about another $1000, anaesthesiologist unsure yet and $250 excess to my private healthcare provider. Medicare reimburses about 1700. Without private health, hospital cost is >$7000 Hope that helps

Community comment#2

I went through the public system in Melbourne. I went to my doctor and she referred me to the closest hospital which was The Alfred. I didn’t get to choose a surgeon or procedure (scarf and akin). Dr Hamish Curry did my Surgery.￼ I had quite a bad bunion that was affecting my second toe and back. My first referral in 2020 was refused. My doctor did another one in 2021 that was accepted, my first appointment with the surgeon was in 2023. I’ve just had surgery five weeks ago today. Be ready to wait it’s a long process. ￼$0 cost to me with Dr. Hamish Curry￼ at the Alfred.

Anonymous member it’s only been 5 weeks so I’m still in the early stages of recovery. It’ll be a few months still and for me because I have an active job I can’t work for 3-4 months. It was excruciatingly painful day 1 & 2, was more manageable during the second week. It’s a tough surgery and I wouldn’t do it again!! I recommend getting your own physio who is experienced with surgery rehab, the one at the hospital was not good. Yes I guess you have to be eligible but it’s always worth trying.
