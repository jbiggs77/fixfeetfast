Lapiplasty success story: quick bone fusion and pain relief

Archive date 2026-06-112 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryPost-Surgery Swelling & Inflammation

**positive story so far* Had lapiplasty in January of this year on the right foot. Not gonna lie it was living hell the first few days. But my bones fused in record time (surgeon's words) and its been pretty seamless since. Still some swelling and tenderness but every day is a little better. Had MIS surgery today on my left foot. So far its been painless! He numbed the foot but no nerve block. (He doesn't like doing them. Thinks it causes nerve damage ) About yeeted myse
