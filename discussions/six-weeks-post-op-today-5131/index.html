Six weeks post-op today.

Archive date 2026-10-0824 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryScarf & Akin Osteotomy

Six weeks post-op today. Finally able to transition out of the surgical shoes that I wore 24/7, able to drive, and feeling very good. Healing seems to be going well, I’ve had no pain throughout, and have great mobility in both feet. Both bunions and both bunionettes operated on. Two more weeks of the good life, then it’s back to work and normality. Here’s to ‘normal’ feet
