Community comment#1

If you’re taking prescription meds, you may need a stool softener. They may stop you up some.

Community comment#2

Take your pain meds now if you’re not already. Set alarms to take them these first few days. Do not wait for the pain to kick in to start taking stuff. Ice behind your knee for 15 minutes at a time.

Community comment#3

Elevate foot above your heart as much as possible!

Community comment#4

Diet- no carbs, no sugar in food. Eat citrus, salads, almonds, and protein ideally not from red meat. Keep it up until you are regular. Attitude is everybong snd remember, generally these types of groups run 70:30 negative stuff. You are in charge of you and if in doubt, call your dr. Good luck!

Community comment#5

Even if your foot feels good and you think you’re doing good, just stay in bed and keep your foot up. Once the swelling starts from it being down, it is insanely painful and takes forever to go back down

Community comment#6

Thank you guys for all the tips!! I am very grateful for each and every one of you! I will continue to post updates. Since I am young (18) I pray that the healing goes by fast with a smooth and painless recovery, so I will be checking in and asking questions frequently!

Community comment#7

Toes above the nose and yes be careful if you're on pain meds. I had quite a time with that. Stool softeners didn't work for me I had to use a laxative and drink lots and lots of liquids and eat lots of fiber foods. Good luck it's quite the healing process

Community comment#8

Drink lots of water stay hydrated! After the first two weeks you will start to get annoyed sad and upset etc! It’s normal it’s hard but you got this! I’m on my 2nd foot now I just hit one week today! If you have any questions feel free to ask!! And the healing should be fast since you are so young! I’m 22!
