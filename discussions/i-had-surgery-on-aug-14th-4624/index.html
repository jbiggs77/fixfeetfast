I had surgery on Aug 14th.

Archive date 2026-09-146 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPain Management After Foot SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound HealingPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

I had surgery on Aug 14th. I had my right foot surgery in Jan. And now my left foot, I had hardware removal, MIS and apparently hammertoe surgery done. Pain level is moderate. The hammertoe pin is uncomfortable but hopefully won’t need to keep it in long according to the dr. I feel weaker this time and trying to do exercises in bed to get stronger. Has anyone else had hammertoe surgery and pin that felt uncomfortable? Not sure how long my recovery is going to be. Blessings to all with foot surgery.
