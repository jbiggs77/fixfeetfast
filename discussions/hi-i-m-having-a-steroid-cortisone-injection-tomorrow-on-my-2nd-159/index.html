Hi I’m having a steroid cortisone injection tomorrow on my 2nd TMTJ due to pain caused by arthritis.

Archive date 2026-10-165 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryHammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPain Management After Foot Surgery

Hi I’m having a steroid cortisone injection tomorrow on my 2nd TMTJ due to pain caused by arthritis. Surgeon not interested in touching the bunion yet. He said if the injection doesn’t work he would consider a two procedures in one operation - fusion of TMTJ and bunion removal at same time. Has anyone else been treated like this - I’m NHS UK patient? Seems to me like they’re addressing the symptom and not the cause.

I guess it depends how they do it. The doctor rotated and thus realigning the bone where my bunion was, which corrected the bunion. The fusion was to address the arthritis.

Community comment#2

Please Be careful with injections. It weakened my bones so much that my second toe fractured. I had injections over a 2 year period ( every 3-4 months ) for ball of foot pain caused by hammertoe. My current surgeon tells me I should have never had so many . And an orthopedic surgeon told me I should have NEVER had injected in bottom of foot. 🤷‍♀️

I think one injection would be just fine and will help with the pain 🤷‍♀️it’s the only thing that made it possible for me to walk for 2 years before deciding to get the surgery .

Community comment#5

I’m UK too . Have you gone down the GP-Orthopaedic consultant route? If so find a podiatry clinic which can refer to to a podiatric surgeon.(which still has to be done via your GP) . Mine specified the surgeon I should be referred to . If it is a podiatric surgeon I’d get a second opinion. The key words for referral for surgery at pain and impacting your quality of life. On the other hand if there is extensive arthritis in the area they might feel surgery and the recovery process isn’t an ideal option. I’d ask why the decision to medicate first
