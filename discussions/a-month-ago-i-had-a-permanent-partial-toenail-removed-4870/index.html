A month ago I had a permanent partial toenail removed.

Archive date 2026-09-267 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot Surgery

A month ago I had a permanent partial toenail removed. It was healing nicely and I was cleaning and putting neosporn on 3x daily- as they said to do. Unfortunately I stubbed my toe, it seemed to get worse so I made an appointment to get it looked at again. However, since making the appointment I have been leaving it open and cleaning it once a day and the pain has improved tremendously- almost none at all. It seems to be scabbing over much better as well. With that being said, should I give it more time to heal before going in? I’m constantly on my feet so I feel like that will play a role in some delayed healing time. Thanks for any input!!!
