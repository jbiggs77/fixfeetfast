Toenail Fungus Treatment

I wanted to share something that’s been affecting me for a while. Living with toenail fungus might seem like a small thing to other people, but for me it’s actually impacted a lot of parts of my daily life. I’ve felt embarrassed to show my feet, wear sandals, go to the beach, things like that. And honestly, one of the hardest parts is trying treatments and not seeing results as quickly as I hoped. I’m curious if anyone else here feels the same way or has gone through something similar. How has it affected you emotionally, your confidence, or your day-to-day life? If anyone feels comfortable sharing their experience or venting a little, I’d genuinely appreciate reading it…

Don’t know what happened to my comment, but here it is again. The last picture is one of my right foot where most of the fungus is gone. I file the nails down every morning after the shower with a Dremel put on oregano oil, sometimes white iodine, sometimes Lamisil sometimes clove oil, the oil sometimes irritate my skin, so I can’t do it all the time. I also put cotton balls with a 50-50 mix of apple cider vinegar and Brown mouthwash from the dollar store and tape those cotton balls to my toes and put my socks back on this is supposed to change the pH of the area to a pH that the fungus does not like and then on top of that a couple times a week I glob a bunch of Vicks vapor rub on the toe and put a Band-Aid over it and the sock back on and sleep with it. Shower and grind it again when I think it’s gone I stopped the grinding and just keep putting on the oil and Lamasil and hope it grows out clear. I think the grinding of the toenail down is the most important aspect of my treatment.

Community comment#41

Yes, I lived like that for about 8 years. It was terrible. 💔 After two appointments, one with my family doctor and one with a dermatologist they both suggested terbinafine. I was not comfortable with but after a couple of years, I decided to take it. I had bloodwork done before starting the treatment just to make sure my liver was healthy. After a month of taking it, I did bloodwork again to check on my liver. I took terbinafine for 3 months, no side effects, I Shaved my 2 affected toenails (or grind it) down to the thinnest I could, it took almost an entire year to grow a new toenail. I can finally wear sandals! 😭 Talk to your doctor and if terbinafine is a good option for you, you should give it a try.
