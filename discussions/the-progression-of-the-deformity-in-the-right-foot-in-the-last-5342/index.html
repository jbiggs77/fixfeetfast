The progression of the deformity in the right foot in the last month and a half is astounding.

Archive date 2026-10-204 archived replies
More foot health experiences

The progression of the deformity in the right foot in the last month and a half is astounding. The left foot has been far more painful as far as the bunion pushing on other bones further up the foot. However, the right foots' toes are being smashed together so severely, I have to alternate using toe separators and not to maintain any bit of comfort. I've developed neuropathy so I am scheduled for an EMG to determine the cause. I also have Sjögren's disease and I think the neuropathy is a result of that, just a guess
