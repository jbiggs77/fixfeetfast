Had bunion and my first metatarsus broken and repaired with a 2 inch pin today.

Archive date 2026-10-1920 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryPain Management After Foot SurgeryScarf & Akin OsteotomySurgery Complications & Wound Healing

Had bunion and my first metatarsus broken and repaired with a 2 inch pin today. I had my nerve block around 7 am. My foot is still numb, but it’s becoming a painful numb. It doesn’t hurt anywhere around the incision or where the break is. My last 4 toes are painfully numb and when I try to move my foot the painful numbness radiates all over. This incision is slightly beginning to itch. Is this normal? I’m sure it is, but it’s making me panic because my dad has neuropathy in both feet and this is the pain he describes.
