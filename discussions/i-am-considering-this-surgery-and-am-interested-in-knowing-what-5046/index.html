Community comment#8

...I'm 10 weeks post op MIS bunion surgery on my right foot and I see a consultant next week to start the process for my left foot. It's been a very positive experience but that could be because I let the pain of my bunions get so bad...no post op discomfort could compete. I have no regrets so far, but I would say that "weight bearing" and "walking" are not the same thing. You kinda zombie walk before you regain full range of motion...I was told 2 weeks but it was a full month before I could walk comfortably in shoes. I think it's important to remember that most people share the challenges...but I think it's at least worth making a few enquiries if you can.
