I'm looking for a unicorn sandal.

Archive date 2026-10-2312 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryFlat Feet & Arch Support

I'm looking for a unicorn sandal. I have a wide toe box and am working on strengthening my feet toward barefoot shoes. I like Birkenstocks, but want to try another brand too. I have tried Oofos- the slide bothered my big toe joint, and the flip flop bothers me from the toe post. I tried several models of Teva which I like the style and fit except for the toe box- my big toe hangs over the side since it has a slightly tapered toe box. I'm looking for: healthy, foot shaped toe box, some arch support for walking a lot on vacation; some water resistance. Maybe a "transitional" shoe. Does such a unicorn exist? Thank you!!
