1 week post-op update!

Archive date 2026-10-1213 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

1 week post-op update! Hey everyone! I’m officially one week post op of an austin bunionectomy and wanted to share an update. I ended up in the ER yesterday and was diagnosed with blood clots in my calf, so I’m now on Eliquis. Definitely scary, but I’m doing okay. My toes are still a little numb, and my doctor mentioned today that I have a lot of bruising on the bottom of my foot. After he changed the bandage, it does feel like some sensation is slowly starting to come back. I’m supposed to be weight-bearing, but because of the pressure and numbness, I haven’t been able to put much weight on it yet. My doctor wants me to start trying to walk around more in the boot, but it still feels pretty tough. Just taking things day by day and hoping things continue to improve from here.f
