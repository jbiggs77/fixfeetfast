I had my MTP fusion and bunionectomy April 10. Fast forward to today, I just completed a 4-day trip to Disney World. Well over 20k steps each day in the absolutely blazing heat. The recovery has been a long road and I felt so discouraged at times. But recovery is possible and anyone who is feeling down and depressed can use my success story to motivate you through your pain.
