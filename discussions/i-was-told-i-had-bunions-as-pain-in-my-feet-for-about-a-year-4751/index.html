If there’s only been pain for a year and a half, it might be worthwhile to assess the footwear that you’ve been using — do you have space to spread toes in the shoes?

Community comment#4

When most of the deformity is at the end of the toe toward the toenail, this is an accommodation for big toe joint stiffness and possibly arthritis. It has the appearance of what we call hallux limitus. Consider a decompression osteotomy if your toe is not stiff.

Community comment#5

Looks like both. I have very similar going on with both my feet. I am very mindful not to cause more harm by using the big toe side of my feet, like gas pedal on car, use outside foot. Also, when I walk, I've learned to use a more march step than flex step like normal. It hasn't gotten worse. Doesn't correct toes, but doesn't increase problem anymore. I worked in Physical Therapy for 30+ years and one thing is for sure, I do NOT want surgery. It's hell to go thru and many times patients toes will revert to same problem down the road. Hope this helps. I use castor oil on them every night before bed to keep skin soft and increase mobility while I'm not weight-bearing. That helps me too. Good luck
