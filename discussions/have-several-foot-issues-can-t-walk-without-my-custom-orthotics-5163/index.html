Have several foot issues can't walk without my custom orthotics which helps Metatarsal/sesamoid pain, burning foot and arch pain. Problem finding sneakers to accommodate the orthotics and foot issues. The only sneaker that was comfortable and worked with my orthotics is New Balance 1340v2 -running sneaker. But discontinued. Need wide, stability, cushioned, deep toe box, sole doesn't bend in Half (not flexible). Sneakers are too tight with orthotics even with larger size, or not deep toe box, too flexible and not enough cushion. Only a couple of sneakers fit orthodics but not enough cushioning and too flexible which causes Metatarsal pain. Walking sneakers not enough cushioning , running best but still can't find. If I can't find new shoes will not be able to walk at all. Also had several pairs of orthotics made but haven't been able to replicate the ones I have that feel comfortable and help my issues. I have a small narrow foot but need room because of foot issues. Shoes like ortho feet, healthy feet, Drew have not walked in the past. Only had success with new balance in the past but can't find sneaker that works. Please advise. Thanks, Mindy
