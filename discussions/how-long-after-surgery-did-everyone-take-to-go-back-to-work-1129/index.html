Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#12

Uhmm I mean, I think it depends on your job, the procedure you have, and the accessibility you'd have when you go back. Like I work in the kitchen, there was no way I could attempt to work had I done it during the school year. If you are a teacher or even an admin assistant you might be able to manage better. It sucked using a good chunk of my summer being stuck on the couch/unable to walk but it was so worth it. Also, I live in the Cleveland area, we get snow and ice. For me, winter is not a good idea for a surgery of the foot in my case.
