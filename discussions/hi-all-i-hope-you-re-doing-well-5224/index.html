Walking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

Hi all, I hope you’re doing well. I had surgery on 12/08 and I’m now at home recovering, but I’m extremely annoyed by the itching. It’s so bad that it wakes me up during the night and bothers me constantly throughout the day. I had no choice but to apply steroid cream to the areas where the skin is most itchy — not on the wounds, as they’re covered with plasters. Has anyone else gone through this? I expected some itching later on, but not at such an early stage of recovery.

Community comment#3

Itching is one of the WORST symptoms to have during this recovery. Cold therapy (refrigerated gel packs) and using a blow dryer (set to the cool setting) provided me with loads of relief. So did Benadryl tablets. There are also oral medications that could help alleviate the itching. Hydroxyzine and Prudoxin are prescription tablets that aide in reducing that itchy feeling. Prescription topical creams like Triamcinolone or Clobetasol work great as well and can be applied (massaged into the skin) around the open wound (not on). I’d suggest speaking with either your surgeon or PCP ASAP. I really hope you’re able to find relief . The itching really sucks.
