Community comment#1

I used a walker in front of the toilet to help pull myself up . A friend that had the surgery told me about that an it was a huge help . I took 3 weeks off because I stand all day for work. If I had it to do all over again I would have taken longer off

Community comment#2

I had minimal invasive surgery and I took 3 days off. The first 3 days were really hard. But my surgery was Friday, then I took Monday to Wednesday off. I work from home as well. A knee scooter helped me the first week a lot! Make sure you ice it a lot and keep it up. When I went back to work, I would take a 10 minute break every hour or two to ice it and elevate.￼￼

Community comment#3

Surgery pillow to elevate as much as possible
