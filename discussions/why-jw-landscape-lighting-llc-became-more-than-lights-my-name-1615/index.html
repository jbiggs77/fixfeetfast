Why JW Landscape Lighting, LLC Became More Than Lights My name is Jim Wielgus, owner of JW Landscape Lighting, LLC. When I first started my company, my focus was helping homeowners improve and beautify their properties through professional landscape lighting. As I spent more time meeting customers and working on their homes, I noticed something important. Many homeowners needed help with much more than lighting. Every day, I was hearing the same things: “Do you know someone who can fix this?” “Can you help with this project?” “I’ve been meaning to get this done but don’t know who to call.” I realized there was a real need in our community for a trustworthy, reliable handyman service. Many homeowners, especially seniors, don’t always know what maintenance items need attention or who they can trust to do the work correctly. Others simply don’t have the time, tools, or physical ability to complete these projects themselves. With my background in construction and home improvement, including experience helping build homes from the foundation to the finishing touches, I knew I could help. That’s why JW Landscape Lighting, LLC has expanded beyond lighting services to become a trusted handyman company serving our community. What makes us different is that I personally meet with customers to understand their needs. I don’t believe in a one-size-fits-all approach. Every home and every homeowner is different. By listening to our customers, we can provide solutions that improve the safety, appearance, and value of their homes. We also believe in educating homeowners. Many of the services we advertise come from real situations we’ve encountered while working in homes throughout Northeast Ohio. We share maintenance tips and information because we want homeowners to be aware of things that can help protect and maintain their investment. At JW Landscape Lighting, LLC, our priorities are simple: • Honesty • Reliability • Trustworthiness • Quality workmanship • Outstanding customer service We are proud to be “More Than Lights.” We are committed to helping homeowners with the projects that make their homes safer, more comfortable, and better maintained. Thank you for trusting JW Landscape Lighting, LLC. We look forward to continuing to serve our community for years to come. See less JW Landscape Lighting · Follow Jun 4
