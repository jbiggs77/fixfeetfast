Mine is definitely from tight calfs I had PF in my right foot, I was on a scooter and in a boot.

Archive date 2026-07-060 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryToe Spacers, Orthotics & Braces

Mine is definitely from tight calfs I had PF in my right foot, I was on a scooter and in a boot. I started to sleep in a night splint this was a life changer and also I had physio on my calf. It disappeared. It wasn’t quick 6-12months. I also had orthotics made for my trainers. I now feel it slightly in my left foot, I’ve started with physio on my calf and tonight my night splint. I’m hoping I have the same response but alot quicker.
