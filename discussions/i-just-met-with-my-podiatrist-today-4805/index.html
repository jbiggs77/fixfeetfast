I just met with my podiatrist today.

Archive date 2026-09-2417 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Plantar Fasciitis Treatment

I just met with my podiatrist today. I had 2 injections-1 in each foot. He basically said until I change my lifestyle and stop being so active with work and dance they will not heal. Its typical for me to be on my feet at least 14 hours a day. Left ft is worse, partial rupture/bunion, right foot small tears are possible. Unfortunately I can't walk away from my business. And I am too stubborn to not ever dance again. So the cortisone is hopefully buying time before surgeries. Both feet fascia release and a bunion repair. I suspected a bunion starting to form. He said it's much easier to fix early on, so he will take care of it at the same time. Has anyone had both feet done at once with a bunion repair? I don't know if he will want to do both at the same time, but I would prefer to get both out of the way for minimal down time. How bad is recovery? Did it ultimately help long term or did you have a relapse of symptoms? So, on a positive note, looks like I am off of dish duty on Thanksgiving since I am off my feet for a week!
