Hi everyone! Thanks so much for this group. I've been following along for a long time and have gotten so many good tips from all of you. Well, it's finally my turn. After going to many doctors over the years and specifically 3 good surgeons this year, I picked the one and the date. I'm scheduled for a Lapidus bunionectomy this Friday, 8/28 on my left foot. Eventually I'd like to do both but it's the left that causes actual prevention of activities through injuries. I'd love y Meg Scheez This surgery is literally slowly taking me out. On week 3 post surgery for: Cut and straightened the second toe Shortened/repositioned the second metatarsal Megan LD I had my MTP fusion and bunionectomy April 10. Fast forward to today, I just completed a 4-day trip to Disney World. Well over 20k steps each day in the absolutely blazing heat. The recovery has been a long road and I felt so discouraged at times. But recovery is possible and anyone who is feeling down and depressed can use my success story to motivate you through your pain. Shout out to my Hoka Cliftons and recovery slides for supporting me!
