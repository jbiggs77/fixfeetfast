If you are still non-weight bearing and have returned to work, how are you getting in and out of the car?

Archive date 2026-10-0617 archived replies
Walking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

If you are still non-weight bearing and have returned to work, how are you getting in and out of the car? I am almost 4 weeks post-op and still non-weight bearing for atleast another 4 weeks. I have a scooter to get around but I have only driven to appointments since the surgery and I've brought my daughter along with me to assist with getting in and out of the car and storing my scooter. I am supposed to go back to work on March 2nd, but I am going to see about extending my leave a little longer because its not practical for me to get in the car and put my scooter away.

If you are non-weight-bearing, then you shouldn’t be driving to an appointment because if you had to slam on your brakes that would really ruin your foot. ￼ I don’t know what kind of surgery you had but I just had my tweak appointment and I am still non-weight-bearing and I’m hoping that by week four I can bear some weight so that I’m able to drive. ￼ Are you able to get another doctors excuse or put on short-term disability or limited work hours?

Community comment#2

I’m still NWB and have returned to work. I use a folding walker to get from the house to the car, then fold it and put it in the front seat. When I get to work, I use the walker to get to the back of the car, and trade the walker for the scooter (resting my NWB knee on the bumper while I exchange devices). I’ve been leaving the scooter in my car at night and use a rollator in the house.
