Archive date 2026-09-2514 archived replies
Surgery Complications & Wound HealingPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Has anyone had their hardware removed? I can feel the screws when I touch my foot and I can also feel them at times with weather changes. I also had a plantar plate repair at the same time and have irritation in the joint where those screws are. I'm scheduled to get them removed on Tuesday, so any advice would be greatly appreciated.

I didn't. Doctor said I could but they don't bother me, I can't feel them. And I don't know how they are removed but I wouldn't go for another general anesthesia for it. The was the only bad part of my surgery; one whole day vomiting.

Community comment#2

The surgeon was able to get my 3 bunion screws out and 1 of the tiny screws from the plantar plate repair. The other small screw had the head bust off, so the rest is in my bone. She cleaned out scar tissue that was in the joint and growing under the incision from the plantar plate repair. Hopefully this does the trick.
