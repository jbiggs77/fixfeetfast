I have to say this was the best experience!

Archive date 2026-04-220 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

I have to say this was the best experience! I was reading all the post op posts on here and was scared to death of the recovery after bunion surgery. Posts of so much pain, not being able to bear weight for several weeks, unable to move their big toe without alot of pain and no driving for several months. I cancelled my surgery. Then I found Foot First Podiatry and Dr Sklar! Holy Moly! The absolute opposite of all the posts! Weight bearing the same day, not on the ball of my foot.
