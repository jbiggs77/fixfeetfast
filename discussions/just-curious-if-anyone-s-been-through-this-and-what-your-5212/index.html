Walking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

Just curious if anyone’s been through this and what your recovery time was... I tore a ligament and stretched tendons, as well as broke my cuboid bone in Nov. Mid April I had surgery to repair the ligament, and clean up the tendons... I had a split for 5 days, hard cast two weeks, and I’ve been in a walking boot since Tuesday... total pins and needles with any weight! What’s next? How long until I can walk without a walker? This has seriously messed with me mentally... not to mention I am dying to clean my freaking house!

Community comment#1

I had a stress fracture in my cuboid bone too in November but thought my peroneal tendinitis was back. Got mri in Jan and then in boot for 8 weeks, weaned myself out the next two weeks and now been out completely for two weeks. Think I have been doing good but also think overdoing. I have some swelling now and can’t wear my birks or Chacos yet. Just wondering how long this thing takes to heal fully...I want my life back too!! Not even sure how I did this!

this is darn near me! I was in the walking boot until March, this surgery was the MOST painful thing I’ve ever experienced! I was back at work after 2 c sections in 3 weeks. I tried to go back and said forget it and went in short term disability last week. I sit at a desk, and even elevated the swelling was terrible!
