So good and bad. I went to the Dr today cause my left toe next to my pinky feels like someone is squeezing it…

Archive date 2026-10-026 archived replies
Toenail Fungus Treatment

So good and bad. I went to the Dr today cause my left toe next to my pinky feels like someone is squeezing it. Sometimes real painful, sometimes not. I got an ingrown toenail yay! And of course that toe has fungus on it. Referral to podiatrist, just have to wait a couple wks which for a specialist as we all know is not bad. My PCP gave me antibiotics and told me to soak it too. At least it is a fairly easy fix! Good news....he said your fungus (on 3 toes) is really not bad. We discussed Lamisil , he is for it and has only had one person have a side effect in his time prescribing it. He said my liver is good so if I want it, he will prescribe it. For now, I'm just gonna think about it, continue with the OTC I been using and get my ingrown toe fixed. And see if the podiatry office offers medical pedicures cause I badly need one!. All in all a pretty good day!
