Community comment#1

I had my right foot done Aug 23. I was two weeks nwb and then I was in a boot until November. I just had my left foot done 2.5 weeks ago and I am still NWB with this foot. It’s taking a little longer to heal up. I will probably be in a boot until July.

Community comment#2

I had mine done January 21 and can wear only a couple shoes comfortably. It might be less recovery than other methods but it was way more than I anticipated. Results are good, but still sore and swells if I do too much.

Community comment#3

It's just a fancier version of lapidus I think. I did lapidus on both feet and am so happy I did. Recovery is hard but worth it!

Community comment#4

I had lapiplasty March 1, and now in a wide walking shoe/boot But still find numbness, especially first steps in am limping around, stiffness, had an implant. Not sure how long “normal” is but it feels like a long recovery

Community comment#5

I was non weight bearing for one week, boot for six more weeks, then a gym show with a carbon fiber insert for two weeks. It was a pretty easy recovery except the first week. I'm guessing it will take some time for the swelling to go away, but I'm averaging 7000 steps per day.

Community comment#6

I had lapiplasty 2 years ago. My foot is now shaped completely different than prior and a whole size bigger than my other foot. I am still in pain.

Community comment#7

My 19 year old had her left foot done in Feb and she was just released from PT today and cleared to do whatever she wants activity wise. She sailed through all of it. However. She has a VERY high pain tolerance. She just fights through the pain and the doctor said it could be a year before it eases up. She is going to get her right foot done next month. Hopefully it’s just as easy.
