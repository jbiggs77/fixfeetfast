I’m looking for experiences and advice from people who have had revision surgery on the big toe/MTP I joint, especially from other runners. I had both feet operated on in 2019 because of hallux valgus/bunion deformity. Unfortunately, the deformity came back quite quickly. Within about a year, by 2020, the angle had already returned to more than 35 degrees. Now they are over 40 and 45 degrees. The pain comes especially in the second toe. So I’m now facing revision surgery. I have recently seen two different orthopedic surgeons, and they have somewhat different approaches. One surgeon is considering a corrective surgery around the first metatarsal (I-MT), with the aim of correcting the alignment while preserving the MTP I joint. However, because of the previous surgery, there are some uncertainties regarding the available bone and the stability of the correction. The other surgeon thinks that both MTP I joints now have osteoarthritis, fixed deformity and limited range of motion. In his opinion, fusion (arthrodesis) of the MTP I joint would be the more reliable option for treating the pain and stabilizing the joint. The case will also be discussed at a foot surgery meeting to get a consensus on the best approach. I have to admit that the idea of MTP I fusion really scares me, mainly because I’m a runner and being active is a very important part of my life. I have run very long distances, including 120 km ultramarathons in uneven mountain terrain. I would really like to continue running and being able to move as naturally as possible. I am nearly 35 years old. So I would especially love to hear from: * Runners who have had MTP I fusion – were you able to return to running? * How does running feel after the surgery compared with before? * Have you been able to run long distances or ultras? * How do uneven surfaces, hills and downhill running feel? * How long did it take before you were able to run again?
