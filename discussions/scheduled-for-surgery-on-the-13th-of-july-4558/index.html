Community comment#9

I had twilight anesthesia, which to me felt the same as general except my throat wasn't sore when I woke up (no need for a breathing tube with twilight) and I wasn' nauseus (differen drugs). Not sure how I'd feel in your situation and kind of surprised (very) that it's done that way, but I can say that I've had a couple of procedures done on my feet (ingrown toenail removal and removal of a deep deep plastic splinter) where they just gave me a lot of local anesthetic and then put a curtain between me and my foot, and I felt absolutely nothing with either one. Anesthetic works great. Nerve block, local, general, twilight- any way about it, it works. You won't feel anything. So you just need to manage your anxiety and the rest will go smoothly. And the're gonna give you somethign for your anxiety as well, so don't let yourself spend much time being worried about this going into it. Trust that you chose a good doctor, and believe in your choice, and just look forward to being on the other side of all this. you can do this. 🙂
