Hi all! I just wanted to introduce myself and share a photo I took. I’m Stephanie. I’m 52. I’ve had bunions since I was a teenager in high school. I put off the surgery as I was told to not do it until it hurt enough. No one tells you what enough is. I waited until it was hard to walk and hurt and I was falling. I had my left foot surgery May 25th as it was the worst of the two and the one giving me the most pain.
