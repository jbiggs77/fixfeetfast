I am 43, almost 6 months post op on my left foot (all 5 toes done) and 4 weeks post op on my right foot (bunion and bunionette done). Back to wearing normal sneakers and sandals with no pain whatsoever on my left foot. Still in the boot on my right foot due to swelling. Its been an easy process so far with minimal pain. Just remember it can take up to a year for your foot to settle after surgery so you have to be patient with yourself.
