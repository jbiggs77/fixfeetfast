Hi, I'm new to this group and due to have Right Tailor's Bunion surgery in a couple of weeks.

Archive date 2026-10-166 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

Hi, I'm new to this group and due to have Right Tailor's Bunion surgery in a couple of weeks. Little toe has got to the point where it is sooo powerfully going under/gouging into the next toe that no longer able to walk properly. I've had a sore on the bunion that the surgeon has wanted to be fully healed before he'll do surgery and that has finally healed! Apart from the usual worries, I'm wondering how I'm going to be able to sleep on my back with a shoe/boot on when I'm normally a front sleeper. I was planning on using spare pillows to make a gentle slope to take my foot up to the right height. I think I will probably have to elevate both legs together to avoid back ache or twisting. Is it OK to turn onto my side with a pillow between my knees if I really can't get comfortable? Or is that a big no no. Anyone got any suggestions or advice? Also - we don't have a sofa (!!) just chairs/rocking chairs. I am planning to buy a sun lounger/campbed for lying on in the living room during some of the daytime, so I don't feel shut off in the bedroom. Do you think that would work? Thanks! Lisa

You dont need to sleep in the boot/shoe thats only needed when walking around i never wore mine whilst elevated, just when I got up to move around bathroom kitchen etc .

Community comment#3

OK, Grace & June, thanks for that. I've not had the op yet, so will see what surgeon says, but had assumed it had to stay on in case foot got bashed/nudged in the night!!

Community comment#4

I am a side sleeper and was worried too. Oddly enough, I don't have an issue falling asleep on my back with my leg up. My surgeon did say I could put a pillow between my knees and sleep like that if I needed to. Ask your surgeon and he should help you with this. Sorry you are having so many issues. Best to you.
