Community comment#3

Please reach out to your surgeon as that is not normal. Your toe should sit flat without lifting up like that. It could be for many reasons. Formed collagen fibers can cause joint tightness that limits the toe from resting flat. Surgeons adjust tendons and ligaments during surgery which can temporarily over-tighten the top of the foot. The bone may have been intentionally or excessively angled upward during realignment to prevent the bunion from returning. Wishing you the best.
