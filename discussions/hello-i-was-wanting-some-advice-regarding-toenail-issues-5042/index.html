Hello, I was wanting some advice regarding toenail issues.

Archive date 2026-10-0213 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound HealingToenail Fungus Treatment

Hello, I was wanting some advice regarding toenail issues. At 13 years old I went to the doctor for an odor when I clipped my toenails, and pain. I was diagnosed with an ingrown toenail but I’m convinced it was untreated toenail fungus this entire time. I’m 22 now, and I’m so embarrassed because it’s worsened and my concerns weren’t taken seriously. I assumed I had an overgrowth of keratin but after recently noticing the grooves and discoloration has gotten worse, I believe it is a fungal infection. I don’t know what my next steps should be, I definitely want to see a doctor asap. Some people have had success with home remedies but I don’t want to risk it getting worse. In the meantime while I wait to be seen, is there anything that I can do? I have discoloration, and my toenails have gotten thicker over time. I’m really worried it has gotten to the point where I might need toenail removal. If anyone can provide me with any advice, reassurance, or information, it would be greatly appreciated. Thank you!
