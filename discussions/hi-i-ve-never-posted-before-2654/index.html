Community comment#1

If you were in UK I honestly don’t think they would even consider surgery for your big toe- yours would I think be classed as mild. I can have surgery- mine are ‘extreme’ - lump is far bigger and my toe almost horizontal across my other toes. Even so I’m still considering whether to go ahead due to the recovery time/pain/ non weight bearing practicalities etc. it’s not straightforward. However, if the lump you mention is in your mid foot ( can’t really tell from photos) that might be a different story. Have you had x rays/scans? Seems to me like you need a confirmed diagnosis to be able to decide anything. I really feel for you that cost has to have a bearing on what you do! Thank goodness for the NHS ! Not perfect but compared to your situation it’s incredible
