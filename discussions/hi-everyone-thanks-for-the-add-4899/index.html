Hi Everyone Thanks for the Add. I’m trying to find other Cancer people on Chemo Taxotere that have been diagnosed with Hand and Foot Syndrome? If you never heard of it your heel is on fire (worse sunburn ever) Your nails start to darken, lift up, causing fungus under them. Without nails on your hands it’s difficult to do things, the 0nes on my toes I extremely painful This is horrible to where I need a cane to walk and only wear cushy sandals. I’ve seen podiatrist all he said was moisturizer (Urea)(Lidocaine) and neosporin for the nails. Nothing helps This weekend put me over the top in pain. I’ve got a message off to my oncologist and also request an appointment with a Podiatrist Surgeon. I’m not sure if I can add pictures as this is my first post?
