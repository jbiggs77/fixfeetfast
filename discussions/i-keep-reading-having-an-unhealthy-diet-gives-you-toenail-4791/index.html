I keep reading having an unhealthy diet gives you toenail fungus !

Archive date 2026-09-2316 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryToenail Fungus Treatment

I keep reading having an unhealthy diet gives you toenail fungus ! As a former professional footballer for 22 years i have had the best dietician, personal trainers fitness programmes I've maintained very good fitness all my life and a healthy balanced diet . I have never had any toenail issues until 15 years ago after my career. I shared changing rooms with many other team mates including showers etc etc My feet and toenails were perfect during that time . About 15 years ago I noticed my nails were becoming slightly thicker but colour was still normal. I assumed it was years of kicking footballs around perhaps took it's toll . Then over time they started to turn yellow brown colours and got confirmed by my doctor as nail fungus. This was deep hidden fungus that you don't see I never knew was there , my doctor said this probably started while I was playing and slow growing fungus at the root just grew over time . You can have surface fungus that is easy to spot and treat but hidden fungus is the worst. No matter how far you file down you will never reach close enough for any product to get through to treat it . When I see people mentioning unhealthy diets and gut issues contribute to toenail fungus it's complete bull!! My best friend since school days has a horrendous diet of pizza's, cakes ,fizzy drinks, alcohol doesn't exercise and he has perfect feet and really good toenails, make that make sense? Doctor put me on terbinafine tablets that only worked for a few months the fungus never really went away......he said there really isn't an answer out there if I'm honest unless you get them removed , some people just get lucky and some don't . Eveyone is on their own journey with this some of you have minor fungus and some have severe , not all products Will work the same for everyone so personal management of your own level of toenail fungus is ongoing for many of us unfortunately. For those that cleared yours up you are extremely lucky .
