Community comment#1

Do a search in the group for 'shoes'. Altra, Topo & Hoka are popular. Look up Gait Happens on YouTube for wide toe box video. Choose wide toe box over wide fit.

Community comment#2

Topo are great! They have a wide toe box. I just tried some on my good foot today at REI.

Community comment#3

I have Altra Experience Form, Altra Timp, Altra Paradigm, and Altra Lone Peak. I wear the Altra Timp and Altra Paradigm without any insole to get more space. I think that the Altra Lone Peak might be my favourite of the ones I have. I ordered several styles in various sizes and therefore I had returns to make. Returns went smoothly with the Altra Running shoe brand website. Specialty running stores carry the Altra running Shoes but I find the style and size selections are better from the brand website. For special occasion footwear look up the website called Anya’s Reviews, she has recommendations. For better foot health I wear my Altra Running Shoes almost all of the time and they have improved my quality of life!

Community comment#4

Brooks are also great for bunions. You can get some styles in extra wide.

Community comment#5

Sounds like you might need some orthotics as well.

Community comment#6

Brooks Ghost Max width 2E are wonderful. One of the only athletic shoes wide enough for my bunions
