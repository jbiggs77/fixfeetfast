Dear everyone, I am reaching out because I’m really devastated and would welcome any advice.

Archive date 2026-09-2619 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryHammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPost-Surgery Swelling & InflammationPain Management After Foot Surgery

Dear everyone, I am reaching out because I’m really devastated and would welcome any advice. On Friday, I had a bunionectomy on the NHS. It was supposed to include surgery on the two hammertoes. When I came to after the anaesthetic, I realised that the hammer toes had not been done. I was told that my consultant had been called away on an emergency and probably had to finish the operation quickly. This slightly contradicted with him phoning me and saying that he hadn’t realised that I was in so much pain from the hammertoes. To be honest, I was in no pain from the bunion (though now it is excruciating!) and only the hammertoe pain persuaded me to go ahead with the operation. I was crying on the ward for 3 1/2 hours and then all night after the operation. I have written a polite email to request a meeting with him and have been told I will be meeting with somebody from the team. I don’t know what to do. When I came to, I desperately wanted to go back into the theatre immediately so that I could have the hammer toes done but apparently one has to wait until all of the swelling et cetera has gone down which will take months. I was supposed to be moving abroad at the end of February and now that is in question if I have to go back and have another operation on the hammertoes. I’m even thinking I might have to save up and try and do it privately. Any words of wisdom or advice greatly appreciated! Thank you so much.
