10 days postop. Had my appointment with my podiatrist today and was fitted for a boot. My initial thought was…

Archive date 2026-06-204 archived replies
Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound Healing

10 days postop. Had my appointment with my podiatrist today and was fitted for a boot. My initial thought was that it looks worse however, my doctor ensured me that I was healing nicely. No sign of infection and my stitches are dissolvable, which is a relief that I will not have to have them removed. Weight-bearing as tolerated for short distances, so I'll be keeping the scooter around if needed. I do have to say my doctor is wonderful and did reassure me that taking one more week off of work is OK. Looks like three more weeks in the boot and then transitioning to shoes just in time for the snow to fall. See less
