I had minimally invasive surgery on my foot on 1/22.

Archive date 2026-10-0328 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryMinimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Pain Management After Foot Surgery

I had minimally invasive surgery on my foot on 1/22. Pain has never been severe and I’ve kept on top of the medication. However, I see all these posts online about how easy and how great this was and I feel at a loss. While there is no excruciating pain- I’m a quite honestly very uncomfortable quite often. For the first time I had my foot unwrapped and tried to sleep that way last night and it was honestly horrible for me. I just don’t know if I expected to feel less or if I’m just a total wimp. I want to cry at the discomfort! To top it off I think they may have forgotten to take one stitch out! I’m supposed to be able to get out of the surgical shoe next Tuesday.
