I'm sitting at about 27 days post-op since my MIS bunion surgery.

Archive date 2026-09-2715 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryMinimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Post-Surgery Swelling & InflammationBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot Surgery

I'm sitting at about 27 days post-op since my MIS bunion surgery. I was recently asked what prompted me to have bunion surgery at all. About a year or so ago, I was walking with my then 4-year-old son, and towards the end of the day, I had an awful, throbbing pain on both sides of my feet by the base of my big toes that made this grown man want to cry. And that was the turning point. You have to remember, I've had my bunions since Judge Judy, first launched her syndicated show. There was no pain for decades. But I dismissed my growing discomfort and misdiagnosed myself with gout, until it got so bad that I was limited to wearing only one pair of shoes. I still have to learn to respect the value of patience because swelling is an absolute BEAST. But I'll use the Michael Bolton technique and apply some Time, Love and Tenderness. Social media is a weird place sometimes where people think it's a fight between MIS vs. traditional methods.That's not the actual reality. It's about finding the method that works best for your personal circumstances. We all know the world can be a little crazy... but EVERY human should have the right to walk pain-free. Be good to yourselves, my friends.
