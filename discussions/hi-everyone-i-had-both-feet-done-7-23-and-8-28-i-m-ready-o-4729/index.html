Community comment#1

I had MIS on my right foot July 2nd. Started golfing at the end of August. 😁

Community comment#2

I’m six months postop and still having a hard time sometimes. Had to walk in a long airport this week and my foot was killing me. It wasn’t the toe though, it’s the top of my foot. I have arthritis as well as the bunion that was repaired. And arthritis on the other foot with still a bunion. My next surgery is in a month. And I am accepting the fact that there will still be pain due to the arthritis. The doctor told me as the foot positioned itself, arthritis might get better. But it hasn’t. ￼I think some of us expect all of our issues to go away when only one thing has been fixed. At least that’s what happened to me. But then I am 74. 🤦‍♀️￼🤷‍♀️

Community comment#3

I’m 3 years out and sadly haven’t forgotten but also able to do everything as before🤷🏼‍♀️

Community comment#4

I’m 2 weeks and a day out and only complaint right now is I’m still in a boot and a splint for 2 more weeks. Big toe did swell more yesterday. I work from home and didn’t prop it up as much

Community comment#5

I had mis on one foot on 7/23. Last week I transitioned from surgical boot to sneakers. My surgeon told me 12 weeks was needed to completely heal. My foot is stiff and sometimes has mild pain. My walking foot movement is far from normal. PT is helpful.

Community comment#6

I feel like everyone has been saying they were able to walk and go back to work within a week or two. I'm on my third week and I still can not walk without crutches. I have been extremely frustrated that my recovery has been much slower. I was in immense pain my first and some of my second week. Pain pills weren't touching my pain, got no sleep, didn't start walking on it until my second week even though I was cleared to walk on it right away. I've just accepted that everyone's recovery looks different. I don't know how you did two one is enough to make me go crazy and not want to do my other foot 😮‍💨.

Community comment#7

... For me I'd say a month but everyone's journey is different and weight-bearing isn't quite the same as walking with full range of motion. I was lucky because I had two weeks off work, but had previous holiday booked. This gave me a total of 5 weeks off...which I'm happy to have had I have no regrets about having MIS. But you may need more than just two weeks.

Community comment#9

I had mine done 9/2 I just went back to work yesterday. Started walking again Thursday when I had my post op appointment, I’m in the boot for another 4 weeks and then next week I can take the wrap off and start using toe spacers. The last time I had this done 16 years ago, it was a whole different experience. After stitches removed, I went into a hard cast for 4-6 weeks and then a boot for another 4-6 weeks.
