Toenail Fungus Treatment

Hi! Just curious if anyone else notices air pockets under their toenails after using nail polish? I don’t know if I have a fungus or not. If I let them air out it grows out but then when I polish them and after about 4 weeks I remove the polish there’s like big air pockets developing under them. Just looks white and I can easily put tools under there to clear out dirt or anything. it’s been happening since I was a child and I’m 38 now.
