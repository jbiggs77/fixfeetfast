Hi I'm getting MIS on the 11th November and it's my sons high school graduation on the 15th realistically how much pain will I be in I don't want to miss his graduation. What would be your best advice on getting through it for a few hours please is foot up on a chair going to be enough?

Community comment#1

If you can get yourself a wheel chair with a leg support so you can have your leg out straight I think you will be okay. You will also need to make sure you keep on top of your medication. Also if you can put an ice pack in a freezer type bag so that you can put it under your knee. I think you will enjoy your son’s graduation. Just make sure everyone takes care off you so you can do as little as possible! Have a wonderful time

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#2

lovely advice thank you so much

Community comment#3

I honestly would reschedule the surgery if you can. It’s a long day celebrating your son and your energy is going to be low and your body really needs you to rest I would not be able to do it and I’m two weeks out.

Community comment#4

Hi make sure you take your pain relief every four hours elevate your foot as much as you can that helped me when I had my bunion operation done in April.

Community comment#5

I had surgery on a Friday and by Tuesday I was up and didn’t need a crutch except for long distance. Until then elevate, keep icing and take the pain pills.

Community comment#6

I had MIS on June 16 and rode with my husband on June 19 - only 3 days later - to pick my daughter up from summer camp, eat at the family luncheon, and watch the final camp assembly. It was 1.5 hours each way - and I was at camp about 2 hours- and I was totally fine. I sat in backseat and propped up leg during the travel time and used crutches when at camp, but it wasn't really necessary to have the crutches - was really easier to heel step. My opinion is that you will be fine- but I know each person is different!
