Community comment#2

If you’re currently wearing wide toe box, aren’t Altra Running Shoes and Topo Athletic Footwear the shoes to consider? Hoka shoes taper at the toe area don’t they? At any rate, the Altra and the Topo are fairly pricey… but they are probably better than worsening the bunions and crippling ourselves. The surgery doesn’t look easy and it must require a lot of food, laundry, and cleaning support right? And if you are employed the surgery requires time off of work which is costly. Are Altra Running Shoes or Topo Athletic Footwear probably worthwhile? Like do we underestimate the importance of our feet? Both the Altra Running Shoes brand website and the Topo Athletic Footwear brand website offer trial periods when ordering directly from the sites. And there are great descriptions of the various styles to help determine the right model for our situations. Also, there is a Facebook group where users of Altra share fit and wear experiences that is helpful in choosing the best style for each of our situations— the Facebook group is called “Altra Shoes Fan Page”. It helped me to read posts and responses on that group before I tried my first pair. The Altra and the Topo have improved my quality of life, hopefully the right footwear can help your situation too. Most of us are born with feet shaped likes pizza slices and the footwear that is shaped that way is better 🍕🍕

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#6

Hi Leslie, I’m also hypermobile 🙂👋 I wore custom (expensive!) orthotics from 2012 to 2025 and I’ve recently put them away my feet have improved so much. I’ve been wearing Altra running shoes and Topo Athletic Footwear for a little over two years and I’ve been strengthening as per the directions of the experts Dr Perez and Dr Conley at Gaithappens. The muscles are beginning to do what the orthotics were doing! Toe splay allows the muscles to develop and support our feet. It’s worthwhile to check out info by both Gaithappens and also the drs of physiotherapy who produce content for “thefootcollective”. It’s been a revelation that our feet do best in wide toe box footwear- more wedge shaped- and it has improved my life so much to change up to “footshaped” (pizza slice shaped) footwear. 🍕🍕
