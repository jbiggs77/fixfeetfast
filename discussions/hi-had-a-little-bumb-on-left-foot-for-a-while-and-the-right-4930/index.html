Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#10

I hope the links help. The reasoning behind my footwear choices are the following. 1. Newton has a lower heel so the foot feels more "in touch" with the ground and to me more stable as I have some new balance running trainers with a big fat heel which angle your foot down onto the ball of your foot putting the pressure where you really don't want it right now. 2. I think I have trouble all the way down the chain in terms of posterior strength/ balance through the back, hips, glutes and all the way down to the feet so the bunions are a result of imbalances. So I'm hoping to work on strengthening the whole body and improving balance to try and fix the issue long term. 3. depending on your work try not to wear shoes that squeeze your feet too much, if possible, shoes off always the best way. Hope I haven't rambled too much just wanted to try and help. Good luck!
