I have what I believe is called Tailor bunion.

Archive date 2026-10-087 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryFlat Feet & Arch Support

I have what I believe is called Tailor bunion. Excessive stinging and sharp pain on the outside of my pinky toes on both feet. It hurts when I walk, especially with tight shoes (I try my best to not wear any of my shoes that are on the tighter side,)￼. The pain and discomfort is getting fairly excessive at this point. ￼I’m sure this question gets asked a lot so I do apologize, but does anyone have any recommendations on things to do for some comfort or relief? My family has a big trip to Disney World coming up in June, so I don’t really want to look at any surgical options until we get back if possible. Would appreciate any advice or suggestions! TYIA. ￼

Community comment#6

My podiatrist cut a silicon-like tube that fits over the little toes, then sliced it open so that it also covers the bunion. Works great. He said to coat the tube in baby powder every three days to keep it from getting sticky. Now…a personal suggestion from someone who spent five days in WDW and tore up both her legs and feet……get a scooter. I’ll never go there agin without renting one.

For you right now....I would get toe spreaders for all your toes and do certain exercises and manipulate your toes while wearing toe spreaders and get a pair of barefoot shoes. They are shaped like feet allowing for natural toe spread. There's a site called The Bunion Bulletin that's got heaps of info and videos that could help.
