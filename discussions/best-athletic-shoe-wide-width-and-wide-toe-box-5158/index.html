Community comment#7

After years of podiatrists recommending/selling shoes to satisfy my hubby's/my very different foot needs, none of them really comfortable but 'okay', we tried a shoe smith who specialized in custom orthodics and shoe modification; that worked pretty great and not too expensive! We now both where HOKA brands. They've specialty lines for supination/over-under pronators/etc. They support differently, some have rocker bottoms, several lines are for running, while those we get are for walking/hiking. Check them out. Most sporting goods carry 1-10 styles, it seems.
