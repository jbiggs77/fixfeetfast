Toenail Fungus Treatment

This September marks two years since my first visit to the podiatrist. I started with laser treatment—initially for nearly a year—but it didn't work. Then I stopped going for a while due to personal reasons and waited until March of this year to resume treatment; I was prescribed terbinafine for six months, and I’ll finish taking the pills in a month. The first pic is from September 2024, and the second is from today! I didn't experience any side effects with the terbinafine.
