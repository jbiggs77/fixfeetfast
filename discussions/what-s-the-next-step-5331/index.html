What's the next step?

Archive date 2026-10-203 archived replies
Walking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

What's the next step? I have been in a below the knee cast for 8 weeks, with a k wire sticking out around the side of my heel, that runs nearly the length of my foot. What would usually come after this? I see my surgeon tomorrow to have the wire out and I'm going insane thinking what could be next. I am also terrified at the thought of them telling me to put any weight on my foot. It's been a very long road for me (haven't walked properly since my first surgery in November, which failed and resulted in a second surgery in March). Wearing this cast has been the biggest safety net ever, as much as I hate the thing.
