I am so discouraged, I could cry.

Archive date 2026-10-144 archived replies
Post-Surgery Swelling & InflammationPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

I am so discouraged, I could cry. I am 3 mos PO lapidus/3 perm screws. All my exercises center on getting the ball of my foot and big toe to have more flexibility. That's the only places I really have any swelling anymore. I'm pretty sure I now have full blown tendonitis at the bottom of the ball of my foot. This has been even more irritated by the tiptoe exercises both my dr. and my therapist gave me. I told my therapist about it this morning and she said to back off, only do repetitions just to the point of pain, don't push through it. At this point, doing just one of any of the exercises hurts. My other huge concern, and not sure you can tell from the photo, but my big toe is slanted to the right so that I am walking on the inside of my toe instead of flat on the bottom. The toenail is not pointing to the sky but rather towards the second toe. My dr. says this will resolve itself although I have no idea how. My therapist says it won't and I have to sit and hold my toe in the proper position several times a day to get the muscles working right. I don't think that's going to work either. I have a call into the nurse. I guess one of my questions is going to be if I might get a cortisone shot in the ball of my foot (I had tendonitis in my thumb one year and after months of suffering, a cortisone shot was an instant cure.) I feel like I can't make progress on anything with it hurting to much. Anyone else suffered from these problems?
