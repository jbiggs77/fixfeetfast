Community comment#5

I have resumed this recently after lapsing for a few months...generally it's best to start gradually and wear them for a few minutes at rest then build up to walking a bit in them. Some progress to wearing them practically all the time. I am having to use them with caution having lapsed or they cause my soles to ache more. It helps overall though.

Community comment#6

Community comment#7

I have been wearing mine for parts of the day for a couple of weeks and making a big difference to me , but you do have to build up and persevere a bit. I've quickly built up to wearing for hours and walking in them for half an hour. I wear them in my Altras where the toe box is wide enough. I do still find them unconfortable when worn for a long time, but now the only discomfort is the inevitable discomfort from wearing what amounts to rubber bands on my toes, which is relieved as soon as I take them off. The actual spacer parts are fine. I'm surprised at how much they've helped. I'm in the UK and mine were £3.99 for 2 pairs from Amazon.
