Walking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

I will be 9 weeks on Wednesday (both feet done). I hit 13,000 steps today, BUT I still walk stiff so my calves are on fire. They feel hard as a rock and I can't get them to soften up. I know it has to do with how I walk. Help! I will have more steps tomorrow. At this point, I think my calves hurt more than my toes/feet. Side note- I had three disolvable stitches popping out that I was convinced were never coming out. They FINALLY came out last night. I think all the walking pulled them loose. I am very happy about this! See less

Pfister Definitely see if you can find some compression socks at an airport shop. If not, try to get them when you arrive at your destination. Walgreens or CVS or another drugstore should have them.
