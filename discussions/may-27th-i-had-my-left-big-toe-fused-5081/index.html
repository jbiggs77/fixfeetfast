May 27th i had my left big toe fused.

Archive date 2026-09-149 archived replies
Physical Therapy & Foot Exercises

May 27th i had my left big toe fused. My other 4 toes were fine and straight. About a month ago 3 of my other toes have started to curl over. My surgeon said the only way to fix them is to go to physical therapy then to have them pinned. I went yesterday and got a second opinion. I got told to stop physical therapy for now because its not going to help will after the toes are fixed. She told me right now they dont need pinned at all. She can straighten the toes just when you let go they just curl back up. So october 1st she is going to do flexor tenotomy on 3 of my toes. Im going to just let her do it in the drs office instead of in the OR. Anyone have just done? Whats recovery like?
