Long post I’m sorry!!!

Archive date 2026-10-1619 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryMinimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound Healing

Long post I’m sorry!!! I had minor invasive bunion surgery on my right foot in August 2024. Then a second surgery to remove a hardware in October 2024, then another surgery to shave my bone down in December 2024. I still have pain and feel sore all the time. I went to the foot doctor two weeks ago and told him I feel something, almost like a piece of the hardware on the side of my foot, and it’s really painful. We rooks X-rays and the hardware looks fine (two doctors said this). He gave me a Cortizone shot in the area that is sore. Not sure if that even helped or not, bexause it still sore. I don’t know what else to do! I’m so frustrated with this doctor!!! I have a Disneyland trip planned next month and would like to be able to wear shoes and be able to walk around. Right now I’m only able to wear crocs without any pain. I don’t know if my feet will be comfortable enough to wear crocs all day at Disneyland! I go back to the foot doctor in 2 weeks for a recheck. I just don’t know what else to do so I don’t have to be sore!!
