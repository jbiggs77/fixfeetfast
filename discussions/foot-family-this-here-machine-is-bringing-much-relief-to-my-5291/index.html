Foot family this here machine is bringing much relief to my feet, legs and knees.

Archive date 2026-10-173 archived replies
Post-Surgery Swelling & InflammationPain Management After Foot Surgery

Foot family this here machine is bringing much relief to my feet, legs and knees. The knees are above the legs and the legs are above the feet. Keep in mind that if the knees are inflamed this could mean that they're congested with inflammation leaving your circulation in your legs to not flow correctly to your feet and ankles. With that being said the feet and ankles become clogged and there's foot problems and most of you know how that pain feels. Horrible! I take this machine and massage 2 times a day from the top of my knees to the bottom of my feet going up and down and around on both knees, legs, calves, ankles to the top of my feet and toes. I can yell you that it truly does help very much. Sometimes you got to think outside the box to find what you need to get relief and praying helps much for direction also. Massaging is the key to releasing all that pain to go away.
