Please help, I am really struggling with my left foot.

Archive date 2026-09-2636 archived replies
More foot health experiences

Please help, I am really struggling with my left foot. I am awaiting a chiropodist appointment but in the meantime I am really suffering and have no idea what it is. In August 2022 I had a swollen toenail with some pus on my big toe (left foot shown here) so had toenail surgery for ingrowing toenail. By now (1.5 years later!) it should have healed but hasn’t really as it seems to have spread to my middle toe and slightly to second toe. You can almost see a ripple of how it has spread on my sole where the skin has peeled away - which doesn’t seem like normal ingrown toenail surgery affect to me? It feels itchy and gets red bumps at times (see third pic where you can see almost a bit of a rash). I have been suffering with this for ages now and just awaiting appointment on nhs but I am so curious as to what it is as I don’t believe ingrown toenail spreads like this and I suspect it’s something more fungal. I really love running but it has definitely impeded my progress sadly. Would appreciate any thoughts or advice.

Community comment#34

I had right big toe pain, top of big toe behind the nail to first joint. Also pain just under the big toe. I had a slew of tests and was never given a diagnosis. I also had discoloration in and around my right foot, to include near the top of my big toe. My doctor finally began treating it like peripheral neuropathy/Vascular disease and prescribed Hydroxychloriquine Sulfate 200mg tablets. Dosing is prescribed based on you. I take a tablet in a.m. and one in p.m. Has nearly eliminated the pain. I could ask to increase dosing, but have not at this point. I had to have my eyes tested to get a baseline. This medication has a small chance for causing long term issues with sight. I was told by optometrist that they find this in smaller stature females, so something to think about and consider before taking. I also am wearing Altra tennis shoes one size bigger than my foot with custom orthotics. Gives me my best comfort. I was in pain for over three years trying to get resolution. I should also add I have a blood disorder that causes clotting, so I am on chronic warfarin. Again, sharing in case you have blood clotting issues. I wish you well and the best of luck.
