OK, my podiatrist wants me to get a walking cast on for a month.

Archive date 2026-10-1127 archived replies
Walking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

OK, my podiatrist wants me to get a walking cast on for a month. I am using a Lacrosse ball, taking a nerve blocker, massaging, vibrator massaging calves and plantar area, but it never goes away. I have had this since April. I am a cyclist and it began when I rode 170 miles in June for AHA fundraiser. It did almost fully go away till July. I hiked to the top of Lassen 2400 ft of climbing and it returned, worse this time. So, have any of you done the 30 day cast and what was the result?
