Bunion calluses on inner big toe: how common?

Archive date 2026-09-174 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot Surgery

Does anybody have pressure blisters/sores/calluses on the inside of their big toe? I have one that is very sore and needs to be shaved down periodically to help with the pain. I'm just wondering how common these are for people with bunions.

I get a thick callous behind my pinky toe. I guess because my gait is off. I use callous pads when it gets painful and that removes them.

Community comment#2

I get them under big toes like you. Had left foot done in April & wear wide toe box shoes now & haven't come back

Community comment#3

I have bunions and have those calluses that you mentioned but have to go about twice a year and get them shaved mine don’t get on big toes just different places on my feet they are very painful until you get them shaved

Community comment#4

I get blisters between my big toe and second toe if I walk more than 20 minutes in a pair of shoes. I use moleskin tape and wrap it around both toes which prevents them.
