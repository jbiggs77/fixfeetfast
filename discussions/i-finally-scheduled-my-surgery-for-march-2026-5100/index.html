Community comment#10

I set up 2 areas one in our family room & one in our living room with a recliner, small table and a few baskets to organize things I need with in reach. A soft blanket, neck pillow for napping, tv remotes, Kleenex, meds, drink cup, etc. My essentials that I used the most were a wedge pillow for both legs for elevation, two soft bendable ice packs, a knee scooter with a basket, a cast cover to keep bandages dry and a shower transfer bench or shower chair. I made as many freezer meals as I could ahead of time and purchased healthy snacks… breakfast bars, protein shakes, yogurt, string cheese, fruit (grab & go stuff). I stocked up on groceries & dog food/treats, paper plates, etc. The 2 different areas were for change of scenery. I got a memory challenge book full of word searches and challenging games, an adult coloring book, and a couple of good books to read. Easy to put on over cast or boot pants & oh’s or night gowns… I’m 68 so crutches weren’t good for me… the knee scooter was much better & a lot safer. If you don’t have a higher toilet you might consider a toilet riser too. Practice ahead of time getting in & out of shower/tub and off toilet if you will be non-weight bearing and build up your arm strength before surgery. Eventually you will need a pair or two of shoes that are a size larger with wide toe box to accommodate swelling for a while once you start to transition back into shoes.
