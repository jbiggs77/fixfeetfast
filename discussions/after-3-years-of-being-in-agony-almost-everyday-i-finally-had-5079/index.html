Community comment#4

I had open release surgery on 1/21. I walked out of the surgery center on my own with the assistance of my husband and a nurse. I came home in a bulky bandage and surgical shoe. My stitches are scheduled to be removed on 2/9. The first 3 days I walked from bed to bathroom and that was it. I have very little pain. I took one prescription pain pill the first day following surgery.. that was it. Pain has been pretty much nonexistent. Very little swelling or bruising. I am now walking still in my surgical shoe all through my house and earlier this week I was able to use the stairs with my husband watching me.
