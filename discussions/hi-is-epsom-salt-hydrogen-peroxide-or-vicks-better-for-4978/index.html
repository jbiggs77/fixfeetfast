Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#21

This has been my routine since November. I soak in white iodine for 1 minute. I put the iodine in 2” glass bowls I purchased on Amazon. (Put the bowls in a disposable casserole pan because they spill). You soak in it twice per day for one week, then once per day for another week, then go to three times a week, then two times a week and then once per week until it’s grown out. Take a nail file and scuff the surface of the nail for better absorption once per week. Dispose of the nail file after each use. I also soak in epsom salt for 20 min per week and clean around and underneath the nails with nail tools. On the non- iodine days I apply Jublia. I’ve had success with this combo.
