For a while I've been wrongly assuming PF was causing all my foot pain.

Archive date 2026-09-2423 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot Surgery

For a while I've been wrongly assuming PF was causing all my foot pain. I've been trying to transition to barefoot and got me some super squishy altras. I have bunions so I've also been wearing correct toes etc. It turns out in addition to pf, post tib, achilles tendonitis, metatarsalgia and whatever else, I have hallux rigidis that's Latin for stiff toe all this crap stems from a stiff freaking toe. Anyway, long story..long the lost flexibility in my big toes can only be corrected by surgery and I need rigid rocker bottom shoes. Everything I was doing to heal pf was aggravating the HR. I'm sharing this because I've done a lot of research and somehow completely missed this possibility despite being aware of and researching bunions.
