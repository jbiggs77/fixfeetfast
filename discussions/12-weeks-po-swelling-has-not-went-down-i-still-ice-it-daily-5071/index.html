12 weeks PO. Swelling has not went down, I still ice it daily. Neuropathy down my entire foot, up to my…

Archive date 2026-10-0330 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryPost-Surgery Swelling & InflammationSurgery Complications & Wound HealingPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

12 weeks PO. Swelling has not went down, I still ice it daily. Neuropathy down my entire foot, up to my though. Mobility and stiffness are still significant issues for me. I’m definitely regretting my decision to have this done to my right foot and I won’t ever have the left one done. I have psoriatic arthritis but my surgeon didn’t view that as a reason for me not to have the surgery. Anyone else have this amount of swelling beyond 12 weeks PO?

PSA is autoimmune, so yo uwill have more swelling and much more scar tissue buildup, that's how AI bodies react to stuff like this. I have to do lots of physical therapy to try to stay ahead of the scar tissue buildup but it's still getting ahead of what my therapist and I can manage. Doc warned me he may have to do an additional surgery to take out the scar tissue after awhile to free the nerve and muscles again to help stiffness.
