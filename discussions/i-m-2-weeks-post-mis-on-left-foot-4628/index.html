Community comment#4

If you still have dressings on - surgeons actually put the dressings in between the big toe and 2nd toe to keep the big toe joint “straight.” The symptoms will improve with dressing change / removal. If you don’t have dressings on - sometimes with a lateral release (small incision by the big toe joint closer to the interspace), can irritate the nerve that innervates the 1st interspace. This will improve with time and massage.
