Wondering is anyone still on this page after a year post-op?

Archive date 2026-09-1120 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryPost-Surgery Swelling & InflammationWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryScarf & Akin Osteotomy

Wondering is anyone still on this page after a year post-op? I “signed out” 6 months ago when I was feeling good and back to normal activities. My bunion surgery ( big toe repositioned with rod and two screws, joint shaved, second toe straightened with rod and screws) was 14 months ago, cleared and discharged from surgical care. Now I’m finding to that my big toe has lifted, doesn’t quite touch the floor when standing . I’m also experiencing my foot swelling again after moderate walking . Is any of this normal? To be expected?
