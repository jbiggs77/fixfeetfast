Thank you for adding me to this group. I suffer from Tarsal Tunnel Syndrome (bi-lateral), and Plantar Fasciitis and am looking for ideas and support on how to manage my foot pain without having surgery. So far, I've tried nerve meds (Gabapentin, Cymbalta) but they had negative effects on my liver/kidneys and did not provide any relief. My doc does have me on pain meds, which I monitor very closely. I've had a few complications wth my left foot - fractured my calcaneous about 15 months ago, had a nasty ingrown big toenail situation, which was removed, and is taking forever to heal. I also have multiple plantar fibromas on both feet. I had my first cortisone shot in my left foot about 2 1/2 weeks ago that provided minimal relief, and this past Thursday, had a cortisone shot in my right foot that seems to have made a tremendous diffeence (about 50% less pain). I've just started wearing Vionic supportive shoes, and had only worn sneakers with ortho inserts prior to that. My doc wants to do Tarsal Tunnel release surgery, and assures me it won't be that bad and that I'd be in a walking boot immediately (after only a couple of days bed rest/elevation). At this point, I'm assuming the Tarsal Tunnel is worse, and that my Plantar Fasciitis is running second, but I'm not sure. I'll be reading all the posts, and will ask a lot of questions on what works/what doesn't to help relieve this awful pain. Thanks again for adding me
