Bunionectomy at 72: managing surgery pain anxiety

Archive date 2026-05-2145 archived replies

Hello my fellow bunion sufferers. My bunionectomy on both sides of my right foot is scheduled for May 5. I am fighting the urge to cancel due to fear of the pain. But that would not be smart as it just keeps getting worse. I am a very active 72yo female with a strong support system. Post-operative care discussed: bandage to remain intact, surgical shoe or walking boot at all times including at home, weight-bearing as tolerated with limited ambulation for the first 2 weeks, elevation and ice.

Best of luck in your decision!!
