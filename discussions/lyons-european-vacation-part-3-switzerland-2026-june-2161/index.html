Lyons European Vacation: Part 3 Switzerland 2026 - June Included: - Speiz (near where we stayed) - interlaken - Ferry tour on Lake Thun checking out the castles - Thun Castle - spectacular sunset from our Airbnb - gondola ride up to Jungfraujoch - Jungfraujoch (top of Europe), including ice castle and tunnels. And kids went snow tubing - Lauterbrunnen - one of the most beautiful towns and surrounding landscapes I’ve ever seen in my life - Zurich - we went on a Lindt tour and had an awesome lunch in their cafe. Then explored old town. Switzerland had some of the most extraordinary views. If you have the opportunity, I highly recommend. We definitely will go back one day. We didn’t have enough time (or energy at that point Then, we crashed. Hard. Still recovering this week. Jason Lyons See less
