I’m frustrated. Doc didn’t tell me 6.5 months ago that the hardware that he had to use on me would need to be…

Archive date 2026-10-2063 archived replies
Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryPost-Surgery Swelling & InflammationPain Management After Foot SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound Healing

I’m frustrated. Doc didn’t tell me 6.5 months ago that the hardware that he had to use on me would need to be removed until recently. Every time I went in to see him he would say “let’s watch it, the hardware might need to come out”. So this entire time I’m trying to heal my foot on my own. I’m very active. But every time I try to walk on it (even a 1/2 a mile in) chronic pain would begin during and afterwards to the point where I can’t walk on my foot. Also, the way I carry myself.. most of my weight goes onto the opposite foot. Now that foot is hurting. An so is my back. I have also torn my LDL on the opposite leg bc of how I’m carrying myself. So I’m scheduled to have the hardware removed from my foot and a tiny piece of bone removed from my large toe bc it’s turning in again at the end of this month. I am also getting a steroid shot in my other foot to help ease the inflammation and pain. He wanted to do it two weeks ago but this my favorite time of the year. As a gardener, golfer, ect. That’s why I scheduled this last fall. My joint had failed over the summer last year and I waited to have the surgery so I could enjoy the rest of my summer. Has anyone else been through this? I just need to be able to use my feet again. I can feel the hardware in every step. Top and bottom of my foot. I went into aquatic therapy/ fitness to be able to work out again and also heal my foot. I highly recommend. It’s helped a lot but unfortunately my foot is trying to reject this hardware. (The dark line in the middle of my foot is from my garden sandals not the hardware) This is my spring/ summer tan for half the year. Does anyone else feel weird about posting foot photos? Someone should be paying for these.

Community comment#7

I had to have the hardware removed from both feet 5 months after my original surgery. I had a total of 1 plate and 6 screws. I also had incision revisions, trapped nerves released, a bone graft and both calves lengthened. Even with all that, it was an absolute breeze compared to the first surgery. I was weight bearing right away in boots. I was back in regular shoes at two weeks after the stitches came out. Allowed back to all regular activities at 6 weeks. I was gutted to have another surgery after 5 horrific months of recovery but am glad I went ahead and did it. I’m 5 years post op now. My feet feel so much better (and healed way faster) without the hardware. You have to do whatever you feel is the best thing for you. I will say the longer the hardware is in, the more it fuses with bone and may potentially make your recovery a bit tougher/more uncomfortable. Hang in there!
