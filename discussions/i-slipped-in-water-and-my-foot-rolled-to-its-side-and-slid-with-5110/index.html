I slipped in water and my foot rolled to its side and slid with all my weight on it.

Archive date 2026-10-066 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot Surgery

I slipped in water and my foot rolled to its side and slid with all my weight on it. I'm not small by any means so that was a lot of pressure. I instantly felt like there was glass stabbing my foot. 30 minutes later I could barely walk on it. That night I took my shoes and socks off and walked 3 steps and felt things moving around in my foot then it popped 3 times. Since then it's been constant pain. I've been up the past 3 hours and I've ran out of things to take. I'm hoping the podiatrist can see me when they open. Anything I can do in the mean time? I have no idea what I've done.

Community comment#1

RICE - Rest (stay off it as much as possible), Ice (frequently), Compression (use as elastic wrap around the foot/ankle), Elevation (prop up on a couple of pillows). Sounds like you need to be seen ASAP.
