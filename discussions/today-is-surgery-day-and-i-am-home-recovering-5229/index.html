Today is surgery day and I am home recovering.

Archive date 2026-10-1411 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryMinimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Pain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryScarf & Akin Osteotomy

Today is surgery day and I am home recovering. I had a Metatarsus adductus correction with fusion TMTJ 1st, 2nd and 3rd - AND with MIS soft tissue release lateral first second third MPJs, possible Weil osteotomies if needed. Bunion correction with akin osteotomy hallux and medial resection of bone first metataras all right foot. But what she actually did I really don’t know till I see her next Thursday. So glad it is done! So far pain is manageable and my toes are straight! I will be no bearing weight for two months but have a knee scooter and wheelchair plus crutches which I am horrible on. Everyone at the surgery center was wonderful! Again my right foot was done and hopefully my left will be done later this year. I will post more pictures and progress as I continue. The worst part was the anxiety before the surgery. My husband and I planned way ahead of time and practiced how I would manage in the car coming home, how to get to the toilet, what bed-room would be the best, and what items actually needed. I planned on meals ahead of time and my church is bring meals for 3 weeks, 3 times each week. I feel all went as well as it could and couldn’t believe the support I received. Usually I am the care giver and the cook and the one who does everything. I am soon to be 69 next month with 3 grown adult children and have 15 grandchildren. The oldest being 19 and the youngest being 4 months. So now I guess I get a turn to be taken care of. Many blessings wings and happy healing!
