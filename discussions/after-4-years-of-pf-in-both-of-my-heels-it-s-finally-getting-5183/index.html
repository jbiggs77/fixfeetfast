After 4 years of PF in both of my heels, it’s FINALLY getting much better!

Archive date 2026-10-1117 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

After 4 years of PF in both of my heels, it’s FINALLY getting much better! I have been going to PT off and on the whole time and honestly, stretching my calves, Graston (massaging my soleus and anterior tibs.) and changing my shoes have been the most help! I switched to Keen tennis shoes from Brooks and I can’t believe the difference! It was already beginning to get better, but now I can walk my dogs for an hour and it just starts to hurt near the end of the walk. A month ago, I could only tolerate half an hour of walking. I have also lost 22lbs in the last 9 months or so with help of GLP1’s. I think that has helped too. Don’t give up! I wonder if it’s just a matter of finding the right combinations of things that personally help and it has to also run its course. Those shoes though! Huge help!
