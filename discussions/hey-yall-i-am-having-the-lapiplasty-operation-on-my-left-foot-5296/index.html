Hey yall! I am having the lapiplasty operation on my left foot this June! I had the Austin procedure done to…

Archive date 2026-10-174 archived replies
Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

Hey yall! I am having the lapiplasty operation on my left foot this June! I had the Austin procedure done to my right foot in 2022. It was a long hard recovery. But the pain was manageable. Just took a long time for my foot to look normal nevertheless, it changed my life for the better! And it’s time to do the left! I am in pain all day everyday! I am wondering if anyone has had the two separate procedures on their feet? Or any insight at all? I’m 21 and have a very active/social lifestyle so I’m worried it’s going to slow me down.

I've just had the Austin procedure on my left foot & will then need right one doing but it will be the same procedure again. Had them done 21 years ago but they just shaved some off the bone so they came back. How is your right foot now? How long before you walked normally again?

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#2

I used a scooter for the first 2 weeks. I could put weigh on it to walk around the house after about 3 days. But I was in high school at the time so I needed the scooter to get around. I was in a boot for 5 weeks. Then just wore tennis as much as possible. It took 9 months for my foot to look normal again. So worth it though.

Community comment#3

I had the lapiplasty done 7 months ago. The boot was off in two weeks and I wore the Orta shoe for about a month. Seem like very little progress after about 4 months. The nerve pain attacks were terrible at times. Not as severe or frequent now but I still have them. Foot still nimb as he'll. I walk 3 or 4 miles a day with no problem.except my stride is off. Overall this has been the worst experience of my life. I think it will take at least a year before things are normal. Allot of nerve damage and this was a severe bunion. Being 75 years old probably didn't help.

Community comment#4

Your social life will need to take a break for a bit. I am three weeks out and still not weight bearing. I am 61 so a bit older than you but had a very active life before surgery. I went to a talk last night and was ready to get home after it. My husband drove. Take it easy and do what the surgeon tells you.
