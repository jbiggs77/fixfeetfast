Something strange just happened and I’m not sure how to describe it - I’m a just over a year past surgery and…

Archive date 2026-09-1914 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot Surgery

Something strange just happened and I’m not sure how to describe it - I’m a just over a year past surgery and have been healing fine - I’m back to running and everything. My big toes are still partially numb and tonight as I was laying in bed one of my big toes started tensing, kind of like a cramp? The toe was raising and I couldn’t fully control it, I had to use my hand to flatten it, and it still kept trying to raise again, almost like it was possessed it didn’t hurt but felt like if I didn’t stop it, it would hurt. Has anyone had anything like that happen? My only recent change is new orthotics and wearing shoes more as a way to manage ankle arthritis, but those things have been very positive, so I don’t think they’re related. Has anyone experienced anything like this?

Your toe and whole foot have a different alignment now that you've had surgery. You should be able to use your toe more effectively in walking and running. If the toe can press into the ground now in the correct direction and wasn't able to before, that may give you more ability to strengthen your arch and foot overall. You might want to consult with a PT. Make sure you get one who is knowledgeable about feet.
