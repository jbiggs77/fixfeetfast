I am 8wks post-op.

Archive date 2026-09-1412 archived replies
Post-Surgery Swelling & InflammationBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryScarf & Akin Osteotomy

I am 8wks post-op. Surgery date: June 24th Procedure: Traditional (Scarf/Reese) Surgeon: Podiatrist Yesterday during my pt session, my therapist and I discussed my overall progress and we both agreed that I can go ahead and discontinue her services at this time. I will continue performing the dorsiflexion exercise with end range holds of 30-45 seconds 2-3x daily with primary focus being decreasing the minimal stiffness that I have in my toe. I will also continue massaging the base of my toe due to a small pocket of stubborn swelling. That’s the only swelling I have!The bone graft continues to heal well and I don’t have any pain. Not even after wearing or walking in my shoes all day. My surgeon has cleared me to return to work next week (August 27th) without restrictions!! I hope you all have a blessed day.
