I’m 1 month post-op (33 days). Although I’ve been waiting for the dead skin to start shedding, I guess I wasn’t expecting it be this much. Especially since it wasn’t there last night!! I moisturize every morning w/coconut oil and at night w/vaseline. **NOT directly on my incision site, though, because it isn’t scab-free yet.** Then I apply my silicone socks for about 15 minutes before bed. Lynn Miller Prinz I am 5 weeks post op. The itching on the top of my foot and around my ankle is driving me crazy. I dont think it is dry skin as I've used lotion or Vaseline every day since my bandage was removed. I think it is nerves. Anybody else experience this? Thinking about asking doctor for gabapentin.
