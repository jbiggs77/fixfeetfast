Hi all .... I am having tailor's bunion surgery on 31st. Two small screws, shavong a bone spur, but no other procedures along with this. I have been religiously reading all your posts to be ready for anything. Thank you so much for sharing your experiences! My question involves future travel: - 3.5 months post op I am planning to drive to a beach vacation. I will be the only driver, and will probably try to do at least 8 hrs the 1st day and 5 the next. Is this possible? -6 mos i am planning to get on a plane for a 3 hour flight. There will be a lot of airport walking. Is this possible? -8 mos i have a planned trip to Italy with my sisters. Really worried about this trip but the doctor said it would be no problem that far out from the surgery. My question is, what has been anyone's experience with something like this? If I had to, I could cancel the first two trips but Italy is paid for. Thx for your help!!

Community comment#1

I am 2.5 months out, if I had to drive 8hrs now I could definitely do it but I would want to take 1 or 2 half hour breaks to elevate and ice that foot. My surgery was on my left foot. I am currently walking between 8000 and 10000 steps a day but not all at once, I do 3 30-40 min walks after every meal. Good luck with your surgery!!

Community comment#2

You will be fine for all of the above. I had tailors bunion surgery on Sept. 19, 2025. Did a 2 hour drive each way solo in November. Flew on December 4 and back Dec. 21 with no issues. Flew again yesterday. Going on a cruise next week. Just make sure you get shoes that fit your new foot!

Community comment#3

The drive will be tough. You're going to need to elevate your foot periodically. Plan for more stops than you normally would. You'll be fine for the airport 6 months out

Community comment#4

This is the same surgery I had but no bone spur. It was my right foot. At 16 weeks I was using my left foot to drive. I couldn't use my right foot to drive because it was too hard and not strong enough and a bit painful to press the brake hard enough to stop. I was also still not even walking long distances without pain. I was 8 weeks NWB and went back to work at 10 weeks for reference.

Community comment#5

I had tailors bunion surgery. I have one screw. I’m 13 weeks. I think you will be able to do those things. Mine was on my left foot but my recovery has really sped up after 12 weeks. I plan to be running by 3.5 months. I would recommend compression socks
