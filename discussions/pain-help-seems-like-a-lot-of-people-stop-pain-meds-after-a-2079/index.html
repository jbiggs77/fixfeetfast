Nerve pain after minimally invasive bunion surgery

Archive date 2026-06-1910 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Pain Management After Foot SurgeryScarf & Akin Osteotomy

Pain help! Seems like a lot of people stop pain meds after a few days, I had a pain pump that last 3.5 days after surgery. Now I feel hardly any pain where the "cutting" of the bone was, but man is the nerve pain/prickling/blood rushing so painful! It feels like my foot is burning any time I get up to just use the restroom and stays burning for like 45 min after laying down. For that alone I need pain meds and I'm day 6 post op and thinking I need to ask for a refill. I still take ibuprofen between and no, ibuprofen alone doesn't help. Did yall feel the same? Maybe I have a low pain tolerance. See less
