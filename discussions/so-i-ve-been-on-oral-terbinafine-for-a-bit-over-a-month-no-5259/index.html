So I’ve been on oral terbinafine for a bit over a month (no side effects, I was nervous).

Archive date 2026-10-1524 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound HealingToenail Fungus Treatment

So I’ve been on oral terbinafine for a bit over a month (no side effects, I was nervous). My big toenails are just slightly yellow at the tips in the corners but I’ve had the infection for a long time, I just use lots of topicals to keep it at bay. So they aren’t a very extreme case. I want to try for a last baby once I’m done with the three month course of medicine, which means I really need to make sure the medicine works. I don’t want to pass this to my kids and I’m ready to not be constantly thinking about my nails I have lots of time for cleaning and I’m making a list of what I’ve done. If there is anything else let me know. -Today I’m having my carpets professionally cleaned. I’m going to do it again in two months once my treatment is done, I’m just drying to reduce the amount of fungus in my house. -threw away all my old shoes and socks. I have new pairs waiting for me when I’m done with my treatment, for now I’m just gonna wear socks and slides and spray them after each use -Bleached my open toed slippers and also I bought a UV shoe sanitizer so I did that. I’ll walk around in only those at home to let my nails breathe but not walk on the carpet directly, and keep using the uv sanitizer on them daily -washing all my kids shoes and using the UV light on those -applying terbinafine topically to everyone’s feet twice daily for the next two months, my kids have some peeling skin on their toes that isn’t itchy and isn’t red but just in case -I mop with hypochlorous acid and always have, that should kill fungus, now I’m mopping every three days instead of weekly -bleaching the tub floor after every use -vacuuming frequently -Plus in general keeping my nails clean. I’ve done vinegar/mouthwash soaks, I spray iodine or hypochlorous acid on my nails, keep my feet scrubbed and sanitize nail tools. Now I’m just doing urea, topical terbinafine, and sometimes Vicks on my nails. -I turned up my water heater so washing on hot can kill fungus better -all our linens and towels and socks and my couch covers are white so I bleach them -I’m washing things like anywhere chair covers and curtains that get washed infrequently this month too -we also have air purifiers in every room (not because of this, because my youngest has bad lungs) and that can help filter out any fungal spores vacuuming kicks up, and we also air out our house frequently too. I know after the medicine people commonly get reinfected from their stuff so I’m trying to avoid that and really get this gone once and for all I looked up tips for ringworm and athletes foot since it’s all the same type of thing.

Fungus is everywhere, but it doesn’t mean people automatically get an infection when they’re exposed to it. I’ve never passed it to anyone in my family. It seems to be somewhat genetic if you’re more susceptible, and it’s also opportunistic. So if you’re more susceptible, and have a pedicure, for example, is what is more risky. I did the oral Terbinafine and cured mine (and athletes foot). The only extra cleaning protocol is I bought new socks and new shoes and/or disinfected my shoes. Something I noticed with mine, is it started to return as soon as I finished the medication, but this topical kept it away. I applied this daily for months afterwards. I was only prescribed it because even though the appearance of my athletes foot went away, it still itched. I noticed it worked on my toenails too, so I just kept applying it. Maybe see if you can get this prescribed too (it was cheap, like $8 copay?)
