I think I finally understand what Cinderella felt like when she slid her foot into that glass slipper...

Archive date 2026-10-220 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryMinimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Post-Surgery Swelling & InflammationWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

I think I finally understand what Cinderella felt like when she slid her foot into that glass slipper... I'm sitting at around 25 days post op...MIS bunion surgery. Before travelling on my summer holidays I purchased a pair of wide toe box sandals with arch support because my feet are so flat ancestry. com called me to see if I was related to Daffy Duck. But with the swelling and healing scars, I just couldn't wear my sandals, so it was back to the loving arms of my orthopedic shoe. My orthopedic shoe has been my best investment; it may not look pretty but it truly deserves the summer 2026 edition of Vogue. I tried on my sandals before my holiday and my right foot screamed no. I tried it on again yesterday and it would seem the "tightness" has subsided a little? So I road tested the sandals on both feet and things felt...pretty ok...not as perfect as your smile but good enough to walk a mile or two on a daily basis. I have walked a slow two miles a day (walking back and forth to the beach) I can comfortably bodyweight squat, continue my seated workouts and some adapted yoga...even though I have as much flexibility as a concrete brick. My right foot still doesn't have full range of motion and the whole process at times feels as slow and my dial up internet connection in the late 90's but I'm sure progress is happening. Look after yourselves my friends.
