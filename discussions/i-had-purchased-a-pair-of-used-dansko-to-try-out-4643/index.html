I had purchased a pair of used Dansko to try out.

Archive date 2026-09-1512 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot Surgery

I had purchased a pair of used Dansko to try out. I wore them for the first time Friday to try them out for” a while” ended up having to go out of town unexpectedly and wore them for 5 hours. Got back home expecting to be in pain but amazingly my feet felt great! I also bought a pair of hoka for my work shoes ( I’m a nurse) they also have worked out great for me! I’m so happy to have a few more options other than my usual Birkenstocks sandals ( still wear them daily) but sometimes want something more dressy and obviously need proper shoes for my work lol . So excited!!
