Hello fellow PF sufferers!

Archive date 2026-10-1130 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryFlat Feet & Arch SupportPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Hello fellow PF sufferers! I thought I would give an update and my shoe recommendations for anyone that might be interested! I have had PF for 3 years, have been down the same road that most everyone goes down... saw a podiatrist, got custom orthotics, had steroid shots, PT, and finally surgery. I had open plantar faciotomy in March. I am not pain free yet but I guess I could say I'm about 75% better. (For other context, I'm 64 and a bit overweight) I have tried so many different shoes and I want to share what works for me! Vionics Recovery Slides - These have been my "house shoes" since I was diagnosed. They feel so good in the arch, I wear them with socks mostly. Birkenstocks - I have many different styles and they all feel good! Pictured are the ones I have walked miles and miles in, they feel better than gym shoes to me for the most part. Thinking I need to try their clogs to wear in the winter with socks. Oofos - I got these fairly recently and wore them to the airport/on a trip and on my regular 2-3 mile walks. They feel good! I wore Brooks for many years but have decided the toe box is just too narrow. I used to be an 8 or size up to 8.5 but now I am 8.5 size up to 9. I got a size 9 in these and they feel great! Taos- I also got these recently in part for "hiking" (nothing hardcore) and they worked well, walked/hiked about 8 miles one day in Rocky Mountain National Park! They are sturdy and have good arch support. Again I sized up to a 9. Other notes: I tried on a pair of Kurus and I did not like the way they cup your heel. Might work for some but not me. Also I have 2 pairs of Altras and they are not good for me either, despite the wide toe box they just don't feel good. Hope this will help someone and I welcome thoughts/comments.
