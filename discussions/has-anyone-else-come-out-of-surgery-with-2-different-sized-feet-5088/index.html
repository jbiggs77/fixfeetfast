Has anyone else come out of surgery with 2 different sized feet?

Archive date 2026-10-065 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot Surgery

Has anyone else come out of surgery with 2 different sized feet? Before surgery, my right foot was a size 10 and my left foot was an 8. After 2 failed bunion surgeries and a 3rd reconstructive foot surgery, my right foot is now a size 11.5 and my left is still a size 8. Finding shoes is a NIGHTMARE. By the time my right foot fits, the left is so big. It’s just flopping around like a fish. I can no longer wear most shoes, including any boots (even the Ugg type boots). I tried 2 different sizes of shoes, but it threw my balance off and I never was able to work past it. If anyone has dealt with this, what fixes did you find?

Community comment#1

Before surgery my left foot was 1/2 inch longer which was a problem bc I have very narrow feet and the left big toe hit the end of the shoe. If I went longer, too wide. My surgeon said the burr usually removes a few mm of toe length, but he could squeeze in a couple more. 4 mm made a big difference for me and now my feet are identical and shoes fit better. It’s something to think about when you discuss w surgeon. I always hated that long big toe.

Community comment#2

Have you tried split sizing, maybe Nordstroms or asking at your local running store (https://www.abilityhacker.com/where-to-buy-shoes-when.../)

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#4

oh man I’m so sorry! Have you also tried PT? It might not just be shoe size but also muscle strength & flexibility differences? My operated foot was only slightly different in size but drastically different in strength for awhile and that made my balance worse. But is better now!

Community comment#5

Did you try orthotics for the smaller foot, so if you bought a pair of shoes, the orthotic would take up the room of the smaller foot to match the bigger foot? I’m sure you’ve tried a lot to figure this out already.
