hi it's the girl with 6 toes again lol.

Archive date 2026-10-1412 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryMinimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)

hi it's the girl with 6 toes again lol. tomorrow marks 3 weeks since my austin bunionectomy on both feet! overall i feel i have had a pretty good experience recovery wise. for sure have my up and down moments. my right foot definitely has been hurting me more since day two compared to my left foot, but doctor says nothing to be worried about. i just wanted to post my results so far!obviously have a long way to go still. i am pretty dang happy with them. a little part of me hates the "little bump" i seem to still have, but i cant even complain. i am so happy i got this procedure done and hope the rest of my recovery goes just as smoothly. doctor told me this tuesday when i got stitches out to start bending the big toes a little everyday. i understand because theyre sooo stiff right now but ouch just curious what you guys think? how do they look? and any advice or tips on the rest of recovery! thank you so much!!
