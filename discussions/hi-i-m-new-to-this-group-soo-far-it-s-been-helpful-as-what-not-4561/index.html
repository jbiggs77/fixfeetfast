Hi I’m new to this group soo far it’s been helpful as what not to do and what to try I’ve been suffering with pf for only bout 2 months I’ve had on going feet pain and swelling for bout 5 yrs since pf has arrived I’ve had no swelling in my feet or ankles I work 12 hour days sooo I kno it’s not helping .. I hav had brooks for bout a yr and they’ve been great til now I ordered bronair shoes ther supposed to be good for pf idk if I didn’t try them long enough I felt no relief I decided to to try my old sketcher crocs and so far they are tolerable I’ve tried numerous sleeves to sleeping in them to wearing them all day I have mama bear neuropathy cream eases the pain roll on pf ointment eases it a lil more voltran cream works the best . As of 2 days ago I invested in kropez sleeves for both my feet one is worse than other and in morning I hav minimal discomfort I can get up without holding the wall by time I reach bathroom im doing better .. I hav wore them for 8 hours the past couple of days i read up on these sleeves for weeks before I bought them they wer costly all reviews wer good and promising and so far correct .. good luck to all of you on this journey its been rough and im early in this i dont drive soo walking is my life .. im optimistic and hopeful it will end sooner than later .. not giving up here in Wisconsin
