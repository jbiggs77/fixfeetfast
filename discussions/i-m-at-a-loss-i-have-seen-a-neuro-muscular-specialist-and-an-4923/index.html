It gets worse as time Goes on first it was the right foot ( the big toe also drifted to the second in a matter of a month then ) they all seem to be super bendy and the joints looking strange … the spread out when I go on toes or Foot to Maintain my balance .. I’m so worried something is terribly Wrong !im only 45 most wth these symptoms seem to be 70 or older . I may have Eds or heds I am hyper mobile . I see rhematology in February. In September 2024 I had bunion surgery on my left foot and planned to have it on my right foot later. My bunion surgery turned out great. However on the 7th week after surgery, I could tell my toes were all trying to turn towards my big toe. When I ask my doctor about it, he just said he really didn't see it and he thought they would be fine. Two weeks later he agreed. He decided to fuse my second toe and put pins in my third and fourth toes, which is what he did. Three days after this surgery, he removed my bandage and my third toe was completely black so he immediately removed the pin from that toe. A few weeks later he removed the pin from my third toe but my toe was still not straight. Within the next few weeks he decided to remove the fourth toe which was black (dead) due to circulation problems. He then informed me there was nothing else he could do and said for me to call him if I needed him. Now since I don't have a third toe, my fourth toe has moved over towards my second toe and goes just enough under the edge of it for it to make a blister on it. Now I don't know where to turn to get the other foot done that I trust and I still have pain every day.
