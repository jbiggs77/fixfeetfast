Bunion surgery with neuroma: surgery timing and pressure

Archive date 2026-09-255 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot Surgery

Hey! My feet are a bundle of problems. I have severe bunions for my age plus Morton’s Neuroma. Both deformities are in both feet! The doctor has said I can have surgery for the bunions (woo) In 8-12 weeks. With the Morton’s neruma, my journey so far has been 2 lots of steroid injections which have been innefective and so and I am booked in for radio frequency as the next course of action. I am waiting on that appointment letter. A horrible thought I had last night was when I get surgury for the bunions, my feet are going to be wrapped up tight in bandages. What if they are too tight and press on that nerve for the neuromas? The pain is triggered now when I wear shoes only slightly narrow. So bandaged up all cramped with no space to breath, the thought of it makes me cringe. Has anyone please experienced this?

I had neuromas. The cortisone shots didn't help. I got custom orthotics and mostly wear birks, Uggs and tennis shoes. I switched off pickleball for hiking boots which is when the neuromas hurt. I don't think I have noticed them in five years. Surgery is iffy and the nerve can pop out in another spot. Researh in the medical journals about surgery for neuromas. I didn't like the odds.
