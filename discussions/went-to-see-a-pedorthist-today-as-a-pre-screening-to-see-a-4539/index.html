Went to see a pedorthist today as a pre-screening to see a surgeon ie I guess they are trying to cull out…

Archive date 2026-09-1214 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

Went to see a pedorthist today as a pre-screening to see a surgeon ie I guess they are trying to cull out those on their list who are not surgical candidates? She examined my feet, analyzed my foot function and gait and said basically my feet are not functioning the way they should which is not only causing pain and degenerative changes, but also affect areas higher up eg hips, knees. Well I knew that already. She said I am a surgical candidate but in the meantime or alternatively I should consider orthotics and physio to help better manage and maintain my foot function. Here’s the thing. Ideally, I should do both. Financally, its gonna be a struggle. So I am considering just doing one. My brain says do the physio to get my feet more “fit”. But if I still walk on the same base will it be an exercise in futility (no pun intended). I would be interested in hearing from anyone who has gone the path of orthotics and/or physio or medical professionals as to what my best choice would be. TIA.

Custom orthodics, made specifically for my feet, helped a lot for 30 years and wearing the widest width of shoes possible, for 30 years. I did physical therapy after surgery for three months because I had a few complications. I am doing great now. I have zero pain in my foot now. I do have a plate and screws in my foot but haven't had any issues with them yet. I don't expect to have any but some people do. Best to you in whatever you decide to do.
