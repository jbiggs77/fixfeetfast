My MIB/Akin osteotomy failed to close properly.

Archive date 2026-10-211 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Pain Management After Foot SurgeryScarf & Akin Osteotomy

My MIB/Akin osteotomy failed to close properly. It looked like it was healing on the surface , but there was a hole underneath it. They had to go in after a month and clean it out. They put in a collagen implant, a scaffolding for bones and tissue to grow into. They did cultures of the tissues and the bone and put me in the hospital for days on IV antibiotics. Nothing grew on the cultures, so it looks like I'm home free. I probably got four days of antibiotics that I didn't need , but it's better to be safe than sorry. The healing seemed to be textbook for a month. I had very little pain, and was only taking Tylenol. But I had a weird feeling about it and i'm glad I trusted my intuition. I have neuropathy and don't feel pain normally. ChatGPT pointed that out when I asked questions about the sore. Does anybody have experience with collagen grafts?
