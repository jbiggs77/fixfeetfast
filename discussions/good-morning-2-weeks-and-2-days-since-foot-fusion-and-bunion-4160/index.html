Good morning, 2 weeks and 2 days since foot fusion and bunion surgery. I’m walking in a boot from heel to toe , stitches are out and in zero pain and zero swelling. Life is good. I have full flexibility in my ankle and can wiggle my toes no problem and can take a shower . My surgeon is amazing !!! Was very impressed on how well I healed .
