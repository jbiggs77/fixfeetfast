Hello all! I’m about 2 weeks out from a lapidus bunionectomy (11/20) and am trying to prepare myself for going back to work after the first of the year. I work as a psychiatric nurse practitioner in an outpatient office setting. First pic is day of surgery, second pic is post surgical x-rays, and third pic is my foot yesterday. Swelling and pain are down significantly but I unfortunately did have a skin reaction to either the surgical scrub, or the adhesive they used during surgery. (Redness isn’t infection) I was thinking of getting some compression socks for later on in the healing process but I was hoping to hear from others things that have helped them!

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#11

socks because they can be unnecessarily tight, potentially cutting off circulation (especially if ill-fitting), causing skin irritation, or masking underlying issues, with most benefits occurring during the day when you're upright against gravity; however, a doctor might recommend it for specific conditions like severe venous issues, requiring mild compression and careful monitoring. Taking them off at night allows skin to breathe and prevents dependency, making it better to give your legs a break unless advised otherwise.
