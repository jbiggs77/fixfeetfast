I am getting ready to have bunion surgery in June and I am so nervous, I have neuropathy in my feet from…

Archive date 2026-10-0317 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryPain Management After Foot Surgery

I am getting ready to have bunion surgery in June and I am so nervous, I have neuropathy in my feet from taking chemotherapy 15 yrs ago . I am afraid the surgery is going to make my neuropathy worse. I already take 400mg of Lyrica a day for the pain. I want be able to take anything else if it makes the neuropathy worse. Has anyone else had a problem like this. The doctor says it should not be a problem because I no longer take chemotherapy and that he will be moving the nerves over while working on my bunion.

My neurologist put me on 600 units of Alpha Lapoic Acid per day. I had neuropathy from chemo and within 2 weeks…huge difference. It’s an OTC enzyme so it won’t clash with any other meds.
