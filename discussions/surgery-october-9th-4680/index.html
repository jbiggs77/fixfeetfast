Community comment#3

Knee scooter, crutches, walker. Get all the things. I couldn’t maneuver the crutches at first because I was too high and in too much pain so the walker really helped. I also highly recommend the ice cooler because the last thing you’ll want to do is get up for ice. The water stays cool in the cooler for like 12 hours and you can wrap it around your leg and then you just pull the hose off to get up and plug it back in when you’re ready and you’re still wrapped. The plug is just against your leg. Seriously. Your foot looks bad. Mine was the same configuration but a bit less. And it was painful for a long time. I just want you to be prepared. Have food and drink by your bed since you’re alone. Have everything by your bed. Your bowel will be a thing as your pain pills stop you up so make sure you have stool softeners. Good luck to you and try to get someone to be with you for the first 2 days at least.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#32

the problem you’re asking about comes from our need to try to resolve the emotions nagging at us while also trying to trust and believe the surgeon with their more factual information. Heart and head conflict, not to mention terror, dread, hopefulness and the desire to stop hurting while getting back some of our normal past function. Many of us would not do it if we’d known how our experience would turn out. Many sailed through and are very pleased to of had foot surgery. I have no answers to give as all of mine failed. I will tell you that after 3 surgeries and nearly 6 months of a knee scooter, the other very helpful tools were 1. a stool with wheels. My hallways are very short, the knee scooter wouldn’t have fit or turned around. I would carefully transfer to the stool then roll backwards into the restroom. 2. An ice machine, 6 frozen water bottles, a voice activated plug and a cardboard box lined with something to muffle sound. You’ll plug your machine into the voice activated plug. You’ll put the frozen bottles of water in the machine along with water. When you wake up in the night simply tell the plug to turn on. I had the wrap for the machine already on my foot. This allowed me to never get up to get ice for the swelling. Also I put the box over the machine so I didn’t need to listen to it. Best of luck.
