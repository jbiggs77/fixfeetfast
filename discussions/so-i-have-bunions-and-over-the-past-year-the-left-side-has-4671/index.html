So I have bunions, and over the past year the left side has gotten noticeably worse.

Archive date 2026-09-1719 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Pain Management After Foot Surgery

So I have bunions, and over the past year the left side has gotten noticeably worse. I have little to no pain still. My feet do ache after being on them awhile but I also have very low arches so not sure what is contributing to that more. My question is, is it better to have surgery that is minimally invasive before things get worse? Or better to wait until there is pain? I don’t like the idea of elective surgery really but I know this will only worsen. I’m in my late 40s if that matters. Thanks!

I went ahead and did it for my left foot before it was super painful. I’m 51, a runner and did not have pain either. My discomfort was from the friction of the bunion rubbing in certain shoes. I knew it was only going to get worse so decided to do it while I was still active and young-ish 🤗. Here’s my before/after. I had 3 pinhole incisions and no metal hardware. Was back to full activity by 10 weeks post op.

Community comment#3

Similar situation, except my right foot started with little to no pain and while I've had bunions for close to 20 years, the pain got worse and worse within the last 5 years- I'm 39. I only have mild to moderate bunions. I ended up getting MIS 3.5 months ago, and I have my other foot (no pain) in 2 weeks. I've read horror stories on this group about severe cases with highly complex surgeries and I personally decided to take care of it sooner rather than later. I spent a lot of time researching surgeons as well. While I'm not 100% healed of course, I already am so happy I did it
