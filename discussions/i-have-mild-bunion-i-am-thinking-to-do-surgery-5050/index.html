Community comment#4

I have a good story. I was a long distance runner prior to surgery. I had a chevron procedure on my right foot in December, and just ran in a Ragnar race this past weekend. My legs were 3-4 miles, so not long, but I can run. I’m up to 5 miles again when running single runs. It is true that it’s a different pain/discomfort than before surgery, however I don’t have the nerve pain in my toe that I had, and it’s not sharp…I just know I had something done recently. I do have quite a bit of swelling after running, but he said I could pop back in for a steroid shot anytime to help alleviate the swelling that is constantly there. You’ll hear the horror stories on here, but I’m super happy to have done it.
