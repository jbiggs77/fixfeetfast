Community comment#5

I had a double MIS in January. A screw was in the wrong place from day one and oh could I feel it. It came out of the bone by about a third of the size of the screw. Yesterday I had it removed. Procedure awake. I can't tell you how it's gone yet but will hopefully know in a few weeks. I am hyper mobile and they knew this before surgery. Both of my big toes are already turning inwards. I have Informed one of the medics that much more needs to be done pre operation. We get told oh things can go wrong but like with all operations there are risks. Not like 70% need redoing. Which I was told just as I was being taken into theatre. I think there is a need for progress ref the operations and information pre operation. This is not a message to scare people. But do ask the questions. Do research. But most importantly post operation be patient. Take care, do as your told. And it does take many months for the feet to recover. Good luck.
