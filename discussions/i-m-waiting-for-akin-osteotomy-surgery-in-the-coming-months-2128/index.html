Community comment#1

Mine peeled off after the swelling went down. I don't know if that is normal or not. It hasn't come back as my gait is different. Plus I am wearing shoes with a wide toe box. My surgeon didn't take it off. I should have asked him to and he probably would have. Maybe ask your surgeon if that can be done. Best to you!

Community comment#2

I haven’t had surgery, but here to say my calluses have changed (are lessened) since I started wearing wide toe box Altra Running Shoes as my daily footwear. I think the toe spread has helped. It’s likely that if you work on your gait at anytime (which will help prevent bunion recurrence after surgery too) and wear footwear with toe splay space— then calluses will probably change and hopefully be reduced. I had the callus under the ball of my foot described in the Gait Happens video linked (this callus is now essentially gone with wearing wide toe box footwear!) and also a “pinch callus” on the side of my big toe.
