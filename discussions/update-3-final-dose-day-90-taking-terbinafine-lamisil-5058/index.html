Update #3. Final dose, day 90, taking Terbinafine (Lamisil). Very obvious improvement from taking it! Now I’m…

Archive date 2026-10-0328 archived replies
Toenail Fungus Treatment

Update #3. Final dose, day 90, taking Terbinafine (Lamisil). Very obvious improvement from taking it! Now I’m just hoping it continues to grow out since I’m done. What were others’ experiences once finished? My step-dad did the same protocol and his continued to grow out. I think it’s been close to 10 years fungus free for him now. I’m a little nervous because my athlete’s foot still itches I think? You can’t see it all, so I don’t know what that’s about or what that means. I was prescribed different topicals along with the oral medication the last two months, but as usual, those don’t seem to do anything. The itching sensation feels crawly, like sweat dripping across your skin. So I don’t know if that sounds like athletes foot or something else. Athlete’s foot is supposed to be easier to treat, so I found that confusing.
