Do most bunion sufferers also have over pronation? I’ve found having “stability” running shoes helps with guiding how I land and how I weight bear which helps minimize the worsening of my bunion. Most running shoe companies make a “stability” running shoe, and Saucony makes a stability runner in the wide option too.
