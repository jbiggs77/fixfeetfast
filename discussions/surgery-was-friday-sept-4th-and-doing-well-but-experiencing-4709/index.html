Surgery was Friday, Sept 4th and doing well but experiencing arch pain but no stitches there.

Archive date 2026-09-144 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryFlat Feet & Arch Support

Surgery was Friday, Sept 4th and doing well but experiencing arch pain but no stitches there. Anyone else dealing with the same pain? Esp big toe seems to jerk at the end of the day. Also I saw surgeon on Tuesday as a quick follow up and he added about an inch of gauze between big toe and the next one to keep them separated as he also had to put in a plate along with screws. While I am full weight bearing I am having trouble forcing myself to the couch. I go back to desk job on Monday (work from home) and trying to tell myself to rest until then. Anyone else struggling to rest ? Edited to add… yes I am unable to relax and anxiety has been horrendous with this surgery. I know I need to learn. Any way, day 8 and feeling great. Almost 6k steps today and some pain now after being out and about in the boot. Big toe is a little tired.
