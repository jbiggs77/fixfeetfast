Getting MIS surgery 1/12.

Archive date 2026-10-0816 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryScarf & Akin Osteotomy

Getting MIS surgery 1/12. She told me I could return to work starting at 6 weeks (end of February). Then she filled out the FMLA paperwork and said I wouldn't be going back until April. Is she just being cautious? I only have 30 sick days. She said, "You will be weightbearing as tolerated after the surgery (based on your pain) and transition into regular shoes as soon as symptoms allow (may be 4-6+ weeks). For the first week or so you will be elevating a lot (50 minutes out of the hour if possible), but you can progress the amount of time your foot is down based on your symptoms. In your experience, when did you go back to work? I work in fifth grade at a school and will have use of the elevator and can sit periodically throughout the day. I had major ankle surgery (trimalleolar fracture- broke all 3 ankle bones) so I am well aware of how long healing can take, but I can't imagine this being anywhere as bad as that surgery (I was out for 12 weeks for that ankle surgery, same amount of time she is quoting on FMLA paperwork for bunion)
