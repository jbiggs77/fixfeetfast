Community comment#5

Try liquid bandaid. Cut/file all the damage nail. Then coat heavily with liquid bandaid. Make sure all exposed nail is covered completely. Keep it on there and see if it will grow out normal. I’ve tried everything but oral meds the last 4 years and this is the only thing that worked. I got an electric file and ground all the white damaged nail away. Then coated with liquid bandaid. My nail is growing out healthy!!
