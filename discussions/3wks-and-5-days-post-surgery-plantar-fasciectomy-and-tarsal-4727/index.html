3wks and 5 days post surgery plantar fasciectomy and Tarsal Tunnel release.

Archive date 2026-09-1515 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

3wks and 5 days post surgery plantar fasciectomy and Tarsal Tunnel release. I have graduated out of my boot and into tennis shoes as long as my foot will tolerate. I am supposed to start doing my exercises and stretches, but not allow anything to hurt. I am also supposed to use vitamin E oil and start massaging my incision site and foot. I have also been allowed to return to work on Wednesday!
