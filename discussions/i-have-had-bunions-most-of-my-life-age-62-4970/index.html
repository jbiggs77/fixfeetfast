I have had bunions most of my life (age 62). They go in long cycles of having no issues and very short cycles of pain. Currently no pain. I’ve contemplated surgery a few times, most recently 15 years ago. Every doctor at that point said don’t do it unless you have to. I know surgery has come a long way and I may consider it in the future. But my question is about the cause. I recently have discovered hypermobility traits in my daughter (possible Ehlers Danlos) which she inherited from me (I have much fewer issues). So looser hips, knees… can this looser walking gait cause the bunions? If so, are there any ever used to help prevent them go my adult daughter?
