I broke my first metatarsal on my left foot back in February and I was non-weight-bearing in a boot for 7 to 8…

Archive date 2026-09-155 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

I broke my first metatarsal on my left foot back in February and I was non-weight-bearing in a boot for 7 to 8 weeks. The last few weeks I have started physical therapy and I am back to walking. I was recommended to buy Brooks shoes so I bought the Brooks Ariel 26 women’s shoes and I was also recommended to use hiking boots as they are good for stability. However, the hiking boots feel very heavy on my feet and I don’t feel like my balance is good. I am noticing in both of these the shoes and hiking boots that my foot that was broken with the first metatarsal is rolling outward. My doctor was trying to tell me that the Brooks shoes are known for that, however it’s also happening in the hiking boots. Has anyone else had this problem after beginning to walk again? And is this something that will correct it itself is it just from non-weight-bearing for so long?
