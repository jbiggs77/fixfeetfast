I wanted to share this for anyone in here who feels like there's no way out of plantar fasciitis right now. It can get better. But it takes time, consistency, and patience. I've been dealing with it since January 2025. Like a lot of people, I tried to push through it at first - kept running until around August when it finally stopped me in my tracks and had me limping.
