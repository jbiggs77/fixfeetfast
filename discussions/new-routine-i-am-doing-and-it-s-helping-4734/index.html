New routine I am doing and it's helping!

Archive date 2026-09-2014 archived replies
Physical Therapy & Foot Exercises

New routine I am doing and it's helping! Moderately warm foot bath while rolling on a lacrosse ball with toe separators on! Slow controlled rolling no hard back and forth. Helping me break up adhesions and started this on Sunday and I was 4/10 daily to more like a 2/10! Have you tried the same?

I use the toe separators also. In addition, I wear wide toe box shoes, use a heavy compression sock if going to be on feet for a long time. She believes in strengthening the foot. She says wearing cushioning shoes makes our feet weak.
