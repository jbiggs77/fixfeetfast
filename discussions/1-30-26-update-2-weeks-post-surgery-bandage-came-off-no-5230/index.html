1/30/26 - UPDATE! 2 weeks post surgery. Bandage came off. No barefoot walking or full weight/walking without my boot. Not even around the block. Have to keep boot on for 4 more weeks. Then xray to see how it all looks. Adding my B&A xrays and my foot 2 weeks post surgery w/out the wrap. 1/16/26 - First Ray Bunionectomy with Distal Osteotomy and First Ray Osteotomy, Hallux. Just had my surgery yesterday. My doctor said Boot for 2 weeks and then at my recheck aappointment we will assess and then hopefully move to a soft shoe. Any suggestions regarding icing, mobility, etc...would be appreciated.
