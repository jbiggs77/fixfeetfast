I’ve had severe toenail fungus for 18 years!

Archive date 2026-09-249 archived replies
Toenail Fungus Treatment

I’ve had severe toenail fungus for 18 years! I’ve tried everything. Finally this year, i feel like I’m seeing huge improvement. - castor oil, tea tree, tinactin cream and Jublia. Wow. Two weeks ago, rashes popped up on my groin, armpits and on my head. Great… athletes foot has spread I assume. I made a doc appt which I couldn’t get in for three weeks. So in the mean time, I thought I’d just adapt my diet- 100% cut sugar, refined food and starchy carbs. Like an anti Candida diet The rashes an itches cleared up almost instantly. My scalp was no longer itchy.. I couldn’t believe how quick it happened. Yesterday, I was caught without lunch and purchased a fruit tray with cheese, meat stick, fruit and yoghurt fruit dip - I figured a little treat would be ok. Last night, the itches came back. While super inconvenient, it makes me think I’m on to something with cleaning up my diet.
