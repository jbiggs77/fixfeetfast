I am 4 weeks post op on the 7th from Lapiplasty.

Archive date 2026-10-163 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound Healing

I am 4 weeks post op on the 7th from Lapiplasty. When were you all able to walk again? I know each procedure varies but I cant even imagine putting weight on my foot tomorrow. They anticipated clearing me for walking at my next appointment tomorrow. (They said between Saturday-Monday I could try a few steps if I felt comfortable) I can slightly rest it while standing with my crutches but to stand normally on both feet without crutches didn’t work a few nights ago, was far too painful. I did have a screw put in and multiple bone grafts if that makes a difference. Any insight would be great. Thanks!

Community comment#1

It’ll take practice to get your brain back into walking after not for so long, so try to add a few more steps each day. As long as you go slowly, you’re not going to hurt anything. It’s good to get blood flow. My dr wanted me walking in gym shoes at 3 weeks post op, altho I had MIS and no bone graft.

Community comment#2

Lapiplasty on 3/7 I was able to put 50% weight after my 7 week check, moved to 80% and heading to 100%. Still painful and not quite there yet but it is getting better.
