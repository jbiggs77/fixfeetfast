Ingrown toenail regrowth: recurring and neuropathy concerns

Archive date 2026-09-254 archived replies
More foot health experiences

Anyone ever have ingrown toenail surgery problems? I keep getting regrowth in the corners of my big toes after 4 to 6 months and have to repeat the process with my podiatrist. He says he's using the acid to prevent regrowth but it seems to be marginally effective. I'm also getting on and off neuropathy in my toes and front bottom of my feet ever since I've had the first procedure.

Community comment#2

I have had a slither of toenail down the inside of both of my big toes removed. It helps tremendously. I have never heard of it growing back if permanently removed. Both of mine were done by a podiatrist. First doctor was very careful to get a good cosmetic result and remove only minimal amount to get relief. Second doctor wanted to permanently remove entire nail. I am so glad I didn’t let him do that. It sounds like you may want to get a second opinion from a different podiatrist. After care is important. First one, I used Dreft powdered laundry detergent mixed in something for soaking. Second podiatrist wanted me to use epsom salt. I think I was able to find info for Dreft and finished last few weeks with that. It works better. You could Google and probably find out how to use Dreft.
