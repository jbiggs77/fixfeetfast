Archive date 2026-06-0910 archived replies

I am 3 weeks post surgery. My foot no longer has the pains shooting thru it! I do have a new pain. When I get up from wheelchair and lift my leg(surgical one) I have pain in muscle on top of my leg! Also some pain in hip area. When I try to lift leg up to get out of wc . It doesn't hurt going side ways. I have to lift my leg with my hands but still will hurt. My other leg is fine-been using it to propel the chair. I have been using the knee scooter so have been somewhat active. I have to lift leg up to get on bed. I know it'll get better as I move it....anyone else have this problem? See less
