Hypermobile bunions with flat feet: Lapiplasty or Lapidus?

Archive date 2026-09-288 archived replies
Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryScarf & Akin OsteotomyFlat Feet & Arch Support

Those of you with hypermobile feet, pretty severe bunins as well as flat feet Help -which procedure did you go with?? I have seen two different Dr's. Both with great reviews.. One is a podiatrist - he recommends lapiplasty. One is a orthopedic foot and ankle dr, he recommends lapidus with akin. The ortho was very upfront and honest, stating that 1 in 5 of these procedures end in the patient unhappy they did it.
