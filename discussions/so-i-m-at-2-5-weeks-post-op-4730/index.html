So, I’m at 2.5 weeks post op.

Archive date 2026-09-2012 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound Healing

So, I’m at 2.5 weeks post op. The pin that is in my foot has shifted due to some thread from the gauze that caught it. Now it’s going through a nerve and lighting up everytime I move. Even the boot hurts to put on because of the pressure. She is taking it out it 3 weeks early next Wednesday. And I have to manage the pain until then. This is awful.

I did feel a ton of relief, but the relief was mostly because there was no longer a wrapping constantly touching the pin. I walked out of the office with nothing but a band-aide covering the hole where the pin was. Your foot will feel very tender while walking, but it’s nice to be able to ditch the surgical boot and wear a normal tennis shoe. Now I’m just dealing with swelling and stiffness but I know that’ll get better with time.
