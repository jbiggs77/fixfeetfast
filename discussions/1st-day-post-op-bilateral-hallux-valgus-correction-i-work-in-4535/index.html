1st day post-op bilateral hallux valgus correction (I work in child protection in the only specialist role, so have a client list which only I can work with so it was best for me to have just the one period off before my waiting list becomes unmanageable). Managing pain with co-codamol and ibuprofen at the moment which is only in my left foot. I’m managing to walk the short distance to the toilet with my step shoes and crutches. Bum shuffled up the stairs to bed last night and then husband picked me up to sit on the bed Bunions don’t look severe, but I’ve had them since a teenager and do a lot of hiking which has been problematic for the past 10 years, although Hanwag alta bunion II boots really helped and highly recommend. Also have arthritis in both big toes which the Consultant recommended I hold off and have fused when I’m older (I’m 44). I’m in Wales, UK. Any questions please ask

DippyEggs43 I see the surgeon in October. The left foot that was done is doing great. I can barely remember what it was like to be non-weight bearing for what seemed like forever back then. I am sure it is hard to do things for you right now. Hugs!! But you are right that it is done now. That in itself is something to praise. It took from March 20th til the end July for swelling to stop completely. I was told swelling can last up to a year. I am careful to wear supportive tennis shoes with a wide toe box and on a rare occasion my Birkenstocks. I only wear Birkenstocks if I am not going to be walking a lot. My foot tends to ache a little still if I walk in those too much yet. My physical therapist said to wear sandals that have a back strap until I am a year out but it is nearly Fall in Wisconsin so sandals won't be an issue soon as it will be too cold to wear them. I can wait and wear my Birkenstocks next summer more often. I am truly glad I had this surgery. Best to you!
