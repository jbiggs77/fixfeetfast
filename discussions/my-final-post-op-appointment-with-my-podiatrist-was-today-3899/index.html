My final post op appointment with my podiatrist was today. I had a 1st MPJ Fusion surgery on my left foot on February 25. My surgery and recovery went well. Here’s pictures of my foot the day of the surgery & what it looks like today. I’m happy with the outcome. No pain or numbness and I can wear shoes that I have with no issues. There’s still some swelling around the big toe and this will continue to go down as it has from month to month and the scar will continue to fade.
