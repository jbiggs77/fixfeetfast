I had the lapiplasty procedure done a few years ago.

Archive date 2026-10-1613 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryMinimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

I had the lapiplasty procedure done a few years ago. It was extremely painful recovery which took over a year before I was walking correctly and without pain. It took a full two years before I was walking without any sort of limp whatsoever. Now I'd like to get the other foot done, but I'm wondering if I should just go for the regular bunionectomy. Is the recovery easier with a bunionectomy? I currently don't have a whole lot of pain on that non treated foot but I don't like how different my feet now look and can't wear any sort of dress shoe on the non surgical foot.
