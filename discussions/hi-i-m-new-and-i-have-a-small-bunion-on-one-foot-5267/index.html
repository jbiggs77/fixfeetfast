Hi, I’m new and I have a small bunion on one foot.

Archive date 2026-10-163 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

Hi, I’m new and I have a small bunion on one foot. I remember the day that it formed. I woke up one day and walked on it. I heard and felt it misalign accompanied by pain. I didn’t realize at the time it formed a bunion. On that day I think a Dr could have realigned it just like when an arm needs to be pushed back into a shoulder joint but I’m guessing that now that a couple years have passed that is not an option. It does feel out of joint though and I can tell that when there is pain. I guess exercises and shoes can help it not get worse but how can exercise make it better?
