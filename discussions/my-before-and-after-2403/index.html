Ohh no! You should have been weight bearing after one week. Go easy now, but constant, if you don't walk a bit the bone will not merge as expected. I had a similar operation in November and was advised to start weight bearing after a week. No boot, just an orthopedic shoe and light compression bandage. It still hurts and it's rigid but way better movement than back then right now. It's a slow process. I am angry with my dr who simply didn't care,so I asked for advice in this and other groups on FB, got conflicting advice but took into account the ones that sounded reasonable.
