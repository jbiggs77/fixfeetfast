Best Shoes After Foot Surgery

Burning, tingling, or numbness along the inner side of your big toe that worsens in shoes and has been going on without a clear explanation is not something you should keep living with or dismissing as just another part of your bunion problem. Joplin Neuroma is a real and specific diagnosis that responds well to targeted treatment when it is correctly identified — and getting that correct identification requires a foot and ankle specialist in Lancaster County who knows to look for it. Too many patients spend months receiving treatment for the wrong diagnosis while the actual nerve compression continues unaddressed and the symptoms gradually worsen. Dr. Keith Fleischman, DPM, FACFAS, at Mount Joy Foot & Ankle brings the diagnostic precision and clinical expertise to identify Joplin Neuroma confidently and create a treatment plan that addresses the nerve compression directly and effectively — whether that means footwear changes, injection therapy, addressing a concurrent bunion, or surgical intervention in appropriate cases. You deserve a diagnosis that actually fits your symptoms and a provider who will pursue it with the thoroughness it requires.
