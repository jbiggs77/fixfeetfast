Fun fact: Apparently foot trauma tells your body to stop using any energy to make toenails on that foot grow.

Archive date 2026-10-148 archived replies

Fun fact: Apparently foot trauma tells your body to stop using any energy to make toenails on that foot grow. Toenail growth gets bumped to the bottom of the list. Could be a money-saving strategy for pedicures, albeit a painful one. Did anyone else notice that their toenails on the surgery-foot stopped growing?
