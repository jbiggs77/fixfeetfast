When cortisone finally stopped working I went with the next best thing. Had my first shockwave treatment today. He said I will prob need 4-5 treatments. Obviously we hope this works. If it doesn’t I can opt for plasma in sections or surgery. I can use my own plasma but he has much better success with immature plasma extracted from umbilical cords. One day at a time.
