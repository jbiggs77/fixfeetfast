Plantar Fasciitis Treatment

This would be a very severe case, but Dr. Staschiak said it is a fairly accurate image of what PF looks like when tissues become calcified. Chronic Plantar Fasciosis. He said this is more severe than what most people have. Most people don’t have this much build up. But this can give you somewhat of an idea of what’s going on and why it hurts so bad. The calcification prevents blood from flowing into the cells. The tissues are dying. His procedure breaks up the calcification. It is the same machine used to break up kidney stones. You must be fully numbed, but it is a one time procedure and it is extremely effective. He cured my right foot completely and now my left foot is well on its way. I still have a few more weeks before it will be completely healed. PLEASE NOTE: This is HIGH intensity shockwave therapy. ￼ Inevitably, there will be somebody in the comments saying that they had this and it didn’t work, but they are most likely talking about regular intensity shockwave therapy. That is NOT the same procedure at all. Best of luck! Happy to share what I know.
