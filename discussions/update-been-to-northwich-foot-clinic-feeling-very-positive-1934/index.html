UPDATE... been to Northwich foot clinic, feeling very positive, got insoles with wedges attached after watching myself walk I can understand why the bursa is not getting better I've been getting so frustrated not being able to get outdoors for a decent walk . I've been diagnosed with Adventitial bursitis involving the plantar surface at the level of the 5th metatarsal head , ive never had problems before and had a tailors bunion for many years, my foot is very painful when exercising and wearing any type of footwear hurts the top and side of my foot .
