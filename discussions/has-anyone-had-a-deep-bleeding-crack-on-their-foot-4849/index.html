Has anyone had a deep, bleeding crack on their foot?

Archive date 2026-09-267 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryToenail Fungus Treatment

Has anyone had a deep, bleeding crack on their foot? What helped? Back story: I have had two bunion surgeries on my right foot, the second one being a fusion in 2020. For the last few summers, I’ve developed a deep open crack where my big toe meets my sole—kind of in between my big toe and my second toe, but more on the big toe side. (I can take a pic if anyone is curious.) Typically I just put a bandage on and switch to socks and sneakers instead of sandals for a few days, and it seems to heal—the pain goes away, at least. But it always comes back. This year, I visited my primary care provider about it. She said she thought the problem was caused by the immobility from the fusion. She prescribed terbinafine cream, which I started today, but she didn’t sound very confident that it would help. I’ll take any advice anyone has. I’m not sure if I should give this cream time to (maybe) work, or if I should just go back to the podiatrist. Thanks!

What struck me is the fact it's not there with sneakers but is with sandals. Generally speaking sandals are not very good shoes as far as proper structure and support go. Your fusion has obviously affected your gait and you must have the proper support. Talk to your podiatrist about this and they can make some recommendations that are right for your new foot structure.
