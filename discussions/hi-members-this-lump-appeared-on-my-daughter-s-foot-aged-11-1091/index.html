Community comment#4

Can a bunion develop suddenly? No, but this is a common misunderstanding. Bunions always gradually develop over time. In some cases, the bunion appears to have formed “overnight,” but this is an illusion. The swelling and irritation is a type of bursitis typically caused by the bunion rubbing against a tight shoe. This is from the bunion institute.
