If you have a busy life with small kids like me, or just a busy life, you simply do not have the time to do vinegar foot soaks 2x a day. In an attempt to find a way to still get my vinegar treatments done, I bought these silicone socks off Amazon for $10 and I love it! I soak my cotton swabs in vinegar, put them on my affected toes, throw these silicone socks on and get my house hold chores done. You could also sleep in them!! Just thought I’d share!
