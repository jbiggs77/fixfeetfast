Edited to remove me saying I had x-rays as I've not had any since before my op Hi all. I had MIS, no hardware, NHS UK back in July. I've hit 4 weeks now and it's going well. I've had one appointment at 3 weeks to remove bandages and the surgeon said see you in 6 months! From week 3-5 I am taping my toes to ensure stability of the big toe. Has anyone else in UK had similar aftercare. I expected more check ins and x-rays sooner them 6 months. Also next week I can loose the surgical boot and try a shoe. I wear Altra anyway but does anyone have any advice on this stage or any footwear suggestions. Thanks

Community comment#3

Hi, I’ve just had corrective surgery MIS at the end of July, based in UK. On leaving hospital I had a surgical shoe, had my stitches out at 2 weeks. Week 4 for I’ve just transitioned into Mazuno wave rider went up a full size and wide fit. I’m going back at 6 weeks post op for xray and to see the consultant and then in my itinerary it says I’ll be seen again at 6 months! 3.5 years ago I had both feet done left foot was a success however right foot had other ideas which is way I’ve resulted in having surgery again. That time I had open surgery on my feet and the healing process was much slower. Other footwear I’ve bought is a pair of new balance again a full size up and put an insole in the left shoe so my foot isn’t slipping and a pair of Birkenstocks with the heal support round the back, normal size and obviously you can adjust the front straps to how swollen your foot is. My consultant did say to not walk along way in the Birkenstocks as they don’t support the foot enough! Hope that helps 😊
