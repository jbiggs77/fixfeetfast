First day, I only taped the sore ankle, but quickly realised, the PF in that foot was so much better from the taping. So next day, I did both feet. I can walk around like a normal person now, even in the afternoon/evening I can still walk OK. I am not hobbling each time I stand up and need to walk. I have googled and googled and I can't find the web page I found from a few days ago. I can explain though as it is pretty simple and only takes one piece of tape. You start at the top middle of your foot, you are going to go around and under your foot, back up the top, then around your ankle and back to the middle. Then cut off the tape, and that is it. The tape is tight and is supporting my arch and is specifically touching the PF spot on the bottom of foot (in the arch, up near the heel). I have tried heaps of taping techniques before, none as easy/quick as this, and none that worked pretty much instantly. My arch just needed the extra support from the tape.
