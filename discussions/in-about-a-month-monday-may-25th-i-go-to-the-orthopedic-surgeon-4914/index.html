Bunion surgery prep: equipment and supplies you'll need

Archive date 2026-09-2720 archived replies
Walking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

In about a month Monday May 25th I go to the orthopedic surgeon and have this fixed. I need to pick up and get use to and use equipment I will need after the surgery. I also have to decide if nerve block, sedation, or general anesthetic is preferred. So I’m looking to hear everyone’s experiences and advice. What do I need to rent/ purchase? Why? What does it do or help with? It’s day surgery in and out the same day. My equipment page lists the following possibilities: Crutches $25 rent a week or $40-45 to buy Walker without wheel $30-45/ month or $15/week Wheelchair $60-90 month $30/week Knee scooters $60/month $20 week Transport chair $50/month Foam seat cushion additions $35/month Include elevating leg rests add $20/month Is there anything missed by this list or a must have item to make this whole healing and recovery process easier and more successful?

Community comment#1

Raised loo seat is very handy. Hot water bottles to freeze & put behind your knee to reduce swelling. Wide leg trousers you can put on over boot/plaster. Waterproof leg cover for once you can have a shower & shower stool.

Community comment#2

I'd definitely suggest getting the wheelchair for the first month, it helps a lot if you need to get around, crutches is what I've been using but I also know a lot of people have found a lot of freedom with the knee scooter. With the elevation leg rest, you could just use some pillows to create a good amount of elevation. I usually have two stacked then one long one on top of that at an angle. I definitely recommend getting general anaesthetic as well as nerve block. My first surgery the nerve blocked failed in one foot and worked in the other. I was in so much pain in the foot that the nerve blocked didn't work. Then in my second surgery they did a nerve blocked of my whole leg and that helped a lot again I was in a lot less pain and discomfort to begin with. Good luck with your procedure! If you get a chance, see if you can ask what procedure they are planning on doing and get a second opinion to make sure it's the correct procedure for your circumstance!

Community comment#3

I got a shower stool and boot cover. I used the boot cover for the three weeks I was in a surgical splint as well as when I was in the boot. I couldn’t have showered without the stool.

Community comment#4

I have no idea how old you are but I am 61. Crutches were a no go for me as I fell with them. I got a rollabout to use instead. Knee scooters are pretty easy to tip over.
