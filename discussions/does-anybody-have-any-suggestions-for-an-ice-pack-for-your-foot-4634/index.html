Does anybody have any suggestions for an ice pack for your foot after the Surgery?

Archive date 2026-09-159 archived replies
More foot health experiences

Does anybody have any suggestions for an ice pack for your foot after the Surgery? I found one that is a slipper with no back you can just slide on your foot, but it's very heavy and I think it will hurt too much to use. I'm thinking the best ice pack might be one you can wrap around your foot and lightly secure. Any ideas or suggestions would be appreciated.
