Toenail fungus: ten years later, trying new Toe Fx treatment

Archive date 2026-09-1327 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot Surgery

Hello, new here. Ten years, three podiatrists and one foot nurse later, here I am. I was told all this time I had "thickened toenails" due to repeated trauma, ill fitting shoes etc. Previous tests negative until the most recent one. I've tried everything over the years, listerine, epsom salts, all the topicals, ACV, Urea. Doctor unwilling to prescribe oral, says liver risk outweighs benefits. Recently started Toe Fx (Canada but I think in US soon). After significantly grinding down nail, they apply Methylene Blue dye which sticks to the fungal spores. Placed under red led lights 660 nm for 15 minutes, the dye makes the spores vulnerable to the light and damages them. I am skeptical, but hopeful. It costs $60 for treatment every two weeks, and it could take ten to twenty sessions. Here is before my first treatment, here's hoping I see some results. Anyone else tried Toe Fx and what were your results?
