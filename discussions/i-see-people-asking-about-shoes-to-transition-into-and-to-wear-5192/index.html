Best Shoes After Foot Surgery

I see people asking about shoes to transition into and to wear before surgery if they need to put off the surgery for awhile. I have had luck with Orthofeet shoes and Fitville shoes. I can post links in comments. I just was able to go to shoes last week and have some nice solid slippers that are for diabetic feet that velcro all the way around but they aren't something to wear every where. I pulled out my Orthofeet shoes I was wearing before surgery and they fit wonderfully and have a very wide toe box to help pressure, plus very supportive soles to help keep my arches up. Hope these help someone find shoes. I posted links for women's but they also carry men's shoes for you guys in here.
