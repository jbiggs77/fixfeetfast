7 months post op. (bilateral lapiplasty, disolveable stiches, 6 weeks in bandages, 8 weeks before I could wash…

Archive date 2026-10-1415 archived replies
Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

7 months post op. (bilateral lapiplasty, disolveable stiches, 6 weeks in bandages, 8 weeks before I could wash them ) It’s not been an easy journey, there’s been lots of tears and cursing at the frustration of being in pain this entire time and not being able to walk properly. I started physio (a little too late) about 3 weeks ago and it has made a tremendous difference to the flexibility and pain level. This is something to discuss with your surgeon and get on with as soon as you are able. I read all these posts in this page and completely empathise with what most people are dealing with. With persistent self care and attention things will get better and someday in the near future you’ll be able to put your dancing shoes on. All the best to you all, you got this!!

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#7

I’m not medically qualified to comment on that due to everyone’s situation being slightly different. I guess it really depends on the reasoning both have given you. For myself, I decided to have both at the same time due to 1) Not wanting to be out of work for too long - I had 6 weeks off. 2) I was afraid that if I had one done and it was super painful, that I would talk myself out of getting the other fixed. Having both done was a major challenge in the beginning as I was u able to walk without crutches for 6 weeks, unable to shower properly, and was in a lot of discomfort. I’m still not walking like I used to, but since doing at home physio each day, my movement has improved dramatically, as has the pain. The only advice I can give is to weigh up the pros and cons of both what your Dr’s advize and what works for you, your lifestyle and your tolerance. Whatever you decide, I wish you all the best ♥️
