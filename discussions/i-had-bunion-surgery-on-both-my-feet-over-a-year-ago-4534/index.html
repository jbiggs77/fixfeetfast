I had bunion surgery on both my feet over a year ago.

Archive date 2026-09-1115 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound Healing

I had bunion surgery on both my feet over a year ago. Doc said it was the worst he had seen in a long time and put in screws and plates and indicated that it had been major surgery. I had physio for a while. My feet still hurt daily and I am limited as to how far I can walk before the pain is unbearable. Walking on uneven surfaces like rocky areas are just torture. Feet still swell occasionally. I have had screws and plates removed. I have tried so many kinds of shoes and find that slip on sketches are the most comfortable. My question is will the pain ever go away and will I be able to e.g. go for a proper hike again?

Community comment#2

I had one foot done and I'm an avid walker. It takes a good 3 years for bone to heal. Not to scare you, but IMO it's a long journey. The key is good shoes for sure. Try Altra shoes. Pricey but worth it. Every wobbly rock was not fun. I'm just now getting back I to shoes I almost took to Goodwill. I also just wore heels to a wedding. It's been three years!!!! I had to wear a stacked heel, and now I found a new slide on heel. It's just time. Also try to sleep with your feet elevated on a pillow. I did that for 2 years. I hope this helps. Take care and be patient as hard as it is. It does get better. 🥰
