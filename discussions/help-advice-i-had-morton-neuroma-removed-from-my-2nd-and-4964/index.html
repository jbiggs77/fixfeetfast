Archive date 2026-09-2910 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryFlat Feet & Arch Support

<<HELP/advice.. I had Morton Neuroma removed from my 2nd and 3rd metatarsal nerve back in Dec2017. I had some custom made insoles but it was too painful in the beginning months... the major pain in my foot went away, but I still have issues with my left forefoot area. I went to the podiatrist back in Jan"20 for a quick check-up and x-ray... the verdict was to wear premium insoles and support the arch and loose sneakers. I don't remember how much money I've spent on amazon on buying different types of insoles hoping they'd help. I gave up and tried Upstep this past month...it's been okay but I still have the feeling on my metatarsal heads...the pain is manageable, but I wonder if this is gonna stay forever with me... I don't understand how come is not healing... I regain 95% mobility on my 2nd and 3rd toe, but I think I still have some nerve damage or healing to do... am I ever going to recover? I need some hope... I can wait..but I need to get back on running/jogging mode... I'm a bit scared... I don't want another surgery....any thoughts?
