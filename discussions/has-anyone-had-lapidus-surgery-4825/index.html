Community comment#1

Lapiplasty is a company that created a modification to the Lapidus with jigs to be used and two plates with (?14 screws) to rotate and fuse the joint. The Lapidus is a procedure to fuse the first metatarsal cuneiform joint. It was originally performed by suturing the joint together with absorbable sutures. It is not a MIS surgery (minimally invasive) and original MIS surgery doesn’t use any screws or plates as well. With companies, similar to Lapiplasty, making hardware = making money, they are now all trying to develop screws for MIS surgery, however they are not needed
