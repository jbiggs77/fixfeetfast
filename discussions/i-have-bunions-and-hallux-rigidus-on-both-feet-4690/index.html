I have bunions and hallux rigidus on both feet.

Archive date 2026-09-187 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

I have bunions and hallux rigidus on both feet. That makes me need a very large toebox for a wide and sensitive forefoot but the rest of my foot is just a medium. I'm a hiker and it's a very hard for me to find a comfortable hiking boot. I've tried Keens, Merrells, Altra, I even tried Hanwag, which advertises itself as the bunion shoe. Does anyone have any recommendations? Oh, I also tried ortho feet and that one wasn't comfortable either.

Community comment#3

Topo and Altra. The Olympus line is stiff soled and rockered. Olympus 6 shoe is good for hiking; I thought the Olympus boot fit too tightly. Topo Ultraventure (shoe) is on the stiffer side and not as cushioned. I am not familiar with whether topo has hiking boots but they're a good option for foot shaped shoes.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#4

Topo Atmos is my go-to sneaker. Topo traverse is what I currently use for hiking. It's not waterproof, which I need for an upcoming hiking trip. Unfortunately the rest of Topos hikers are too narrow in the toe. I'll look into the Olympus, thanks.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#5

Left too much material that sharply bent into my foot. I just felt the forefoot was too snug. I gave up on finding hiking boots. But I'm writing down those Topos to try sometimes, so thank you!
