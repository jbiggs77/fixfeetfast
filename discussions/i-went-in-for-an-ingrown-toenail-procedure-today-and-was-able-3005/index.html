Community comment#1

I had a chevron osteotomy and an akin osteotomy. I was non weight bearing for 17 days, heel weight bearing for 4-6 weeks (can’t remember), and out of work for 9 weeks. But it took a long long long time for me to feel “recovered.” At least a year. I have a lot of issues in that foot now, but that’s just me.
