Day 1 post op (Scarf and Akin osteotomy) and feeling great. No pain at all but taking every pain med they are offering in the hospital. Had full body wash this morning and changed out of hospital gown and into fresh nightwear. Feel like a new woman! Also met physio so mobile now with crutches while flat foot weight bearing. Drinking loads of water! Delighted to be rid of bed pan I’ll be here until tomorrow but should be discharged then.
