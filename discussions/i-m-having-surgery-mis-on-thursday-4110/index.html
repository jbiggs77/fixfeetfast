I’m not sure which 2nd toe procedure he went with and can’t tell from sutures. It was an inter-operative decision for the 2nd toe and I’m sure he told my husband what he did. But my husband didn’t retain that info lol. I was in post op recovery when he spoke with my husband afterwards so I haven’t spoke with my surgeon yet. Anesthesia was MAC (monitored sedation) with a regional nerve block — no general anesthesia or intubation. No pain whatsoever yet and have been immediately weight bearing in my boot. Don’t need any help getting around. So far, really great! Right toe still wanting to lean right so I’ve been instructed to stuff gauze between big toe and 2nd toe as it settles into its new position. Wild to see the excess skin my massive bunion left behind. Haven’t seen my actual foot yet but from the post op pic I think it looks good! I’ll do my left foot later this year.
