Multiple ingrown toenails: could your sandals be the cause?

Archive date 2026-10-0115 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesFlat Feet & Arch Support

Thankfully, I've managed to keep my PF at bay. I do my toes to the nose exercises every morning before getting out of bed and I wear my birkenstocks as indoor shoes. However, I fear that my birks have caused me to get ingrown toenails. I've already had to have the ingrown toenail removed on my big toe. Buy now my second and third tow on the same foot are also bothering me. I have also had some really badly cracked heals. My question is, can anyone recommend a sandal that I can use as an indoor shoe? Something with really good arch support, a flat, wide toe bed and some cushioning at the heal?
