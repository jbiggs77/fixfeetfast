I hit my One Year mark yesterday!

Archive date 2026-09-182 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Pain Management After Foot Surgery

I hit my One Year mark yesterday! Today, my foot feels great! I had MIS for my bunion. My toe is still not as flexible as I’d hoped and lunging with is still not comfortable because it’s so stiff! But, the pain is gone and it’s bliss. Yall hang in there! The worst of it is the first 2 weeks. Honestly, what was hardest for me to heal from was the sciatica I got after sitting wrong. Make sure your hips are even when sitting. Don’t let one leg be propped up significantly higher. Prop them both up.
