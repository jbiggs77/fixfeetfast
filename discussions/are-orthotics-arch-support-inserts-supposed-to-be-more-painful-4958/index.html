Orthotics are big business especially with podiatrists. They are expensive and I feel like they prey on the pain and suffering of people. Anyway my orthopedic sports medicine doctor specializes in feet. He said if you never needed orthotics before you don’t need them now I did some retail inserts etc early and they all hurt my feet. Doctor told me to stop all that. Wear decent shoes. Use my feet. Stretch and strengthen and eventually it will go away. It’s almost gone after two years

Community comment#6

I bought New Balance 880 orthotic shoes and got orthotic insoles made by specialist for my high arch. Felt back pain immediately. They hurt my feet as well. After awhile they were the only shoe I could wear. I am now almost unable to wear anything else and loathe putting those shoes on. Now trying to find new shoes. I can see other posts that recommend different shoes so am looking into that. I do stretching and strengthening exercises and have recently started the Kinesiology Taping. That is helping a lot. Best wishes.

I went to the Hanger Clinic, who actually made me an entirely new pair when the first ones didn't work. The second pair was much better and they did adjust that pair one time They've helped me for over 2 years now. I go up a shoe size to make room for the orthotics..
