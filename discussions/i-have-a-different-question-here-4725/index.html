I have a different question here.

Archive date 2026-09-1917 archived replies
More foot health experiences

I have a different question here. We're not born with bunions and I don't know if any children that have bunions. From a little bit of research, which a lot is contradictory, it looks like it could be a muscle issue that is not holding the bone in place. To me it looks like if our foot was a plastic skeleton and we could manipulate it, we could move that bone back in place naturally and not by surgery. I just don't understand the theory behind cutting the bone, and then sticking pins in it. Couldn't there be a device that could push that bone back in place over time? Thank you for listening

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#13

They already have. You can check out the Non-Bone Breaking Syndesmosis Bunion Procedure. No pain at all But it does require your own body to grow a new ligament, which means it will take a full 6 months recovery time. You can walk on day one, but the number of steps are limited. As time passes, you can increase the number of steps. You can check it out on YouTube or Google it for the website. There is also a Facebook Group where all the patients share their experiences.
