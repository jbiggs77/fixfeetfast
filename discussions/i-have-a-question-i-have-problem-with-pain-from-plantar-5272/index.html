I have a question I have problem with pain from plantar fasciitis and metatarsalgia which has been going on for 2 years.The issue that I have is that I need something with extreme shock absorption and when I am mean shock absorption I mean I don't want hit the ground at all just hitting the ground is killing me. I don't go to see another podiatrist for a little bit but I need something for now until I go to see the podiatrist. I discovered I'm more of a low medium Arch and I need something for the front of my foot my toes my toe bones the ball of my foot I don't want any insoles that have memory foam, cheap foam, cheap cotton, silicone gel, anything like the default gdefy insole, or anything quickly gives out for flattens. Something that has good moisture wicking. I've even been to the Good feet store and they were too expensive and they gave me a medium to high arch 3/4 insole. I need something that's like $50 and under an insole that'll work for the brooks glycerin 23. Let me add another challenging part of my formula here I sweep all day with a push broom every now and now I use a straw broom but mostly a push broom for 8 hours a day on a concrete floor and I mean old school hard concrete floor.

Community comment#1

I went to the Good feet store and spent a good chunk of change because it was a lifetime guarantee and I can come back and get a new insert whenever I need. I have gone back to Traverse City, (which is an hour and 15 minutes away from home.) and exchanged my inserts probably about five times by now. And my pain has not changed. Unfortunately I cannot recommend their inserts since they did not work for me. The one thing that has given me a little bit of relief is Oofos sandals for every day walking. Unfortunately they don’t make them in nonslip so I can’t wear them at work. ￼ They have a really good cushion so I would recommend trying those. They sell them at my local shoe store. (Snyder’s shoes.) I live in Manistee, but I know they also have a store in Ludington. You may be able to also buy them on Amazon.
