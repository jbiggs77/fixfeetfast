Has anyone heard of this: Okay, I had a stump neuroma removed between my 3rd and 4th toes last year.

Archive date 2026-09-289 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

Has anyone heard of this: Okay, I had a stump neuroma removed between my 3rd and 4th toes last year. It was a huge neuroma and the Dr. went through the bottom of the foot to remove it. Well about 4 months ago I started having severe pain on the bottom of my same foot right where there is a callous (next to the are where the neuroma was), and the burning is worse now in the 3rd and 4th toes. I went back to Dr. about a month and a half ago and his partner wanted to inject a steroid in there, but I said no, Ill wait and see my primary foot Dr. So, I went on Wed and he said its a nerve that is fired up and sold me these insoles, which I cant wear (long story). He told me to pad the area around the callous and that it may take 4-6 moths to it to settle down. He said he couldnt promise me total relief, but that this should help some. It doesnt make any sense to me. He said with my age and all my foot surgeries Ive lost a good share of my metatarsal padding on my feet. He explained its like Im walking on my knuckled, only in my feet. I was wondering if anyone else has gone through anything like this.

I've gone thru repeated ingrown toenail surgeries on my two big toes starting in April 2020. I've had on and off neuropathy and the on and off redness and pain since then. I've been to several podiatrists over the past year and a half and they just scratch their heads. So yeah, I'd say nerves get cut and they take a long time to get back to normal. I've finally graduated from wearing open toed memory foam slippers to closed toed bedroom slippers to now shoes that are an extra size bigger and are wide version. It's a long process for nerve damage in my experience
