I haven’t the heart to file constantly away at my toenails.

Archive date 2026-09-198 archived replies
Toenail Fungus Treatment

I haven’t the heart to file constantly away at my toenails. I go to a podiatrist for treatment. Boiling infected socks (and face towels, used to dry the feet after a shower) for ten minutes will kill the toenail fungus spores completely, according to online information. (As will the sanitation cycle of a washing machine if you have it.) I’ve been boiling several pairs of 100% cotton socks at a time for 10 minutes in a stainless steel pot (I change my socks twice each day), then machine washing in a regular, warm wash, and have been wearing them again, without fear of reinfection. With a podiatrist I have completed laser treatments over a 6 1/2 month period and am 90% cured so far. A year’s time from the first treatment will tell as the new nail completely replaces the old. I hope that boiling the socks will spare me reinfection.
