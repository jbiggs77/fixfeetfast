Community comment#1

If you search this group, you'll find a ton of posts with this combo surgery and great tips to boot! I had Lapiplasty about 6 weeks ago and am very happy with progress so far. Definitely invest in a knee scooter and ice packs or ice booties because that really helped control pain and swelling. Good luck!

Community comment#2

What is a knee scooter please? I’m in the uk and certainly this was never suggested to me!

Community comment#3

I’m 9 days post op and was off crutches by day 4 - have had no need for a scooter. The shoe given to me allows for only heal weight bearing and is fine. Off all pain meds by day 4 as well. Saw surgeon on Thursday - now wearing toe alignment corrector for next 5 weeks along with the shoe. Back to work next Friday. I’m in the UK.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#6

I’m in the Uk too. A knee scooter means you rest on the scooter on your knee so that you are not weightbearing on the operated foot. I have had no need for one as off crutches by day 4 and getting around fine. I’m now day 9 post-op.
