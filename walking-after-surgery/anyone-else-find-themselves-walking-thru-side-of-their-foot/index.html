Community comment#4

This surgery definitely takes patience. You are going to run into other issues if you walk on the side of your foot. My physical therapist would have a fit if I told her I did that. Your bone is still healing. It is going to hurt. Call your surgeon and ask for a referral to physical therapy please. I am seeing one once a week and it is really helping. She has explained so many things to me. Surgeons need to do a better job on the "healing" end of this. They are great at doing the surgery but not so much after in my experience. I am 10+ weeks out now. I had to ask for a referral to PT. So glad I did. I can almost walk normally again without limping which is bad for my knee, hip, and back. If I limp at all, I grab my crutch. I use it on the non-surgerical side. I definitely don't want to have this surgery again. Once is enough. Be gentle and kind with yourself. YouTube has some great videos about walking again after being NWB. Best to you.
