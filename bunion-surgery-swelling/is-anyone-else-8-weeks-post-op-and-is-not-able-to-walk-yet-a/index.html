Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#20

I am sorry to report I had the surgery in May, and there was a delayed fusion, so the big toe is drifting back in like with the bunion. I still have a lot of swelling in the toe and nerve pain, it is sore & uncomfortable in a lot of closed toe shoes. The second toe was dislocated and hammertoe, and it healed perfectly. I need to have a revision surgery, where my surgeon will remove the hardware and replace it, using a bone graft to make sure the joint fuses appropriately. My first surgery 2 1/2 years ago involved an ankle fusion, tendon transfer, arch lift, and hammertoe repair in addition to the lapidus bunionectomy. It was complex and a year long recovery, and I am very happy with the results. The second foot was much more straightforward, and this is disappointing. I am telling you this because it is the reality we all need to be aware of, but I have also had a lot of success and don’t want to discourage you either.
