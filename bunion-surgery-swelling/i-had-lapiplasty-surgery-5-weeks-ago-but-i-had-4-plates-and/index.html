A CAM WALKER, if worn correctly should take 70% of your body weight and redistribute it up your leg. I would suggest doing what’s comfortable. Take small, short steps, possibly touch down with crutches to start, and go from there. I weight bear my MIS patients immediately as tolerated. However, that would not be customary with lapiplasty. Best of luck!

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#5

The instep is where that procedure is performed. The joint is fused. I have had many patients come into the office having a fusion done to one of their feet, not knowing what a fusion is. A fusion is destroying a joint (the space between 2 bones) and making two bones one bone (“glueing” the bones together). That joint will never move again, and that motion will be absorbed by the joints in either side of the one that is now gone.
