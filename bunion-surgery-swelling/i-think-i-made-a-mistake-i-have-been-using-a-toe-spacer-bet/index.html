Archive date 2026-04-0415 archived replies
Post-Surgery Swelling & InflammationToe Spacers, Orthotics & Braces

I think I made a mistake. I have been using a toe spacer between my big toe and second toe and have just left it there for months - I guess now that I think about it pretty gross. Now my second toe is all inflamed and swollen and the skin is peeling off. Feels like it's broken. I finally took the toe spacer out and slathered neosporin on it. Has anyone else had this happen?
