Hi all, I had surgery to repair my tailor's bunion, about 2 months ago - this included 2 screws being inserted.

Archive date 2026-04-108 archived replies
Post-Surgery Swelling & InflammationPain Management After Foot SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound Healing

Hi all, I had surgery to repair my tailor's bunion, about 2 months ago - this included 2 screws being inserted. I can walk but every step hurts and my foot swells easily. For those that have had the surgery, when did the pain ease up and you could walk without pain or discomfort? Just going into the office for a day leaves me in agony and hobbling around, hoping this won't last too long!

Community comment#1

I had surgery 3 months ago but no screws. They said your foot can swell up to a year. Mine does swell after too much walking (or a plane ride), but not too much pain. It does ache sometimes, but not too bad. Good luck.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#2

how was your recovery from surgery with no screws? Did you have crutches or a walking boot? Looking for insight on recovery

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#3

recovery was pretty good. I had a walking boot as well as a stiff sandal like shoe for about 4 weeks. I have a 6 month appointment on May 2nd. The bone hadn't fused as much as the doctor had hoped at 3 months. Might be partly cause I'm older. At least I can wear shoes again and it only gets a bit sore occasionally because it's not completely healed yet.

My hubby had surgery April 14, had a pin inserted the first 6 weeks, He can walk with out pain most days but, he is starting to play golf, mostly 9 holes a few times a week, and Pickle ball a few times a week, which he loves, that can irritate it for a few days......or if on his feet too much.
