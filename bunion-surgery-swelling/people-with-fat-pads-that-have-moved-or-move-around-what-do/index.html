For what this may be worth to anybody, i use liquid filled insoles but I do not have fat pads on my feet (only my knees). But they are good for things like pain under soles of feet, neuropathy, etc. My feet land on liquid with them in my running shoes. They remove the "hard floor surface" when the foot lands with each step. Pricey but I got mine on Amazon. I think they are called Happy Feet, can't remember. But they have a few brands out there. These type of thin insoles have to be kept sand and debris-free to ensure they last the longest.
