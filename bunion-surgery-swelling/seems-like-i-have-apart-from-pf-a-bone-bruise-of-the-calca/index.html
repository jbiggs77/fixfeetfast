Seems like I have, apart from PF, a bone bruise of the calcaneus, trapement of the Baxter nerve because of that and oedema in a muscle near that nerve. The specialist at the hospital said she could tell what my problem was, even with these problem in my MRI report. She said to go back to work and take some painkillers. Do some stretches. 1 year after still in so many pain. I threw all my results online and thanks to that I know what to do and going for a second opinion. Just to say, never settle with a doctors' diagnose when they tell you, 'you just have PF', and go one living in pain when you feel that there is more going on. I wish I did something much earlier, I would be healed by now.
