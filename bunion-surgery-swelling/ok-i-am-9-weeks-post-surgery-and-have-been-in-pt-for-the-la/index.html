Ok, I am 9 weeks post surgery and have been in PT for the last 5 weeks but one of the things they don't do is…

Archive date 2026-09-2516 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPost-Surgery Swelling & InflammationSurgery Complications & Wound HealingPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

Ok, I am 9 weeks post surgery and have been in PT for the last 5 weeks but one of the things they don't do is massage the foot after my toe was severed due to hammer toe and they also are not doing anything about the scar tissue. I looked up how to do lymphatic massage on the foot and I immediately started to feel a lot better, the swelling is down drastically. When will medical professionals and PT staff begin to take more needed approaches? My doctor provided with limited info on recovery, PT staff have 4 to 5 additional patients at the same time so they are not really helping me address the lack of movement in my toes, nor do they have access to X-rays to determine where the hardware is although they are part of the same clinic as the orthopedic surgeon. I am shocked at the fact that a clinic like Resurgens in Atlanta GA has fallen so behind on medical approaches. SMH

Community comment#10

Wow! You’re definitely not receiving the full, proper and individualized service that you ought to be receiving and I’m sorry to hear/read this. My first/initial appt with my pt was spent with her and I in a room where she assessed my foot (incision, toe stiffness, swelling, discoloration), performed a lymphatic massage for about 10 minutes while we spoke and got a little familiar with one another. We spoke about my previous functioning level pre-surgery, how recovery was affecting my life thus far and my goal of returning to work, activities, etc. She then did passive ROM (range of motion) and had me perform active ROM so that she could assess where I was and to ensure I was doing it properly. She also did ROM measurements. She went over my ICP (individualized care plan) that she’d already prepared based on my procedure and surgeons recommendations. We went to the therapy room. There, she tested me on strength, balance, gait, functional mobility and heel-to-toe push-off, which wasn’t great as I was in a CAM boot). She ended up making a few changes to my ICP based on that initial evaluation. The real physical therapy work began from my 2nd appt on. That’s when she’d start with the massage and ROM exercises, before having me go over to the therapy room to perform different exercises for several minutes. As I progressed, so did the recommended exercises. Beginning 2wks post-op and ending at 7wks post-op, I had a total of 6 sessions with her. Your appt with your therapist is supposed to be with and for you only! Not several other patients at the same time. That’s a recipe for the therapist to neglect one or more patients by trying to do too much with too many patients simultaneously. I’ve never heard of that being done before. Is it possible for you to request another therapist and/or location?
