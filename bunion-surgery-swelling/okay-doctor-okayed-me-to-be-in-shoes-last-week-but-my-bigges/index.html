Community comment#15

My husband had a couple of old wool ski socks that were kind of stretched out. One works perfectly over my dressing and keeps my foot warm. I have neuropathy and wool is softer on my skin than most cotton. I'm having trouble with the dressings irritating my skin. Next dressing change , i'm going to use cotton cast padding next to my skin. I'll do a modified meyerson wrap. I'll put cotton strips between the toes instead of the 4 x 4s. I'll , put it around my foot before I wrap the conforming gauze. Hopeful way that will take care of some of the burning and tingling.
