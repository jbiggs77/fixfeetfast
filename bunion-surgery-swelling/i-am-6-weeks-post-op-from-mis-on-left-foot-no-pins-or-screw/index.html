I am 6 weeks post op from MIS on left foot.

Archive date 2026-04-040 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Post-Surgery Swelling & InflammationBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

I am 6 weeks post op from MIS on left foot. No pins or screws. Cleared to get into regular shoes and cleared for most types of exercise. First 4 weeks I was in a boot, then went to oversized shoes wearing a brace on the toe to keep it in line for 2 weeks. Very little swelling, minimal pain. All recoveries are different but that's my story.
