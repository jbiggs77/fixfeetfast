Tailor's bunion with swelling: early symptoms

Archive date 2026-05-2113 archived replies
Post-Surgery Swelling & InflammationFlat Feet & Arch SupportPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Hey everyone, need some advice , I suffer from gout and plantar fasciitis, but recently I’ve suffered a kind of injury on the side of my foot on the pinky toe joint. I initially I thought it might be gout, but it doesn’t throb at night or anything, so now I think it might be a bunionette aka “Tailor’s bunion” …. Does anyone have any experience with this? I’ve had it near constantly for months , and seems to swell up once a week or so.

Community comment#1

It Maybe acting up due to the gout which likely caused you to change your walking pattern. When this happens over time, a compensation occurs that often times brings up problems in a different area. Let me know if that makes sense.

Community comment#2

Go to podiatrist to get hard skin rubbed off and then maintain yourself with foot pumice stone. Helped me loads and never came back.

Community comment#3

It looks very early stages of a tailors bunion. I’ve just had surgery to remove both of mine. Make sure you get shoes that fit correctly and don’t pinch the area. I would recommend seeing a podiatrist. Early intervention can really help prevent you needing surgery down the line.

That is early stages of a tailors bunion. They can get much worse
