Hello everyone! I had two surgeries last year, initially it was to fix my bunion and remove a Morton’s…

Archive date 2026-10-0118 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPain Management After Foot Surgery

Hello everyone! I had two surgeries last year, initially it was to fix my bunion and remove a Morton’s neuroma. The surgeon removed bone from my second toe during this surgery. 6weeks healing time. It went downhill from there. I ended up getting a hammertoe which I had to have another surgery for. 8 weeks healing time. I have so much pain, my second toe sticks up because he over corrected the hammertoe, my bunion is worse than my first surgery. I get horrible Charlie like cramps and have constant pain. I saw another surgeon who told me I needed surgery to fix everything the first surgeon did. I am very upset about this because I know the pain of the surgery but I know I have to get it fixed.
