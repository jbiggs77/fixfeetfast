Hi guys! I’m new to this group and don’t have typical bunions. I believe I have tailor bunions because I have painful bones that stick out on the outside of my feet and my pinky toes basically piggy back on my other toes. They’ve started getting more and more painful. I have Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome so I have an extremely high arch and tiny (non-existent) heels. I’m trying to find tennis shoes that actually have a wide enough toe part not to rub on the bunion. I have Brooks and although the toe is pretty wide, I think I need it even wider. I also wear inserts so that tightens up every shoe. I’ve heard about Hokas, extra wide New Balance and some others. Any suggestions? Thanks!

Community comment#1

I have Taylor's bunion and I ended up developing a hammer toes in both Pinky's. I was wearing hokas with orthotic, which was making things even more painful on my pinky toes. I did physio, Chiro, acupuncture and spend two hours a day stretching and strengthening my feet. I'm not out of orthotics completely and wear altra lone peaks. I wear a met pad under my arch and wear correct toes. My hammer toe, which was flexible on my right side is back to normal and my left side is way better. I no longer have any rubbing on my Taylor's bunion and because of this it hasn't continued to grow.
