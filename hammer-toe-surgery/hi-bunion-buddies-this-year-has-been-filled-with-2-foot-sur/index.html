Hi Bunion Buddies!

Archive date 2026-09-196 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionBest Shoes After Foot SurgerySurgery Complications & Wound HealingFlat Feet & Arch Support

Hi Bunion Buddies! This year has been filled with 2 foot surgeries, one on each foot. My r-foot is 9 months out that a adductoplasty - not sure spelled right- with toe release. It was more complex and took 2 months till I was able to walk and then another month till I was in a shoe. I also did pt which helped but still having feeling in my arch and side of my foot is still sore. The l-foot surgery, I am 4 weeks out and it was hardware removal and MIS surgery plus hammer toe correction. The pin is already out. I am able to walk partial since surgery. All in all the surgeries have been good and I am able to walk in shoes -right foot- so much better. Not in a shoe yet with the left foot. I am glad I got the surgeries done as I had tried everything before the surgery and nothing was going to correct my foot but surgery. I will say that it takes effort and patience and trust in your dr and time to make these surgeries beneficial. Like I said my r-foot I am still feeling a pinch or something in my arch but going to give it awhile to see if it subsides. But in the meantime, I feel all of it was worth the effort in getting done. Feet are very complex and it seems everyone has a different kind of surgery. But I am glad to have a group to talk to and can relate to while healing. Many Blessings!
