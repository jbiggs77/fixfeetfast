Hi, I'm hoping for some advice. I live in the UK and I hurt my foot at the beginning of June. The pain is around the ball of my foot, it feels like there is a lump there and it's painful to walk on. I have been to my GP, who thinks it maybe a Morton's neuroma and has referred me to hand and foot surgery. I'm not convinced as it is now making my second toe stick up and there is generalised swelling in the area. I'm thinking I may have a hammer toe? There delays in seeing anyone, so any suggestions on how I can self manage my issue would be greatly appreciated as I am awaiting a hip replacement on my other side, so am now lame on both sides! Also does anyone know if it's hand and foot surgery that will help or should I try to be referred elsewhere? Apologies for the long post, but I'm a bit desperate!

Community comment#6

I am a horse rider and currently diagnosed with a Morton’s Neuroma in the ball of my foot between my 3rd and 4th toe. Feels like a sharp rock in that spot. Horse riding shoes/boots are narrow, plus the pressure of the stirrup is what i think caused my nerve impingement. I now have to find wide toe box shoes so I can wear toe correctors inside them when I’m active. It was almost immediate relief!! It took two days for the pain to be almost completely gone! The nerve in my foot can no longer handle shoes even slightly narrow. Maybe this is what you have? Bike riders get it too.
