Hi everyone. I'm due to have bunion surgery at the end of July, and I'm starting to feel quite anxious about it. From the initial conversation I had with the surgeon over six months ago, my understanding is that they'll be reducing my bunion, taking a bone to create an arch in my foot, and inserting two screws into my ankle. I have a telephone pre-op appointment in a couple of weeks on the phone, but nobody has explained what to realistically expect. I'd love to hear from anyone who's had a similar procedure. What was the pain like in the first few days and weeks? What pain relief did you need? How long were you non-weight bearing? When were you able to drive again, shower normally, and start getting back to everyday life? I know everyone's recovery is different, but hearing other people's experiences would really help put my mind at ease and help me prepare. Thank you!
