I have a lot of foot pain; you can see the veins, even just from light touch I get aching.

Archive date 2026-10-196 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot Surgery

I have a lot of foot pain; you can see the veins, even just from light touch I get aching. The bunion I’m told (by podiatrist) is not bad enough for surgery and that I have arthritis in the joint and should wear good shoes with inserts - which I’m doing. She also said the vein issue is neuropathy (I do have neuropathy but the neurologist says it’s an ortho issue). I believe the Latin shoes I wear for dancing (on average an hour a day) are harming my feet, maybe from tightly pushing against my feet. I’m in so much pain after I dance and it lingers. I’m trying to figure out a way I can wear them without it making my feet worse. I have marked where the pain is, the worst is on the top not on the actual bunion. I tried putting cushioned band aids on with the shoes to lesson impact and it helps a little. I just ordered a bunion guard shell from Amazon that I will try. I have an appointment with an orthopedic surgeon in a few weeks but wanted to see if anyone here has input.

Community comment#1

I would say if you're having pain in the bunion area, it's from bunions. However, I would certainly stop wearing those shoes. That will exacerbate the problem. Try wearing flat, minimalist shoes with wider toe boxes and see if your foot pain goes away.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#2

there aren’t really other shoes I can wear for dancing this style 😞 I would have to quit. That’s scary that it worsened after such a short time. Did you need surgery?

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#3

Group member, yes I had surgery on my left on 1/20 and will have surgery on my right sometime this spring or summer.

Community comment#4

I had minimal bunions my whole life and then started wearing heels once a week. After about 6mos of that, the bunions flared up and I started having pain under the second toe, where you have it circled. A couple months after that second toe pain started, my toes started curling and becoming hammer toes.

Community comment#5

Size doesn't really matter. A huge bunion can have no pain and a tiny one can hurt a lot. If it hurts and you think it's a bunion get a second opinion. I had Tailor's bunion on both feet. The larger one does not bother me. I got the smaller painful one fixed but I'm not in hurry to do the big one until it's bothering me.

Community comment#6

I was in the similar situation with my dance shoes, so I got a pair of dance sneakers for classes and practice and it made a big difference. You really need to find shoes that you can wear comfortably to not exacerbate the situation. There are a variety of dance shoes that might work for you besides narrow-toed ones. Of course I still had to address my developing bunions, and I had surgery on my right foot 2/1. My left foot will be addressed 4/5. I'm 67, and I had bunions removed when I was a teenager too, so they can come back.
