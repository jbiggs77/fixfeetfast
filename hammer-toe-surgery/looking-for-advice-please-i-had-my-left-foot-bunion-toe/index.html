Looking for advice please I had my left foot bunion & toe correction done in Feb 2023 and was walking fairly…

Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

Looking for advice please I had my left foot bunion & toe correction done in Feb 2023 and was walking fairly normally and wearing flip flops by the end of April. I’m booked to have my right foot done at the end of April this year, but I’ve recently started playing golf and don’t want to miss most of the summer. The pain is manageable, but my toes cross over and I really dislike how it looks. I could delay surgery until October and play golf all summer, but I’m worried about things getting worse if I wait. What would you do — get it done in April or wait until October? Any experiences or advice welcome
