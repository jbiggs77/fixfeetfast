Hopefully will be put on the waiting list fingers crossed Shared post Elana Traurig Good news post. I am 6 years post double bunion surgery. I had a fusion on my right (arthritis) and akin/osteo on the left. My surgeon had me weight bearing from day 1. I was struggling with my bunions for a number of years. Wore orthotics and good quality shoes but knew they were only getting worse (pain). I like to run, 5km - 21km. 6 years ago I was training for a half marathon and ended up with a fracture in my 3rd toe. My podiatrist said it would likely keep occurring if I kept running. Being Covid, no fun runs, no travel, and could WFH - I was lucky to get into a good orthopaedic surgeon (Sydney). Good news - through run/walk and building up over the years, I finally ran my first full marathon aged 59. No blisters, no big toe pain. 6hrs+ on my feet. For recovery I used a c25 program to build up carefully. Walking - fast walking - slow jogging, speed work. Especially with my fusion I actually find slow jogging easier than walking. There are successful outcomes where we can reach goals we never knew were possible without the surgery. Winning 🏅 💪🏻 Good news post. I am 6 years post double bunion surgery.
