Is it weird that I wore slides (sandals) into my surgery on 4/29 and I still can’t fit my feet into them?

Archive date 2026-09-2217 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryLapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryHammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPain Management After Foot SurgeryScarf & Akin Osteotomy

Is it weird that I wore slides (sandals) into my surgery on 4/29 and I still can’t fit my feet into them? I mean, not even half way width wise. It seems odd to me that I got bone shaved off and yet my feet seem wider. I can’t believe they could still be that swollen. My dr says up to a year, but really? I also feel like I still see a bump on the side, but I can see on the X-ray where they were shaved off. I will also add they were not extreme to begin with, but I had pain.

Community comment#1

My surgeon told me 6-8 months before you don’t notice your foot anymore. Swelling can persist 6-12 months as well. You’re through major healing, but your bones and tissue continue remodeling for up to a year

Community comment#2

I had surgery in Feb. Swelling improved a bit in May, but once summer heat started I've had constant swelling. Like others I was told it's normal for up to a year.

Community comment#3

Did you have the bone shaved without realigning the metatarsal bone…? If the bone wasn’t aligned the bunion will grow back. I’m 6 weeks post op of 3d Lapiplasty surgery. My last visit doctor said I wasn’t elevating foot enough and keeping a nice tight wrap around foot . This was why I still had swelling.

Community comment#4

8 weeks PO and still have a ton of swelling. Not cleared for a shoe yet, in a boot, but can’t imagine putting it in a shoe. I do think the swelling has gotten better though. It was swelling instantly when I’d put my foot down, now it takes longer to swell and doesn’t turn as dark purple. Progress…

Community comment#5

...I'm no doctor but even though the bone has been cut and realigned, there will still be some scar tissue which may feel firm to the touch. I have a similar issue with swelling but like you my doc said anywhere from 3 - 12 months.

Community comment#6

It takes a full year. I had my left foot done in October of 2025 chevron osteotomy and my right one done in April of 2026 The left foot is almost 100% healed 10 months later but it can still swell slightly if I am on it a lot. The right foot at 4 months post op still swells and can get sore and stiff. Still doing physio and working on range of motion. My shoes a s sandals from before all fit though. It is a very long recovery and you need to still be elevating as much as possible. My doctor was very clear. He stated that if you cannot dedicate a full 4 months to healing and then continued healing with more normal activities for the next 6-8 months the surgery isn’t for you. Everyone procedure, surgeon and obviously person having the surgery is different. This is just my experience. It is not a quick recovery
