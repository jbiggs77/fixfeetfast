Community comment#6

Wider toe box shoes and maybe go up either a half size or full size. Lower heel drop shoes if possible. Work on stretches for proper toe splay when planting your feet and keeping your toes in line with your gait (not duck footed). Maybe look into a toe separator like "CorrectToes", they did wonders for me but I didn't have bunions. I was dealing the over pronation, hammer toes, and between proper shoe fit, loads of stretching, and the CorrectToes my issues have been greatly reduced.
