Hi, thanks for adding me to the group. I'm 62 and have had bunions since I was in my teens , which I understand are hereditary, however I have now developed tailors bunions on both feet. I also have flat feet. I find it very difficult to walk barefoot or in shoes, with thin soles. I had a x-ray on the right little toe and was told it was osteoarthritis. My G P just said to wear comfy shoes with wide toe box. Is there anything else I can do? I don't want to sound like a whinge, but I have osteoarthritis in most joints and spinal stenosis as well. I wear skechers wide fit mostly and have some wide fit boots, but wondered if anyone can recommend any other comfortable shoes please. Thanks in advance

Community comment#1

Strength and providing toe splay space in footwear can prevent bunions - so pass that information along to all younger relatives! For your relatives: Wide toe box footwear and physiotherapy are key to prevention. It’s worthwhile to try Altra Running shoes and Topo athletic footwear- they are probably better than sketchers. And if you overpronate look for the “stability” styles. Type in stability in the search feature of the Altra website for your country and a few styles come up. Your physiotherapist can let you know if you overpronate. It’s important to be diligent with physiotherapy exercises, it’s key to keeping going. The Altra and the Topo shoes give ample space for toe splay in the toe box area and the return policies are great! Good luck

Community comment#2

I have had bunions for years and was told I have osteoarthritis about 10 years ago. I was diagnosed with Celiac 8 years ago, going on whole foods due to gf processed foods not sitting well with me. This is when I realized grains were causing deep bone and joint pain so I went grain free. Since then I no longer have bone and joint pain unless I have grains. I just had bunion and hammer toe surgery, the foot pain was becoming unbearable, not excruciating but constant plus a ball was forming on my lower leg that needed constant massaging. I am now looking into shoes.
