I'm scheduled for bunion (ludloff) and hammer toe surgery on July 26.

Archive date 2026-10-192 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionScarf & Akin Osteotomy

I'm scheduled for bunion (ludloff) and hammer toe surgery on July 26. I've seen mention of people not being able to bend their big toe after surgery, but my question is a little different. Has anyone experienced the inability to bend their big toe before surgery? For about 3 years now I've had that and partial numbness creeping across my toes, starting with my big toe and over the months progressing to my other toes. A podiatrist told me it was neuropathy when it first started, and that it wouldn't spread, but it has. The doctor who is doing my surgery insists it is not at all related to my feet issues and advised me to see a neurologist. I saw two neurologists, had a nerve conduction study and an MRI. Those tests showed some disc compression in my back, but according to them, not enough to cause my feet issues. They tell me the numbness and inability to bend my toe is related to my feet. I've considered putting the neurologists and my orthopedic foot surgeon in a small room together and letting them duke it out, but of course that's only a fantasy. Given how bad my bunions are getting I'm having the surgery regardless, but I'm curious if anyone else has had these other symptoms.
