Today is my 5-week footaversary!

Archive date 2026-10-0237 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

Today is my 5-week footaversary! Feet seem to be coming along after bilateral minimally invasive surgery (all 10 toes were operated on). Photo was taken post shower, but feet were rebandaged, thanks to hubby, and I’m back hobbling in my surgical shoes and bandages 24/7. Next week is my 6-week x-ray and…if the surgeon gives the green light…I can move on to Hokas and socks. Woo hoo! I attached a pic of my lovely pre-surgery feet as well.
