Asymptomatic bunions at 77: does age matter for surgery?

Archive date 2026-09-3021 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesToe Spacers, Orthotics & Braces

I'm 77, a guy in great health, had x-ray yesterday, confirmed bunions both feet. Hallelujah . I'm 77, a guy in great health, and I work out and walk every day. Yesterday, I saw the orthopedic doctor, and he had me get X-rays of my feet. They clearly showed I have bunions on both feet. I've noticed them for a long time, probably five years or so. They never bothered me, and I always wear wide shoes, but lately, I'm starting to feel a slight tingling at the bottom of my right third toe. Anyway, the doctor said surgery would be an option down the road. I "love" surgeons who "cut" as a solution. I joined this group to hear about your experiences and alternatives to avoid surgery. What kind of exercises might help stop the progression, etc.? I'd really appreciate your feedback.

Surgery corrects the developed deformity. Orthotics stabilize the medial column to deal with the mechanical influence that allows progression of the bunion. I usually recommend a bunion night splint you can buy on Amazon which can help with reducing the deformity. And for running shoes look at the shoe made from a company out of Utah called ALTRA.
