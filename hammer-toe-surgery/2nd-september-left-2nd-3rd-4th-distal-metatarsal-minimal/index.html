2nd September - LEFT - 2nd 3rd 4th distal metatarsal minimally - invasive osteotomy (DMMO) + percutaneous tendoachilles lengthening. I'm not doing too bad at moment I am getting quite bit of pain at back of ankle where I've had Achilles done. The Achilles lengthening wasn't planned for my surgery but he said it needed doing so when I woke up in recovery I knew something was different as the pain in back of ankle I knew I shouldn't of had. But when he explained he'd done it I then clicked on to why I had pain at back of ankle. I'm hoping the lengthening is successful as my right foots been done twice now and I have tendonitis and I need debridement on my right foot on my Achilles due to thickening of Achilles and I may need FHL tendon transfer on right foot too due to the issues I'm having. If anyone else has had similar surgery I'd love to hear about your recovery.
