Archive date 2026-05-200 archived replies

Making my introduction with my after and before surgeries! I have been dealing with bunions for over 30 years. My left foot had MIS 1st MTP fusion and hammer toe correction of the second toe in January 2025. My right foot had MIS 1st MTP fusion July 18th and just got the stitches out. I was in a surgical boot both surgeries... still am in the surgical boot for my right foot for 3 more weeks. Recovery going better than I expected! I have a phenomenal orthopedic surgeon.
