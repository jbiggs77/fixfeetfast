So from this angle it doesn't look like I have a real problem.

Archive date 2026-10-217 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

So from this angle it doesn't look like I have a real problem. My question is, has anyone had a genuine positive outcome from wearing separators? I've had bunions from 12 yrs of age, I'm now nearly 50. It's definitely genetic as my Grandmother had bunions and my Mother is now hospitalized due to having a fall a year out from having her Osteomyelitis foot surgery. She most likely will not be able to properly walk again. I am absolutely scared. I can't walk far as it hurts. If I do walk a decent distance it actually changes my foot shape as it changes the bone structure. Has anyone else experienced this?

Community comment#1

I have been wearing the socks that serve as separators at night. I don’t know if it helps. I suspect I’ve always had genetic bunions too. I’m 68 and just now having a little discomfort. I laid off walking for a few months and changed up all my shoes (wider toe boxes) and started doing some exercises found on YouTube. Foot yoga, toe yoga and stretching. What discomfort I had is pretty much gone but I stay diligent. I’ll wear flats the rest of my life. The only thick soled shoes I own are the ones I walk in (Hokas). Every other pair is more flexible.
