Community comment#1

Yes you can over stretch and make it worse Many people stretch too hard and too short of time Get a slant board or however you do your calf stretches and once you feel just the first pull,,hold it for at least one full min and give that muscle time to relax and stretch. Increase along the way As far as the facia in the bottom of your foot. Same thing. Don’t be stretching so deeply that you are just adding to micro tears. Stretch till the pull and hold at least a min till you feel it relax and soften. Longer if necessary
