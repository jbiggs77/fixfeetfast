Comfortable dress shoes for bunions and hammer toes

Archive date 2026-09-268 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionBest Shoes After Foot Surgery

Hi - new member here. I have bunions and hammer toes. I've been able to get by with shoes with a wide toe box and/or stretchy material. Merrell shoes have worked for me the best, but of course limited styles. I want to avoid surgery as I live alone and am a senior citizen. As long as I have comfortable shoes I'm fine. I'm looking for suggestions for other brands, especially for a dress shoe, as my niece is getting married in August. Has anyone tried Vivaia shoes? Thanks in advance for your help/suggestions.
