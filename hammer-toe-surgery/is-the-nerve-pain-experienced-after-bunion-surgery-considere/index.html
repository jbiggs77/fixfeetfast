The Dr said peripheral neuropathy but I did have the nerve conduction test so not sure if it really is.

I have both, I have had neuropathy for 10+ years associated with drop foot. I have nerve pain post-op. The neuropathy is a dull, numb, and constant type of pain. The nerve pain is sharp and jolting and comes in waves, worse at night. Just had my third surgery, and had the nerve pains each time and they eventually get better. I have found an Alpha Lipoic Acid and Vitamin B regimen to be very effective for both nerve pain and neuropathy. It's over the counter and has way less side effects than Rx medicines that are used for the same conditions.
