Community comment#3

The procedure I had was very similar. No fusing, mine just used 2 screws instead of one. But toe is straightened by realigning the bone and excess shaved off. Supposed to be able to walk right away (and my dr claims most people do) but I definitely could not due to pain. I still can't walk normal. Swelling is still a lot - it does not change that aspect of things.

Community comment#4

I went to Dr Blitz in 2021. He did a great job on my large bunion. I stayed in NYC a few days to wait for the bandage change then it was up to me to get x rays taken at home after. Dissolveable stitches. He IS on the pricier side 💰💰💰💰 Out of pocket. I would recommend David Wong Tred Foot & Ankle also in NYC. I flew in from Wyoming. He was amazing and technique is very advanced. Minimal swelling, I used crutches for maybe 4 days. They of course do very similar surgeries but I would reccomend Dr Wong. His office is much easier to work with than Blitz. https://tredfootankle.com I am 5 weeks post op on left foot which was a very severe correction of a failed surgery over a decade ago.
