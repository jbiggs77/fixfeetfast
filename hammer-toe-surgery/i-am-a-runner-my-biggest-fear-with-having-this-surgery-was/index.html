I am a runner, my biggest fear with having this surgery was that it would take that away from me.

Archive date 2026-10-1517 archived replies
Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryHammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionScarf & Akin Osteotomy

I am a runner, my biggest fear with having this surgery was that it would take that away from me. It didn’t. I had lapiplasty and hammertoe correction on my right foot in Oct. 2022 and in April 2023, I had lapiplasty on my left foot. I have since then completed tons of races and ultra distances. And last month completed a 100mile race and even got a huge PR from my previous 100milers before I had the surgeries. You can have a successful story, research, ask questions and decide what you want done.
