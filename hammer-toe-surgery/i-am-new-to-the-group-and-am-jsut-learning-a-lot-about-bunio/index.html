Community comment#1

MIS is minimally invasive surgery. Can be an osteotomy (fracture bone and move to better position) or remove bone bump, or Sometimes fuse two bones into one bone by removing joint. Lapiplasty is a brand or product used to fuse two bones together and destroy/remove the joint and then hold the bones together with plates and screws. It is a form of/modification of a Lapidus procedure which has been around for hundreds of years.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#6

many factors. There are over 200 types of bunion surgeries. Surgeons do what they feel most comfortable with, or what they have been trained to do. Oftentimes companies will teach doctors how to use their products, so they can use their products. It’s patient and physician choice as far as what is best. Interestingly, 85% of foot surgeons perform 1-2 bunion surgeries a week, and a majority less than 50 a year. It may be best to find a surgeon that specializes in bunion surgery if possible.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#8

- I would consider MIS surgery where the doctor creates an osteotomy in the phalanx bone. Most traditional hammertoe surgeries involve an arthrodesis - joint fusion, or arthroplasty - removal of joint. Regardless of 2nd or other hammertoe procedure, if the big toe joint is fused, flexibility will be zero for that joint, and your body will have to compensate and make up for that motion elsewhere.
