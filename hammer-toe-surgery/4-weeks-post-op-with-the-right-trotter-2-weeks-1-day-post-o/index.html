4 weeks post op with the right trotter.

Archive date 2026-10-1212 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPost-Surgery Swelling & InflammationPain Management After Foot Surgery

4 weeks post op with the right trotter. 2 weeks 1 day post op with the second trotter! I feel like I'm healing now and can see the end of the road!! Stitches on the left foot come out tomorrow night. I'm a bit nervous because people have been saying it's painful and the stitches on my right foot were taken out while I was under a general with the second op! Managing to hobble about in the beautiful boots. It's hot here in the UK right now (shock horror!) so I've had some swelling. I keep getting a random and very intense pain in my right side big toe. It feels like a knife. It's an external pain minus any cuts there. What's that all about? I hope you are all coping, healing and keeping your spirits up. I'm not sure what I would have done without this group during my operations and healing journey. Thank you all so much x
