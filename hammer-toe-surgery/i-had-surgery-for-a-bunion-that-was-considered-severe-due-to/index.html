I had surgery for a bunion that was considered severe due to how many degrees off normal it was.

Archive date 2026-09-1216 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPain Management After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

I had surgery for a bunion that was considered severe due to how many degrees off normal it was. Yesterday I had my last physical therapy appointment. I went from 34 percentage of foot usage to 96 percent since May. Praise the Lord!! I have zero pain now. My foot still swells a little and I am aware my second toe is bending left but that is from when I fell with the k-wire in it. Looking forward to 100 percent usage by 1 year. I had surgery because of pain and no other reason. If you just had surgery, it does get better in most cases. Hang in there!! I want to thank everyone for your input along the way as to what to do. This group has been amazing!!
