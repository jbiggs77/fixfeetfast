Almost 4 months post op on my right foot.

Archive date 2026-09-2728 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionSurgery Complications & Wound Healing

Almost 4 months post op on my right foot. The surgery went so well I got my left foot done last Friday. My feet have been horrible ever since I was a little kid. They still aren’t perfect but I am beyond pleased with the results. When my mom told me my father told her he never would’ve married her if he had seen her feet prior I never showed my feet to anyone EVER. This is a major step for me. I will post a before picture in the comments. View at your own risk. I got tattoos on my toes when I was in college over 20 years ago to try to hide the imperfections but that didn’t work. I also had hammertoe surgery in my 3rd toe 18 years ago, hence the scar.
