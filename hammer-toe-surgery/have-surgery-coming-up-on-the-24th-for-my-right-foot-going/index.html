Community comment#9

Get some ALA (alpha lipoic acid ). Do a search in this group and on Google on alpha lipoic acid Helps with burning/tingling/zap type of pain. It has helped me so much. I initially bought the Thorne brand (pricey). When I went to re-order the Thorne brand was sold out so I got another brand from Double Wood Supplements. I’ve gotten other supplements from them before, so I trusted them, but the shipping took longer than anticipated and it took a few days to get to me and I ran out of pills. Within a day of not taking them the burning pain came back so I did a same delivery from Vitamin Shoppe and within a few hours of taking it the burning /zinging/zapping pain stopped. I wasn’t 100% sure that it was doing anything at first but it was the worst way to find out that it was working lol

Community comment#10

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#16

I could not agree more. The first 2.5 weeks are as mentally challenging as physically. Exercise beforehand and feel good going into the surgery. Realize that you are going to be in managed pain for the early phase and that after that period you’ll not have pain but will be bored ~5-6 weeks. Have friends bring you lunch or stop by, if possible. Shower to feel normal. As for stuff you really need. I bought everything from scooter, walker, shower chair, leg pillows, shower bandage cover, Nice machine, knee ice wraps, ALO/Lulu softstreme pants I pulled over🦶etc. ALL I used was NICE (it’s easy to use an icing machine that doesn’t require you to fill it ever, and can be set on intervals of compression/icing-24/7), leg pillows, shower cover, knee ice pack. If you are weight bearing don’t buy other stuff.
