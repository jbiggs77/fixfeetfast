Curious to others experience with hammer toe surgery.

Archive date 2026-10-1417 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryHammer Toe Surgery & Correction

Curious to others experience with hammer toe surgery. I had 3 toes straightened on my left foot on Thursday (also had my toenail removed on my big toe) I go for my first PO appointment tomorrow. I can’t wait to see my toes! I am being super careful to not put any weight on that foot. Stairs are so scary! It’s amazing how many normal things are now a challenge and exhausting! I need a nap after I shower! It will all be worth it though. My pup is keeping me company

I had hammertoe surgery as well. Same three toes. Just had the pins removed last Wednesday. Still some swelling so it’s hard to tell what the new normal will be like. The pins weren’t painful but making sure I didn’t catch them on anything was the hard part. One did get caught in the dogs collar once - that was scary but we got her untangled without any damage. Scared the crap out of my husband though! 🤣. I did make a little cardboard “hat” to wear when I slept so they didn’t get tangled in the covers.
