Day 12 now post MI bunion hammertoe and metatarsalgia surgery.

Archive date 2026-09-269 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPost-Surgery Swelling & InflammationPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

Day 12 now post MI bunion hammertoe and metatarsalgia surgery. No real pain just some discomfort in boot. Significant bruising and swelling still which is all normal I know so I’m icing it regularly. I have some back pain on the same right side, I guess due to this constant elevating position when seated. Finally found a boot for the other foot that matches the height of the surgical shoe and that is so much better. Have ventured outside but only shuffled up the road so not far at all. Still quite paranoid of someone knocking my foot/ toe so being sensible. Roll on next 2 weeks for dressing change
