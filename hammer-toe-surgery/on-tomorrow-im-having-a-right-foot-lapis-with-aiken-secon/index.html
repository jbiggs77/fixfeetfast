Lapiplasty with hammer toe repair: pre-surgery anxiety

Archive date 2026-09-273 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPain Management After Foot SurgeryScarf & Akin Osteotomy

On tomorrow, I’m having a right foot lapis with Aiken, second hammer toe, repair, and weil osteotomy. I am kind of freaked out at this point the thought of laying around and not doing much of anything for the next few weeks has me crazy. I don’t mind the thought of the pain. I don’t mind the thought of the surgery, I’m just terrified of the recuperation. I’ve never been one to lay around. Then as soon as possible I want to get the left foot done to try and shorten the recuperation. My doctor didn’t want to do them at the same time because he feels that his patients who haven’t done at the same time don’t do as well as the patients who have them done separately. I’m trying my best to follow the doctors recommendations.
