I had changed my mind about having surgery after reading what people bad to say.

Archive date 2026-07-090 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionBest Shoes After Foot Surgery

I had changed my mind about having surgery after reading what people bad to say. My bunions stopped hurting years ago but I have neuroma and hammer toe and my feet generally are not good. Winter is fine because I can comfortably wear my Blundstones with orthotics no problem. It’s finding summer shoes- I change my shoes about 3times per day nothing is comfortable- especially because of the hammer toe. I like to exercise and walk a lot and no sandals work and my feet are so ugly anyway!!
