2 weeks 1 day post op and had my first little walk around the house.

Archive date 2026-09-1317 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & Correction

2 weeks 1 day post op and had my first little walk around the house. That first few steps felt wierd! It's wonderful not having a big lump sticking out the side of my trainer - left foot. You can still make out the lump in my right foot and that is quite a bit smaller than the what the left one was. Feeling quite emotional now. But very happy.
