Hi everyone! Looking for advice. I have an upcoming bunion surgery (Lapidus) - been needing it for years, but I am an avid runner and didn’t want to miss any races. Been battling Plantar Fasciitis for 9 months, so decided I might as well get one of the bunion surgeries out of the way. My doctor says he does a Gastroc Recession as part of his procedures, but I am just not sure I want him to do that. Anyone else have the Gastroc Recession with your bunion surgery? Pros and cons? TIA!

Community comment#1

Hi Bobbie Jo-I’ve had this procedure in each leg to reduce some of the tightness which is related to my flat feet. I also had two big toe fusions which were lapidus. While the recovery from the lapidus was pretty rough, the Gastroc Recession was not difficult to recover from. Anytime I walked or ran, I always had pain and swelling from the big toe procedure and that went on for nearly a year. I didn’t notice the Gastroc Recession at all.
