Community comment#1

Mine was done 3 days ago. So far, it has been easier and less painful than most other surgeries I’ve had. I had a long acting pain block near my ankle that worked for 24-36 hours, and take one hydrocodone tablet every 4 hours, plus ibuprofen. Very little discomfort at all while leg & foot are elevated, but it hurts to walk or climb stairs (of course). It’s enough for me to say “ouch,” but not enough to cry out or cuss. Four hours of sleep that first night, eight hours the next. It helps to use a knee scooter to get around the house. I also have a rollator that I use to carry a plate of food from the kitchen to the other room.

Community comment#3

I had mis on left last year 7/23 and recently mis with hammer toe correction (2 toes) on right foot 9/9. Surprisingly moving around alot faster even with the two extra procedures on this foot. 1st surgery i had high boot and crutches first week. This go round - shorter/lighter boot only. Hard pain meds stopped after day three (Sat) as pain is tolerable with Ibuprofin. I have Iwalk crutch to assist with chores/children as scooter was not much help (carpeted home) Do not plan to do much until stitches are removed at post op visit. Elevating, Kindle and Netflix. Icing 15 mins every hour with water bottle behind knee.
