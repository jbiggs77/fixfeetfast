I had MIS Lapiplasty August 2023.

Archive date 2026-10-0813 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryHammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

I had MIS Lapiplasty August 2023. It had great reviews of people getting rt back to work, life etc. I work for ups so on my feet all day long. I hv always been very fit and my body heals quickly after any procedure or surgery. I asked my surgeon how many lapiplastys he had done prior to mine. He claimed to have done too many to count. Upon entering the O.R., apparently I told him, not so gently that "he had better not eff this up, this foot is my moneymaker". I was quite sedated so I will not deny it as I know the thought must have been replaying nonstop in my head subconsciously. Flash forward to present day. I can not bend my big toe more than45 degrees, my incisions have still not healed, they ooze. My 2nd toe which was hammered, is still up in the air when I was promised it wld be lying down with the rest of them. My foot is more swollen today than it was 2 weeks post op. I'm very concerned that something went wrong in the operating room. I just have a gut feeling. I know my body and I know what healing feels like. This is stagnant. I have been to physical therapy but they are nervous about stretching my toe bc of the incision? When I see the doctor for follow uls he wrenches it to the point I'm begging him to stop. I'm lost here. Sorry about the novel but can anyone lend their 2 cents??
