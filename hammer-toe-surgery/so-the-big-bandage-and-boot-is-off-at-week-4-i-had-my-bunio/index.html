So the big bandage and boot is off at week 4.

Archive date 2026-09-2612 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionWalking & Weight Bearing After Surgery

So the big bandage and boot is off at week 4. I had my bunion done and 2nd hammer toe and metatarsalgia surgery. The 2nd toe now needs taping for the next two weeks to bring it back in alignment with the other toes and it will now no longer bend at the first joint which I knew. I’m back in a trainer , walking feels strange trying to put weight through the whole foot and for me it's the 2nd toe that feels so weird whereas the old bunion area is fine but I now need to do my physio and I can’t wait to properly soak and clean my foot. But just look at the difference..all thanks to Mr Bianchi at the private clinic London, I thoroughly recommend him.
