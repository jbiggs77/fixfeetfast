Bunion recurrence after bunionectomy: managing comeback

Archive date 2026-09-2714 archived replies
Bunion Surgery RecoveryMinimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryHammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionPain Management After Foot Surgery

I had a bunionectomy and a hammertoe surgery on my left foot and the bunion came back worse than before. I’m afraid to have another surgery on my feet. I have tried every toe separator there is on the market. Now I just buy rolls of gauze and fold them and wrap them and tape them around my big toes every day and that alleviates the pain of my toes leaning so far.￼
