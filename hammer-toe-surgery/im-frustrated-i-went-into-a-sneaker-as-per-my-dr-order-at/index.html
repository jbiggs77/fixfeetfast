I’m frustrated. I went into a sneaker (as per my Dr order at my 5 week post op) It felt weird but I did it…

Archive date 2026-10-0820 archived replies
Hammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot Exercises

I’m frustrated. I went into a sneaker (as per my Dr order at my 5 week post op) It felt weird but I did it because that’s what I was told to do. I wore the sneaker for a few hours and then took it off at home and had to elevate and ice. I wore the sneakers to work Friday. I was miserable. My foot was swollen, I had to ice it once at work and when I came home. I tried again today and while I was walking around the store, I had pain which I haven’t had in weeks. So now I’ve decided to go back to wearing the surgical shoe because when I wear it, I have no pain or anything and my foot feels great. I’m going to wear it until I go to physical therapy Wednesday and see what he says. I’m just so frustrated and feel like I’m not “keeping up“ with the doctor’s plan. I’m just not ready for a shoe yet.

Hi, sorry to hear your having a tough time. I’ve been wearing trainers/sneakers now for the past 3 weeks and I’m post op 8 weeks with both feet having had bunion surgery & hammertoe. I got myself a pair of mizuno wide fit and went up a complete foot size from a 6 to a 7. Started wearing them without the inner sole and now I can wear them with the inner sole in. I think key is not over doing in and trying to keep the shoes on as long as possible so that your feet adjust again, that’s what my surgeon/consultant told me. I ended up buying the same shoe again so that I have a pair for inside and outside 🙈. Hope all goes well 😊
