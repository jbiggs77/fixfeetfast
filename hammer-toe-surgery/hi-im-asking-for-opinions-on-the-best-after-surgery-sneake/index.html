Hi, I’m asking for opinions on the best after surgery sneaker. I’m 8 days PO. Bandages are off and I”m in a boot full weight as tolerated. My surgeon said to bring a pair of sneakers to my next appointment ( 2 weeks) I’ll be 4 weeks recovered and I’ll be walking in them. Excited it’s moving this quickly but the thought of wearing sneakers scares me a bit. I had bunionectomy and correction on big toe , 2nd toe - Weil procedure on dislocated hammertoe and planter plate repair. Internal stitches and no swelling thus far. Thanks for any suggestions.

I started out in Paradigm b/c it has some stability features and I found it to be a great shoe to transition to zero drop. I hike in Olympus. Here are their "podiatrist recommended" styles.
