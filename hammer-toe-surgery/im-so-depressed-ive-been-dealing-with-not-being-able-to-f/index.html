Community comment#1

Gabe, I have 6 pairs of custom orthotics; none of which I could tolerate with my conditions. I use Dr Scholl’s Running Insoles. I add a met pad inside them and/or wear a gel met pad that sticks to the bottom on my foot. I also tape my toes(at the base) to control the curling. You can find utube directions about where to place met pads and how to tape hammertoes. It helps me to walk a little but not much. Don’t give up on finding treatment but also proceed with caution. I know my first surgery made things so bad I had to wear a surgical boot or shoe until I had the second surgery. The second surgery did help my second toe somewhat. It didn’t help my third toe and definitely made my fourth toe worse. All I’m saying is don’t jump into surgery without seeking and seriously adhering to conservative treatments first.
