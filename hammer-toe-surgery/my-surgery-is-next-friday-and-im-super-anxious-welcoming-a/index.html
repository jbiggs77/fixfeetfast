Community comment#6

Things to have at the ready: 1. Refrigerated gel packs. At least 4 so you can alternate. 20 minutes on/off. 2. OTC pain meds. 3. OTC stool softeners. Narcotics can bring on constipation! 4. A Vitamin D3 supplement. I took it to promote bone healing while I was recuperating. 5. Something to use as a bedside/couch side table so that your basic necessities are within reach. 6. Items for entertainment (books, magazines, adult coloring books, knitting/crocheting items), etc. 7. Subscriptions to Netflix, Hulu, YouTube TV, etc. 8. Elevation pillow (if you prefer). I used regular pillows. 9. Ready-made frozen meals (microwavable) to get you through the 1st week-2wks. I also purchased some Skillet Meals from Family Dollar. Simple & quick, so I wasn’t standing too long. (Frozen roasted veggies were my go-to initially). 10. Get some high-protein snacks/foods. They help with muscle & bone healing. 11. Eat fiber-rich snacks/foods as well. They’ll help with digestion (even with the stool softeners). 12. Wash basin! I used it to take “bird baths” until I was cleared to shower! 13. Bed Bath Wipes helped keep me clean and fresh as well. 14. Wet wipes were great for toileting, too! 15. Travel neck pillow. It was a game changer for me while sitting up on the couch and falling asleep. 16. Comfy pj’s, if you don’t have any already. Wear comfortable clothing on surgery day! Yes! I’d say request a pt referral. I understand that some patients don’t/didn’t require pt but it’s an important part of the healing process. Wishing you a successful procedure and a smooth recovery journey.
