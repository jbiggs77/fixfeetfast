Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#14

, when I practiced podiatry, I had frequent referrals from dermatologists to determine if fungus, melanoma, or other problem. Your G.P. does not have the ability to GENTLY remove the area of the effected nail. Dermatologists either. Podiatry is the only sub specialty that can do this. Observing the effected area after that portion of nail was removed is Paramount in making the correct diagnosis. Sometimes appears as fungus with nail intact. And melanoma after that portion of nail excised. In my practice of 38 years, some of my happiest patients were those referred by M.D.s, and I removed part of nail, and told them I was 100%positive, not malignant. And in the cases of melanomas under the nail, the patients thanked me incessantly for making the correct diagnosis.
