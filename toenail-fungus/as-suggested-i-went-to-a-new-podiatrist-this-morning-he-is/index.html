As suggested I went to a new podiatrist this morning. He is on board with another round of terbinafine. Hoping it helps. I previously Completed a three month regimen in October. He said the one nail has very good new growth at the base howeber the other problematic nail don’t. This is the last hope besides maybe jublia after this round.
