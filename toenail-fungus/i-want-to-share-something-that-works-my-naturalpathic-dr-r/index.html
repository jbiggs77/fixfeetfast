Toenail Fungus Treatment

I want to share something that works! My naturalpathic dr. Recommended it for toenail fungus. It is called agrisept-L. I put about 20 drops in a bucket of hot water and soak my feet. Also put a drop on dry nail after soaking. Fungus will turn black and die. It also works for periodontal disease. Put 4-5 drops on toothbrush with toothpaste and brush daily. My dentist said I have reduced my periodontal disease by 4mm in a couple months. It is all natural and it works! I want to share something that works! My naturalpathic dr.
