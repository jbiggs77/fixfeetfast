To be honest the toenail doesn’t look fungal. It looks like great toenail dystrophy. Only 50% of thick, abnormal, discolored nails are due to fungus.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#3

it’s possible your toenail has fungus but it is not the main reason your toenail looks that way. Nails that have horizontal lines/cracks, nails that are multi-layered, uniformly discolored, thick, grow slowly, etc are signs of being dystrophic or damaged, possibly by trauma. It is not caused by fungus. It’s called Great toenail dystrophy or also traumatic onychodystrophy.
