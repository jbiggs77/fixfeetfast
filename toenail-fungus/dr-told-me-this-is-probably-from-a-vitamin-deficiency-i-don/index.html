Community comment#6

It looks a bit like subungual abscess. It looks like pus under the nail. Or could be subungual hematoma. I don’t see any signs of fungus honestly. You might need an antibiotic or trephination of the nail to drain the pus and relieve the pressure. They put a tiny hole in the nail but it shouldn’t hurt. Towards the tip of your nail with the horizontal line/crack, that looks like a remnant of onychomadesis (nail shedding). Do you injure your toe/feet often? Or do any activities that are hard on the feet? Your doctor doesn’t sound competent. There’s nothing about your nail or toe that is indicative of a vitamin deficiency. You have classic signs of infection- pus, pain (esp waking you up), redness.
