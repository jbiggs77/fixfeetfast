I took the terbinafine for 5 months and my toenail kept reinfecting. I was doing apple cider vinegar soaks and tea tree oil daily, also filing it down to really get it in there. Nothing worked so I finally just cut literally ALL of my infected toenail off (almost 1/2 my big toenail), there is a crumbly yellow fungus that grows so thick underneath! Under that, there was skin because the nailbed is separated by the fungus which doesn’t adhere to the nail. I trimmed all of it back and I feel SO MUCH BETTER, I still use vinegar and clean it well daily and apply tea tree oil. After 2 years, I’m excited to have my toenail healthy again!!
