Archive date 2026-10-1810 archived replies
Toenail Fungus Treatment

Has anyone got their toenails removed, maybe even permanently and is it worth it? I have had ongoing fungus and dermatitis for years, sadly I am not a candidate for terbinafine as I have liver disease. I have tried every remedy in the book creams , toenail laquer, vicks, tea tree oil, oregeno oil, vinegar, listerine and so on. I am tired. I have to shave down my toenail multiple times with a drimmel tool. It grows back thicker... My podiatrist said the only option is toenail removal or laser, she said there is not much effectiveness with laser because the fungus is already to deep. the toenail removal would only work if it is permanent.. I cannot put acrylics or anything over the fungus as that would make it worse.... I have asked for jublia she said that cost out of pocket about 1,000 and it is not as effective as oral.....
