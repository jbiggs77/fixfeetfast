I’ve had toenail fungus on my large toe nail for years.

Archive date 2026-10-0634 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryToenail Fungus Treatment

I’ve had toenail fungus on my large toe nail for years. I saw someone post about using this developer cream from a box of hair bleach. It’s worked better than anything I’ve ever tried. I take a q-tip and dip in the cream then apply to the nail (thick). I either leave it like that or if I’m going to bed I put a bandaid around it. The first few times I did this, it hurt so bad after a few minutes, like throbbing but I powered through and within a few days it stopped hurting. Now it doesn’t hurt at all anymore and my nail has grown out clean. *I got this developer from a box of hair bleach from Amazon*
