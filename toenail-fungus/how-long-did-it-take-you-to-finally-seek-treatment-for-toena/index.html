Shared post Kim Hatton Update 6/6/26: The white iodine is currently unavailable on Amazon. Look for a topical iodine with the only ingredient being 2% iodine. I’ve had several ask me for the routine that I used to cure my fungus. It took a year of following this routine daily to grow them out. I soaked in white iodine for 1 minute per toe. I put the iodine in 2” glass bowls I purchased on Amazon. (Put the bowls in a disposable casserole pan because they spill). I soaked in it twice per day for one week, then once per day for another week, then go to three times a week, then two times a week and then once per week until it’s grown out. Take a nail file and scuff the entire surface of the nail for better absorption once per week. Dispose of the nail file after each use. (I cut the nail files in thirds.) I also soak in epsom salt for 20 min per week and clean around and underneath the nails with nail tools. Keep the nails short. On the non- iodine days I apply Jublia. (My insurance didn’t cover it but there is a manufacturer coupon available). I’ve had success with this combo. Update 6/6/26: The white iodine is currently unavailable on Amazon.
