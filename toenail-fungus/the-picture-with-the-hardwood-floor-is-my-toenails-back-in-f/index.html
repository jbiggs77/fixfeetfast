The picture with the hardwood floor is my toenails back in February of 2025.

Archive date 2026-10-030 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryToenail Fungus Treatment

The picture with the hardwood floor is my toenails back in February of 2025. The picture on the black rubber mat is my toes today. In February I started using a product called Emoninail at the recommendation of a friend of mine who had success with that. What I have noticed in the past 8 months... My middle toe on my left foot used to have a very high ridge of keratin and it was slightly painful all the time when there was pressure on it. That ridge is now gone and the toenail is flat and no more pain when there is pressure. My right foot big toenail for many years was very brittle and would always break at around half growth. For the past 2 months I have had full growth all the way out to the end of my toe. That toenail is now very hard when I push on it. Thick and healthy. A couple months ago I noticed many of my nails now have clear nail growing out of the cuticle. I wouldn't say that my toes are cured. I definitely still have a battle ahead of me. To date I have spent a little over $100 ( 3 full bottles and a bottle of soap ) on the product and my thought is that I will continue this for at least another year even if I have full clearing in all of the nails.
