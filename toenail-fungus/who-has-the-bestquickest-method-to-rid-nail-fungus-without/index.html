My fungus was in the matrix and I have regrown the nail out completely. I’m Still treating it and probably always will in case it ever comes back. I would have to say there is no point trying to treat it unless you are prepared to wear open toe shoes for 6 months or wash socks daily in anti fungal solution and hot water. Recontamination is your biggest enemy. I used phrena cream - direct from their website - not Amazon as these are knock off creams from China. The phrena cream direct from website I used for 8 months - still continue to use - it took around 3-4 weeks to see improvement. Also daily soaks in selson blue (anti dandruff shampoo with anti fungal properties in it) I did also use the Orivelle pens and applied 2-3 times a day. Daily filing with a clean disposable file each time. Good luck 🙏🏻
