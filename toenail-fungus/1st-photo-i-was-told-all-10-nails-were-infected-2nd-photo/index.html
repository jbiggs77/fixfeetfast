Toenail Fungus Treatment

1st photo: I was told all 10 nails were infected. 2nd photo is after the last of three laser treatments 6/25/26, along with “Fungifoam” creme and a topical liquid with antifungal and antibacterial properties, 2x daily, then 1x daily after the last laser treatment. I see the podiatrist almost a year after the first treatment, after most of the new healthy nail has kicked out the old, sickly one. It’s expensive but beats taking the oral treatment which is said to have severe side-effects for many. I’ve had enough lately of taking medications with severe side effects for another ailment and tapering off them.
