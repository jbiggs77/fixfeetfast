Almost healed nail I’ve had nail fungus on my big toe for years.

Archive date 2026-05-150 archived replies
Walking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesToenail Fungus Treatment

Almost healed nail I’ve had nail fungus on my big toe for years. I tried every cure there is except for oral medication. Nothing worked. Finally I started putting hexane free, cold pressed, organic castor oil on my nail all day and all night with a bandaid on. I applied it once in the morning with a bandaid and once at night. My nail was horrible before and now it’s almost clear (it’s been about 6 months). It still needs to grow in on the sides a bit better but it’s clear!
