Community comment#1

I had very similar. I finally beat it. I got 2 shoebox size plastic bins. Filled half way with warm water and then poured in a cup of bleach in each bin and soaked my feet (one in each bin) for 20 minutes. I did this for about 2 weeks. The nasty yellow nails, I was able to pull off with some effort. Underneath was a paper thin membrane over my flesh where the nails were. The small nails grew back quickly. My toes are pink and the nails are clear that are growing back. Big toe nail is about 2/3 grown in.
