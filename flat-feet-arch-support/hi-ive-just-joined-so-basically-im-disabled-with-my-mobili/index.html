Drop foot with severe pain: managing mobility and comfort

Archive date 2026-09-2819 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryWalking & Weight Bearing After SurgeryFlat Feet & Arch Support

Hi I’ve just joined so basically I’m disabled with my mobility but can walk with a Rollator. I have drop foot in both feet but for months I’ve had terrible pain in my feet mainly left one . It’s making my walking so unstable and I’ve already fallen and broke my Neck of femur. My feet hurt in bed and all day long . It can be all over or just the ankles and underneath the feet. I’ve tried braces from orthotics but they hurt too much. My Dr doesn’t do anything as I don’t tolerate meds so every night I put Deep Heat on my feet but that’s not helping either. I’m really fed up with as I have to have my husband walk behind me all the time encase I fall. Even when I’m in my wheelchair when I have to go outside my feet hurt so much that I now hate using my wheelchair. Sorry for the long post but I just don’t know what to do. Thanks
