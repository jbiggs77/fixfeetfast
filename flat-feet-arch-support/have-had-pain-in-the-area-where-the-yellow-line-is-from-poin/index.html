Have had pain in the area where the yellow line is from point a on top side to point b into the bottom.

Archive date 2026-10-1110 archived replies
Pain Management After Foot SurgeryFlat Feet & Arch Support

Have had pain in the area where the yellow line is from point a on top side to point b into the bottom. I can walk but barely. It Hurts alot with pressure. Been going on for a week and a half now. I have had 3 xrays at 3 different ers and all normal. I'm stumped. Any ideas?

Community comment#1

Peroneal brevis tendon

Community comment#2

I'm not a DR, but suffer from pain in that area due to severe flat feet and pronation in that foot. I have been able to alleviate my pain with orthotics and a wedge specifically for pronation. Look up"Sinus Tarsi Syndrome"

Community comment#3

Ask for an MRI. There are two peroneal tendons that are around that area. Might be a tear

Community comment#4

Is a podiatrist reading the xrays? I had a piece or 3 of bone spur break off over that way last year.

Community comment#5

Check out your cuboid bone. It could have been dislodged. It can be cured slowly by wearing a small wedge underneath that area IF that's the problem.

Community comment#6

You probably need an MRI...would see a podiatrist.

Community comment#7

I had a fractured cuboid which only showed up in MRI

Community comment#8

There’s a tendon/ligament that attaches there. Mine gets sore at times. Google the anatomy of the foot and you’ll see it.

Community comment#9

Probably perineal brevis tendon

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#10

yes, my cuboid was out of place. My podiatrist made a padding (specifically positioned) under an orthotic that helped it fall back into place.
