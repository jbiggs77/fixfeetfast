Hi everyone! About me I’m 54, obese and a former marathon runner (17 marathons, one ultra). My arches are high…

Archive date 2026-09-2938 archived replies
Physical Therapy & Foot ExercisesToe Spacers, Orthotics & BracesFlat Feet & Arch SupportPlantar Fasciitis Treatment

Hi everyone! About me I’m 54, obese and a former marathon runner (17 marathons, one ultra). My arches are high and I under pronate. Actually, considering, I’m surprised it’s taken this long for the PF to start. It started this December and got progressively worse. So far I have bought ankle wraps, shoe inserts, a night sleeping thing (technical term) and done exercises from the Hinge app provided by my work. Currently wearing Brooks Ghost because the shoe store recommended them. Really interested in what my high arch buddies here do. I have read some of the posts already and look forward to learning more. Part of me feels like I should just not exercise at all until it all heals.
