"I had minimally invasive bunion (MIS) surgery a touch under 4 months ago.

Archive date 2026-10-228 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Pain Management After Foot SurgeryFlat Feet & Arch Support

"I had minimally invasive bunion (MIS) surgery a touch under 4 months ago. I never took a painkiller, needed any ice, was able to move from day one, and am back to running." That's ALL true. But not the whole story... It's great to see a good ol' before and after, but we shouldn't forget the origin story. So, allow me to bore you a little... I've had bunions since Shakira was telling us that hips don't lie. 20+ years with no problems, but slowly over time, the mild discomfort turned into pain. Then, the pain got so bad that it literally made me cry. That is not good. The bunion pain got so outta control, I knew the wisdom of Sinéad rang true because nothing compared to the pain. No post-op zap or zinger could come close, so my recovery was actually a pretty comfortable one. I understand people who have bunions and decide to wait... I may not be as gorgeous as who you see in your reflection... but I was one of you. I'm not saying you have to act like Predator and start stalking your nearest podiatrist or surgeon, but you can't base such a fundamental decision JUST on online forums boosted by algorithms. A bunion IS a structural deformity, so find a medical professional talk that talk so you CAN walk the walk. We all deserve to walk or run pain-free. Take good care of yourselves, my friends
