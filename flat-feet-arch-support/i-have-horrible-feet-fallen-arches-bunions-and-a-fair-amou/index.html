Best Shoes After Foot Surgery

I have horrible feet. Fallen arches, bunions and a fair amount of neuropathy thanks to back surgery gone south but I have finally found my shoe manufacturer. Taos brand. So far I have 2 terrific pairs, a sneaker and a sandal. I just ordered my 3rd. Taos is the 3rd brand marketed to me (us?) and the last. They are so comfortable all day & I swear my feet are improving a little. Last but not least, they are adorable.
