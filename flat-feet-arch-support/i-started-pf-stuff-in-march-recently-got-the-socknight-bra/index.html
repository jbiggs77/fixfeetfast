I started PF stuff in March. Recently got the sock/night brace to keep toes up and stretch. I now apply voltaren layered with tiger balm, put on a sock and then the brace thing. It has helped by 35-45% pain reduction. And I wear shoes mostly till I go to bed and adjust the laces so my foot doesn’t rub or shift in the shoe.
