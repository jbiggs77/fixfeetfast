Question for those of you who say you’d never do this surgery again because recovery was much harder than…

Archive date 2026-09-1229 archived replies
Minimally Invasive Bunion Surgery (MIS)Lapiplasty 3D Bunion SurgeryHammer Toe Surgery & CorrectionBest Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot Surgery

Question for those of you who say you’d never do this surgery again because recovery was much harder than expected… What was the alternative for you? I’m struggling with the “pay now or pay later” thought process. I have hypermobility, a partially torn plantar plate, and I’m almost 60. Part of me worries that waiting another 10–20 years could mean things progress further and recovery becomes even harder later in life. So how do you decide whether to roll the dice and manage it conservatively, or bite the bullet while you still have good home support, decent health, and insurance? I’ve already modified my lifestyle for nearly 10 years. I’m fortunate that I don’t have to wear restrictive shoes for work, I wear orthotics, and I’ve adapted around it pretty well. I’m not in severe pain (but I have limited my walking/hiking etc), which is what makes this decision harder. For those who chose to delay or skip surgery, what was the payoff? Did it actually stay manageable long term, or do you wish you had done it sooner?
