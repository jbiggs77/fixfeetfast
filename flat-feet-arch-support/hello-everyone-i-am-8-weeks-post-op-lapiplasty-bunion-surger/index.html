Community comment#8

There’s quite a few but I ended up purchasing the Brooks Adrenaline GTS 23. It was recommended to me on here by a member then another member bought it after I recommended it to them lol whatever you get, just make sure you have a wide toe box. I also wore my crocs around til my shoes came in. I still wear them. They’re too comfortable lol

Community comment#9

I went out searching for wide toe box but found that I couldn’t slide right into it, even with laces completely loosened. I went up a whole size and extra wide. My toe still won't bend. And it was difficult and painful. So had to ditch the wide toe search, for now. Still would like to get into those once foot heals completely. I found that the Hoka Bondi 9, not my favorite, worked best. Foot glides in nicely, protects the ball of my foot which is still pretty sensitive, and was able to go with just 1/2 size larger and regular/wide fit. Going up to big and too wide with with toe box was not ideal for my non surgical foot. I've been in the Bondi for 2 days now and so far, no complaints.

Community comment#10

I adore my Brooks Adrenaline GTS and all shoes from Orthofeet. Highly recommend both.
