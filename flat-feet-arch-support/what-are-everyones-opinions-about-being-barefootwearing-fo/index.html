What are everyone’s opinions about being barefoot/wearing footwear indoors?

Archive date 2026-10-0215 archived replies
Best Shoes After Foot SurgeryPain Management After Foot SurgeryPhysical Therapy & Foot ExercisesFlat Feet & Arch Support

What are everyone’s opinions about being barefoot/wearing footwear indoors? Iv had flat feet pain and planta in both feet now for a year. Doing the exercises, customised orthotics and now they’d like me to do shockwave therapy. I’m a bit hesitant about that as some people have said it’s made their pain worse and I’m trying to see what else I can do to help. Iv looked into getting a foot sleeve, calf massager, multivitamins ect and was wondering if I’m going wrong by being barefoot indoors? I think due to my flat feet I’m most comfy barefoot and always have been, but what’s your opinions? If you were sliders/Sandals indoors what’s your recommendations?

Community comment#12

Not flat footed - Since a kid I couldn’t wait to take my shoes off. Minute I get in the house, shoes come off. Retired and within seven months of 90% of the time being barefoot I developed PF in my right foot, now I cannot walk two feet without a shoe/sandal on. I keep cushioning flip flops beside the bed so I don’t have to walk bare foot, it really sucks! I have tried many things suggested and nothing really seems to work. Only help for me is wearing a compression sock or tape with comfortable shoes, and I still feel a bit of pain ☹️. Community comment#13

There is nothing wrong with transitioning to barefoot over time. It your pain must be a 4/10 or below and go slow. Avoiding things like this just keep you in pain for longer. Shockwave is designed to cause an inflammatory response that your foot heals. That’s how it works. You are suppose to be in more pain after to get better. ❤️‍🩹
