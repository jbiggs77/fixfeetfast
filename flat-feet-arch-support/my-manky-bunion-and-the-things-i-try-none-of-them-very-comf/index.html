Community comment#1

Wear wide, soft shoes with lots of toe space. Avoid tight or high-heeled shoes. Use gel bunion pads or toe sleeves to reduce rubbing and pressure. Try silicone toe separators (start slowly) to realign and prevent friction. Apply moisturizer or callus remover gently to soften thick skin. Night splints can help keep the big toe straighter if not too painful. If pain worsens or you notice infection or severe deformity — see a podiatrist for nail and bunion care. Comfort is key — stop any gadget that causes more pain. 👍👍👍
