I was told that they determine it by many factors including our lifestyle. I was given a lapidus because of the severity of my bunion and other issues with my foot. Was told since it involves a fusion that it has the lowest reoccurrence rate of the bunion coming back. My doc does not do mis. The doc I first saw does do mis though, but still recommended a lapidus for me. So I went ahead and had that. I’m very happy. I had my left foot done last year and my right foot done last month. I would bring any concerns you have up to your doc and discuss. And you can always get a second or third opinion if you feel differently about things.

Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#23

I also want to add, it’s important that you’re confident and are comfortable with your decision on which type of surgery to go with. So I think it’s best that you do bring your concerns up to your doc. Ask them why they would perform lapidus over the other and look at their reviews ofc. Seek out a secondary opinion. I had two before I decided on the lapidus. Do your research on which one to go to and if your second opinion says mis, get a third opinion, so you don’t have to chose between the two. It’s difficult for me to make that answer on which one would be best because unfortunately im just a mod, not a podiatrist or orthopedic surgeon, so all I can give you is advice and what I would do if I was in your shoes 🫶🏾 Lapidus may have a low reoccurrence due to a fusion at the root of the problem and is often used when there is instability in the foot and/or hyper-mobility that a mis cannot handle, however, recovery is longer. Mine is much longer because I had two other procedures alongside my bunion correction. Check here and look into the bunion community on Reddit. Do your research and make sure to be fully confident in your surgical choice 🩷 Just like with any other surgery, there are risks and make sure you’re aware of them and that things may not go as planned or that surgery may fail.
