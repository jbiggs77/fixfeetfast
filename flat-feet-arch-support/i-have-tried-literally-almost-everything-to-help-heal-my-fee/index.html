Community reply · In reply to an earlier comment#45

A lot of different ideas for sure. start with isometric holds. Meaning raise the arch of your foot while standing (or sitting -keeping your foot on the floor..still raising your arch). For 30-45 seconds. If you have no pain doing that. Do 1 more set. Go to a wall (barefoot) face it lean forward as far as you can wihout falling into the wall.. pressing your toes into the floor hold for 30-45 seconds. Start with 2 sets if it doesn't hurt. Toe yoga is good to do while sitting. Do that for a week. Up the set the next week and 1 more set the week after that. For a total of 4 sets. I use a sleep boot too. But see how you fair after the isometric hold exercises. If either hurt stop. Try it sitting down. If they still hurt. I would see a doctor. Collagen and vit c.- ask your primary if it's safe to take. May be beneficial for your fascia at 80 years old -(congratulations) That's huge milestone. Best wishes
